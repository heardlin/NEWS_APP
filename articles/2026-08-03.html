Rapists and child sex offenders to be barred from early release scheme

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Prisoners who committed rape, grooming, and serious child sex offences will be excluded from the forthcoming early release scheme for England and Wales, the government has said.

政府表示，英格兰和威尔士即将实施的提前释放计划将排除犯有强奸、诱骗和严重儿童性犯罪的囚犯。

Prime Minister Andy Burnham paused plans to release some prisoners in September as part of a bid to stop unprecedented prison overcrowding after backlash from survivors and victims. The scheme will now restart in October.

首相安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）在受到幸存者和受害者强烈反对后，暂停了9月释放部分囚犯的计划，以阻止前所未有的监狱过度拥挤。该计划将于10月重新启动。

Burnham said he "wanted to go even further", but that would not be possible without the prison system collapsing and putting the public "at much greater risk".

伯纳姆表示，他“希望更进一步”，但如果不让监狱系统崩溃并让公众“面临更大风险”，这是不可能的。

Lissie Harper, the widow of police officer Andrew Harper, said it was "disgusting" that his killers could be eligible for early release.

警察安德鲁·哈珀（Andrew Harper）的遗孀莉西·哈珀（Lissie Harper）表示，杀害她丈夫的凶手可能有资格提前释放，这“令人作呕”。

PC Harper, 28, was killed in 2019 when he was dragged by a car down a country road as three teenagers fled the scene of the quad bike theft in Berkshire.

28岁的警察哈珀于2019年在伯克郡（Berkshire）一辆四轮摩托车被盗现场，三名青少年逃离时被一辆汽车拖拽在乡村道路上致死。

His family previously said they had received a letter from the Ministry of Justice (MoJ) confirming the offenders who killed the officer could be included in the scheme.

他的家人此前表示，他们收到了司法部（Ministry of Justice，MoJ）的一封信，确认杀害这名警察的罪犯可能被纳入该计划。

Burnham had paused the scheme on 23 July to conduct an urgent review.

伯纳姆于7月23日暂停了该计划，以进行紧急审查。

In a statement announcing the changes on Monday, he said: "I fully understand the anger, anxiety and distress that's been caused. I've heard that loud and clear."

在周一宣布变更的声明中，他说：“我完全理解由此引起的愤怒、焦虑和痛苦。我已经清楚地听到了这些声音。”

But Lissie Harper said that she was "beyond appalled and bristling with anger to hear that Andrew's killers are soon to be free to roam the streets".

但莉西·哈珀表示，她“听到安德鲁的凶手即将自由漫步街头，感到无比震惊和愤怒”。

Under the government's changes, it also announced an extra Â£10 million will go to frontline victim support services, including a new national helpline.

根据政府的变更，还宣布额外投入1000万英镑用于一线受害者支持服务，包括一个新的全国热线。

The Sentencing Act was amended in January, with the eligibility for early release based on the length of fixed sentence, rather than the type of offence.

《量刑法》（Sentencing Act）于1月进行了修订，提前释放的资格基于固定刑期的长度，而非罪行类型。

The act already excluded some of the most serious and dangerous offenders, such as murderers serving life sentences or those on extended sentences.

该法案已经排除了某些最严重和危险的罪犯，如终身监禁的谋杀犯或延长刑期的罪犯。

Under Burnham's announced changes, rape and child sexual offences will now also be excluded.

根据伯纳姆宣布的变更，强奸和儿童性犯罪现在也将被排除在外。

The government said this move does not require changes to the existing legislation before October, though it may make amendments in the future to exclude certain offenders from being released.

政府表示，这一举措在10月前不需要修改现有立法，尽管未来可能会进行修订，以排除某些罪犯被释放。

Offenders of other crimes serving longer sentences of four years or more will be eligible for release halfway through their sentences rather than two-thirds as had previously been the case.

其他犯罪刑期四年或以上的罪犯，将在服刑一半时间后有资格获释，而非之前的三分之二。

Those serving less serious sentences could be freed after a third of their time, rather than half.

刑期较轻的罪犯可能在服刑三分之一时间后获释，而非一半。

The changes are due to be phased in, beginning with prisoners serving sentences of less than 575 days, going up to inmates jailed for 12 years or more being eligible for early release in June 2027.

这些变更将分阶段实施，从服刑少于575天的囚犯开始，逐步到2027年6月服刑12年或以上的囚犯有资格提前释放。

The "presumption" will be that offenders are tagged and will face longer periods of supervision and tough new rules including "restriction zones", Burnham said.

伯纳姆表示，“推定”是罪犯将被佩戴电子标签，并面临更长的监督期和严格的新规定，包括“限制区域”。

The number of individuals released each time will depend on the make-up of the prison population, which the government says will evolve between now and implementation.

每次释放的人数将取决于监狱人口的构成，政府表示，从现在到实施期间，这一构成将发生变化。

Reacting to the announcement, a prison officer told the BBC that they were "creaking at the seams" and that "delaying the releases until October could mean we simply run out of space".

一名监狱官员在回应这一公告时告诉BBC，他们“不堪重负”，“将释放推迟到10月可能意味着我们根本没有空间”。

A probation officer said it is "not clear there's anything actually new here in terms of how probation staff are going to manage people being released earlier".

一名缓刑官表示，“目前尚不清楚缓刑工作人员将如何管理提前释放的人员，这里是否真的有什么新东西”。

Separately, those who serve the full sentence and are convicted of rape or serious child sex offences will face stronger surveillance after leaving prison, the government said.

此外，政府表示，那些服满刑期并被判犯有强奸或严重儿童性犯罪的罪犯，出狱后将面临更严格的监控。

This includes 12 months of GPS monitoring and strengthened community supervision.

这包括12个月的GPS监控和加强的社区监督。

Calling the announcement a "U-turn", Shadow Justice Secretary Nick Timothy said it "doesn't go far enough".

影子司法大臣尼克·蒂莫西（Nick Timothy）称这一公告是“180度大转弯”，并表示“这还不够”。

"Some child sex offenders and killers ... will still be released early. And the trauma of victims being told their abusers may be released can't be undone," he said.

他说：“一些儿童性犯罪者和杀人犯……仍将被提前释放。受害者被告知施虐者可能获释的创伤无法抹去。”

Domestic Abuse Commissioner Dame Nicole Jacobs criticised the decision to not exclude domestic abuse perpetrators.

家庭暴力专员妮可·雅各布斯女爵士（Dame Nicole Jacobs）批评了不排除家庭暴力施暴者的决定。

"It is critical that no abuser is released in October unless I am confident that victims' safety is not being compromised," she said.

她说：“除非我确信受害者的安全不会受到损害，否则至关重要的是，10月不得释放任何施暴者。”
