Ukraine air raid app apparently hacked as users receive false radiation threat, Air Force says

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Ukrainians are used to reacting to the warnings of Russian attacks sent via the air alert app

乌克兰人已经习惯通过空袭警报应用程序对俄罗斯袭击的警告做出反应。

Ukraine's air raid alert app was apparently hacked on Sunday and for a short time said the capital Kyiv was subject to a radiation threat, the Ukrainian Air Force has said.

乌克兰空军表示，乌克兰的空袭警报应用程序显然在周日遭到黑客攻击，并短暂显示首都基辅受到辐射威胁。

The alert on Telegram read: "The Air Alarm application has probably been subjected to hostile interference. There is no radiation threat! Stay calm and follow our updates on our channel."

Telegram上的警报写道：“空袭警报应用程序可能遭到了敌对干扰。没有辐射威胁！保持冷静，并关注我们频道的最新消息。”

The Ukrainian Central Intelligence Agency said there had been a failure in the app, and urged people to use other sources of information to clarify the situation on the threat of air strikes.

乌克兰中央情报局表示，该应用程序出现了故障，并敦促人们使用其他信息来源来澄清空袭威胁的情况。

The alerts, in Ukrainian, started at approximately 22:47 (20:47 GMT), with the first reading: "Radiation threat. Radiation threat declared in Kyiv."

这些警报以乌克兰语发出，开始于大约22:47（格林威治标准时间20:47），第一条警报写道：“辐射威胁。基辅宣布辐射威胁。”

The next message, written in Russian, came at 22:48, which read: "Hi! There will be a nuclear alert across Ukraine. Don't worry, it's Oreshnik flying".

第二条信息用俄语写成，于22:48发出，内容为：“嗨！乌克兰全境将发布核警报。别担心，是‘奥列什尼克’导弹飞过”。

A third alert, at 22:49, again in Ukrainian, said "Chemical threat. There is a threat of a chemical attack on Kyiv. Don't go outside."

第三条警报于22:49发出，再次用乌克兰语表示：“化学威胁。基辅面临化学攻击威胁。不要外出。”

At 22:49, a fourth alert stated there was a "Street fighting threat. Street fighting is taking place in Kyiv. Don't go outside."

22:49时，第四条警报称存在“巷战威胁。基辅正在发生巷战。不要外出。”

At 23:53 a series of all clear messages were then delivered to users' phones, which were connected to each of the previous Kyiv alerts, individually declaring each threat clear.

23:53时，一系列解除警报的消息发送到用户手机上，这些消息分别与之前的每一条基辅警报相关联，逐一宣布每个威胁已解除。

In a separate post on Telegram, the Ukrainian Central Intelligence Agency said that due to the Air Alert application, the Radiation Hazard signal was played on loudspeaker devices in the Lviv region, and added there was no threat of radiation hazard.

在Telegram的另一条帖子中，乌克兰中央情报局表示，由于空袭警报应用程序，利沃夫地区的扩音器设备播放了辐射危险信号，并补充说没有辐射危险威胁。

If the alert app is not working, Ukraine's Centre for Countering Disinformation advises the public to turn on notifications on their smartphones, pay attention to sirens, and to only read official sources

如果警报应用程序无法正常工作，乌克兰反虚假信息中心建议公众打开智能手机上的通知功能，注意听警报器，并且只阅读官方来源的信息。

Several governors of other regions also posted on Telegram that the app had apparently been hacked and there was no local threat.

其他几个地区的州长也在Telegram上发帖称，该应用程序显然遭到黑客攻击，当地没有威胁。

"Everything is calm in the region for now," said Svitlana Onyshchuk, the governor of the Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast. The app had reported a red-level air raid alert for the oblast in Ukraine's east, between 22:50 and 23:57.

伊万诺-弗兰科夫斯克州州长斯维特兰娜·奥尼休克表示：“目前该地区一切平静。”该应用程序曾在22:50至23:57期间报告该州（位于乌克兰东部）发布了红色级别的空袭警报。

The governor of Vinnytsia oblast, Natalya Zabolotna said there had been a cyber-attack on the Air Alert app and posted a warning on Telegram not to click on the link in the message, which could be a phishing attempt.

文尼察州州长纳塔利娅·扎博洛特纳表示，空袭警报应用程序遭到了网络攻击，并在Telegram上发布警告，不要点击消息中的链接，这可能是网络钓鱼的尝试。

The Air Alert app is supported by the Ministry of Digital Transformation of Ukraine, and informs users instantly about the beginning and end of defence alerts. The ministry had earlier said the app was temporarily disabled for maintenance after it had "apparently been compromised". It urged residents to turn on their smartphones' emergency notifications so they could receive state-issued distress signals.

空袭警报应用程序由乌克兰数字转型部支持，即时向用户通报防御警报的开始和结束。该部早些时候曾表示，该应用程序在“显然遭到入侵”后暂时停用进行维护。它敦促居民打开智能手机的紧急通知功能，以便接收国家发布的遇险信号。

The alerts occurred just minutes before the end of a localised ceasefire in Ukraine's front-line Kherson region , which was implemented last Tuesday and officially ended at 23:00 Sunday.

这些警报发生在乌克兰前线赫尔松地区局部停火结束前几分钟，该停火于上周二实施，并于周日23:00正式结束。

Fighting had continued elsewhere while the Kherson ceasefire was in place, to allow civilians to evacuate the Russian-occupied city of Oleshky .

在赫尔松停火期间，其他地方的战斗仍在继续，以便平民从俄罗斯占领的奥列什基市撤离。
