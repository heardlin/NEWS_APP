Families of two police officers killed in crash say they were heroes

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

PC Matthew Blades (left) and PC Tom Clough were killed in a crash near Middlesbrough on Saturday

PC Matthew Blades（左）和PC Tom Clough在周六米德尔斯堡附近的一场车祸中丧生。

Two police officers who died when a car travelling the wrong way down a dual carriageway crashed into their vehicle have been hailed as heroes by their families.

两名警察在一条双车道公路上被一辆逆行汽车撞上后殉职，他们的家人称赞他们为英雄。

PC Tom Clough, 38, and PC Matthew Blades, 37, died when a Volkswagen Passat, which officers had been pursuing until seconds before , collided with their marked armed response car on the A66 near Middlesbrough on Saturday.

38岁的PC Tom Clough和37岁的PC Matthew Blades在周六米德尔斯堡附近的A66公路上，一辆大众帕萨特（Volkswagen Passat）撞上了他们标记的武装响应车辆，导致两人死亡。此前，警察一直在追捕这辆帕萨特，直到事故发生前几秒。

The Passat's occupants Michael Robert Cahill, 23, Jacob Matusiak, 23, Theo Rae, 17, Makai Saddington, 18, and Cole Robert Worthy, 17, also died.

帕萨特车上的乘客Michael Robert Cahill（23岁）、Jacob Matusiak（23岁）、Theo Rae（17岁）、Makai Saddington（18岁）和Cole Robert Worthy（17岁）也一同遇难。

In tributes released by Cleveland Police, PC Clough's family said he was "willing to help anyone", while PC Blades' wife described him as "loved beyond words".

在克利夫兰警方发布的悼念中，PC Clough的家人称他“愿意帮助任何人”，而PC Blades的妻子则形容他“被爱无法用言语表达”。

"No words will ever feel like the right words to honour our Matt," she said, adding he was a "beloved husband" and the "most devoted father" to their two young sons who would grow up knowing what an "incredible" dad he was.

“任何言语都无法恰当地纪念我们的Matt，”她说，并补充道，他是“挚爱的丈夫”，也是他们两个年幼儿子“最尽职的父亲”，孩子们长大后会知道他是多么“了不起”的爸爸。

"He gave his life in the line of duty, protecting and serving others with courage, dedication and selflessness," she said.

“他在执行任务时献出了生命，以勇气、奉献和无私精神保护和服务他人，”她说。

"The pain of losing him is beyond words, and our lives will never be the same.

“失去他的痛苦无法言喻，我们的生活将永远改变。”

"Neither will the love he gave us ever fade.

“但他给予我们的爱永远不会消逝。”

"He may have left this world committed to the duty he believed in, but his greatest legacy will always be the love he gave to his family."

“他可能带着他所信仰的职责离开了这个世界，但他最伟大的遗产将永远是他给予家人的爱。”

She ended the tribute to her husband: "Forever loved. Forever missed. Forever our superhero. Our Matt and Dad."

她以这样的话结束了对丈夫的悼念：“永远被爱。永远被怀念。永远是我们的超级英雄。我们的Matt和爸爸。”

PC Clough's family said he had a "fantastic sense of humour" and "jumped into action whenever needed", adding he was "our hero" and always "willing to help anyone for any reason".

PC Clough的家人表示，他有“极好的幽默感”，并且“在需要时总是立即行动”，并补充说他是“我们的英雄”，总是“愿意为任何人提供任何帮助”。

They also said he "proudly fought for his country to make the world a better place" and was "always on public duty throughout his short life".

他们还表示，他“自豪地为国家而战，让世界变得更美好”，并且“在他短暂的一生中始终履行公共职责”。

In other tributes, a man who knew PC Clough posted on social media he was a "true friend, fantastic soldier and I have no doubt even better police officer".

在其他悼念中，一位认识PC Clough的男子在社交媒体上发帖称，他是“真正的朋友，出色的士兵，我毫不怀疑他更是优秀的警察”。

Seaton Carew FC, where PC Blades had recently become a coach and was preparing for his first season with its under-sevens team, said the officer and his family were "much loved members" of the club.

Seaton Carew FC表示，PC Blades最近成为该俱乐部的教练，并正准备带领其七岁以下球队参加首个赛季，这位警官和他的家人是俱乐部“深受喜爱的成员”。

FC Hartlepool, where PC Blades had coached the under-10s team, said he was a "role model, mentor [and] friend" who the children "looked up to and admired".

FC Hartlepool表示，PC Blades曾执教其十岁以下球队，他是孩子们的“榜样、导师和朋友”，孩子们“敬仰和钦佩他”。

"His kindness, patience, and infectious personality made him loved by all who had the privilege of knowing him," the club added.

俱乐部补充说：“他的善良、耐心和富有感染力的个性使他受到所有有幸认识他的人的喜爱。”

Carmel College in Darlington, where PC Blades taught English before joining the police, said he was an "intelligent, kind an approachable teacher" who "gave generously of himself to the young people in his care".

达灵顿的Carmel College表示，PC Blades在加入警队前曾在该校教授英语，他是一位“聪明、善良且平易近人的老师”，他“慷慨地奉献自己，关怀他所照顾的年轻人”。

On Sunday evening more than Â£180,000 had been pledged for the officers' families in an online fundraiser set up by Cleveland Police Federation, who said the two officers had "paid the ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty".

周日晚，由克利夫兰警察联合会设立的在线筹款活动已为殉职警察的家属筹集了超过18万英镑，该联合会表示，这两名警察“在执行任务时做出了最大的牺牲”。

"They leave behind heartbroken loved ones who will struggle to come to terms with the loss of their loved one," the federation said, adding the organisation would support the families "at this unfathomable moment" with the money to be used "as they so wish".

联合会表示：“他们留下了心碎的亲人，这些亲人将难以接受失去至亲的事实。”并补充说，该组织将在“这个难以理解的时刻”支持这些家庭，这笔钱将“按照他们的意愿”使用。

Earlier a group of police officers laid floral tributes at the police cordon on Eston Road.

早些时候，一群警察在Eston Road的警戒线处献上鲜花。

Some removed their hats and bowed their heads and took a brief moment of silence to remember their colleagues.

一些人脱下帽子，低头默哀片刻，以纪念他们的同事。

They spoke briefly to other officers at the scene before leaving, with some hugging each other as they walked away.

他们与现场的其他人简短交谈后离开，离开时有些人互相拥抱。

Local residents Elisia and Vincent Haarhoff also went to the scene to express their solidarity with the police.

当地居民Elisia和Vincent Haarhoff也前往现场表达对警方的支持。

"No family deserves that," Elisia said, adding: "God bless all the families, honestly.

“没有家庭应该经历这些，”Elisia说，并补充道：“上帝保佑所有家庭，真的。”

"We wanted to come... [the police here have] always been so lovely."

“我们想来……（这里的警察）一直都非常友善。”

The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC), which is investigating the crash, said the officers who died were "in the area to assist in a search" for the Passat.

正在调查这起事故的独立警察行为办公室（IOPC）表示，殉职的警察当时“在该地区协助搜索”那辆帕萨特。

Cleveland Police had received reports linking the car, which was believed to be using cloned registration plates that activated an ANPR camera, to "numerous incidents", the watchdog said.

该监督机构表示，克利夫兰警方收到报告，称这辆汽车涉嫌使用克隆车牌，触发了ANPR摄像头，并与“多起事件”有关。

Officers were pursuing it for almost seven minutes before the Passat turned the wrong-way on to the A66, the IOPC spokesman said.

IOPC发言人称，警方追捕了这辆车近七分钟，随后帕萨特逆行转入A66。

About 10 seconds later, at 03:39 BST, the car hit the officers' vehicle which was on the correct side of the dual carriageway, the IOPC said.

IOPC表示，大约10秒后，即英国夏令时03:39，该车撞上了位于双车道正确一侧的警车。

Security footage from a house on Lime Road in Middlesbrough appears to show a car being pursued by police vehicles three minutes before the crash.

米德尔斯堡Lime Road一处住宅的监控录像似乎显示，在事故发生前三分钟，一辆汽车正被警车追捕。

雷德卡和克利夫兰自治市议会表示，A66南岸Eston Road和Normanby Road之间的路段将关闭至周一，以便对车道进行维修。
