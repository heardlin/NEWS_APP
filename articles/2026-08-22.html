Two police officers among seven killed in wrong-way car crash

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

A huge emergency response could be seen along the road

道路沿线可以看到大规模的应急响应。

Two police officers were among seven people killed when a car travelling the wrong way down a dual carriageway crashed into a force vehicle.

一辆汽车在双车道高速公路上逆行，撞上了一辆警车，造成七人死亡，其中包括两名警察。

A Volkswagen Passat with five occupants said to be in their late teens struck an oncoming marked Cleveland Police vehicle on the A66 near Middlesbrough at about 03:39 BST.

一辆载有五名据称年龄在十几岁末期的乘客的大众帕萨特，在米德尔斯堡附近的A66公路上，于英国夏令时约03:39撞上了一辆迎面而来的克利夫兰警察局标记车辆。

The BBC understands a pursuit of the car had ended before the crash, with the police vehicle just happening to be in the South Bank area. The occupants of both cars were pronounced dead at the scene.

据BBC了解，在撞车事故前，对汽车的追捕已经结束，警车恰好位于南岸地区。两辆车的乘客均被当场宣布死亡。

The officers have been named as PC Matthew Blades, 37, and PC Tom Clough, 38. Chief Constable Victoria Fuller said they were "brave officers protecting our communities".

两名警官已被确认为37岁的马修·布莱兹警员和38岁的汤姆·克劳警员。警察局长维多利亚·富勒称他们是“保护我们社区的勇敢警官”。

She said it was an "incredibly sad day for Cleveland", adding: "No one should leave their home and not return safely.

她说这是“克利夫兰极其悲伤的一天”，并补充说：“没有人应该离开家却无法安全返回。”

"These were brave officers protecting our communities and serving our residents, who didn't return home to their families at the end of their shift."

“这些勇敢的警官保护我们的社区，服务我们的居民，却在轮班结束时未能回家与家人团聚。”

Fuller said she and her deputy chief constable had visited the officer's families to "offer our condolences, support one another, and begin to come to terms with this unimaginable loss".

富勒表示，她和她的副警察局长已探访了警官的家属，以“表达我们的哀悼，互相支持，并开始接受这一难以想象的损失”。

The families of those identified had been informed and were being offered specialist support.

已确认身份的遇难者家属已得到通知，并正在接受专业支持。

"This tragic incident will be felt across Cleveland, within our communities and throughout the entire policing family," Fuller added.

富勒补充说：“这起悲剧事件将在整个克利夫兰、我们的社区以及整个警察大家庭中引起共鸣。”

Prime Minister Andy Burnham said he was "devastated" by the news and thanked emergency services for responding in "incredibly difficult circumstances".

首相安迪·伯纳姆表示，他对这一消息感到“悲痛欲绝”，并感谢紧急服务部门在“极其困难的情况下”做出的响应。

Security footage from a house on Lime Road in Middlesbrough appears to show a car being pursued by police vehicles three minutes before the crash.

米德尔斯堡莱姆路一处住宅的监控录像似乎显示，在车祸发生前三分钟，一辆汽车被警车追捕。

Fuller said the officers were in a marked armed response vehicle travelling on the correct side of the road.

富勒表示，这两名警官当时乘坐的是一辆标记的武装响应车辆，行驶在正确的车道一侧。

The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) said it had opened an investigation.

独立警察行为办公室（IOPC）表示已展开调查。

The watchdog said: "At this early stage, we understand Cleveland Police sighted a suspicious vehicle and a pursuit began."

该监督机构表示：“在早期阶段，我们了解到克利夫兰警方发现了一辆可疑车辆，并开始了追捕。”

The A66 is the major artery through Teesside and the location of the crash is surrounded by industrial units and factories.

A66公路是提赛德的主要干道，车祸发生地点周围是工业区和工厂。

The road was closed in both directions between Normanby Road and Church Lane with the force saying it was set to stay shut for the rest of the day.

诺曼比路和教堂巷之间的双向道路已封闭，警方表示预计当天剩余时间将继续封闭。

The crash occurred on the A66 at South Bank shortly before 04:00 BST on Saturday

车祸发生在周六凌晨约03:40，地点在南岸的A66公路上。

The North East Ambulance Service, which sent 14 "ambulance resources", said: "We send condolences to all of those affected by such a tragic event and thank everyone for all they did to help."

东北救护车服务中心派出了14辆救护车资源，并表示：“我们向所有受此悲剧事件影响的人表示哀悼，并感谢所有提供帮助的人。”

Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood said the force had her "support as they work to identify all those involved".

内政大臣沙巴纳·马哈茂德表示，警方在“努力确认所有涉事人员身份”时得到了她的支持。

She said: "Police officers put their own safety on the line to protect their communities every single day.

她说：“警察每天都在冒着生命危险保护社区。”

"My thoughts are with all those affected, their families, friends, the emergency services who attended the scene and the wider police family."

“我的心与所有受影响的人同在，包括他们的家人、朋友、到场处置的紧急服务部门以及更广泛的警察大家庭。”

The A66 is the major artery through Teesside

A66公路是提赛德的主要干道。

Cleveland police and crime commissioner Matt Storey called it an "unimaginable loss" and said "few words [could] bring comfort" to those affected.

克利夫兰警察和犯罪专员马特·斯托里称这是“难以想象的损失”，并表示“言语难以给受影响者带来安慰”。

Anna Turley, MP for the local Redcar constituency, said she was "deeply saddened" and also thanked emergency services.

当地雷德卡选区的议员安娜·特利表示，她“深感悲痛”，并感谢紧急服务部门。

Cleveland Police Federation chairwoman Lauren Somerville said the loss of the officers was "unfathomable", adding: "Words cannot describe the pain the Cleveland Police family are feeling today."

克利夫兰警察联合会主席劳伦·萨默维尔表示，失去这些警官是“难以理解的”，并补充说：“言语无法描述克利夫兰警察大家庭今天感受到的痛苦。”

Shadow Home Secretary Chris Philp shared his condolences, adding the IOPC "must be given the time and space to complete their investigations without speculation".

影子内政大臣克里斯·菲尔普表达了哀悼，并补充说，IOPC“必须给予时间和空间来完成调查，而不受猜测干扰”。

The Mayor of Tees Valley Ben Houchen said it was a "horrible and tragic incident" and police officers were "heroes".

蒂斯谷市长本·豪琴表示，这是一起“可怕而悲惨的事件”，警察是“英雄”。
