Burnham announces scheme to help first-time buyers on to housing ladder

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

The prime minister announced the scheme on the eve of the Labour Party conference in Liverpool

首相在利物浦工党大会前夕宣布了该计划。

Prime Minister Andy Burnham is to introduce a new scheme to try to help young people get on the property ladder.

首相安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）将推出一项新计划，试图帮助年轻人登上房产阶梯。

The "Your First Home" scheme would be open to first-time buyers in England with a deposit of 2.5%. It would provide them with a loan worth 20% of their new build property's value to help pay for the purchase.

“你的第一套房”（Your First Home）计划将面向英格兰的首套房买家，首付只需2.5%。该计划将为他们提供相当于新建房产价值20%的贷款，以帮助支付购房费用。

Burnham said people who do not have support from the "bank of mum and dad" are among those the scheme would aim to help.

伯纳姆表示，该计划旨在帮助那些没有得到“父母银行”支持的人。

There would be an initial interest-free period for the equity loan, with more details expected in next month's Budget.

股权贷款最初将有一段时间免息，更多细节预计将在下个月的预算中公布。

The Conservatives warned the policy would leave first-time buyers taking on more debt and risks "pushing up the price of new homes".

保守党警告称，该政策将使首次购房者背负更多债务，并可能“推高新房价格”。

Burnham said: "Too many young people are struggling with the cost of housing, with many giving up hope of ever having a home to call their own.

伯纳姆说：“太多年轻人在住房成本中挣扎，许多人放弃了拥有自己住房的希望。”

"So we will step in to help more first-time buyers on to the housing ladder."

“因此，我们将介入，帮助更多首次购房者登上房产阶梯。”

The PM added he believes the scheme will give builders the "confidence to deliver the high-quality new homes the country needs".

首相补充说，他相信该计划将给建筑商“信心，以交付国家所需的高质量新房”。

Funding for the scheme is expected to come from reprioritising existing budgets, but housing developers will pay towards the running costs.

该计划的资金预计将来自重新调整现有预算的优先次序，但住房开发商将支付运营成本。

Ministers have not yet confirmed whether there is an age restriction on the scheme, or a cap on the price on the value of the property.

部长们尚未确认该计划是否有年龄限制，或房产价值是否有价格上限。

The policy is similar to initiatives from previous governments, including the coalition's Help To Buy scheme, introduced in 2013 by then-Chancellor George Osborne.

该政策与历届政府的举措类似，包括2013年由时任财政大臣乔治·奥斯本（George Osborne）推出的联合政府“购房援助”（Help to Buy）计划。

It expanded a previous scheme, known as FirstBuy, which was aimed solely at first-time buyers.

该计划扩展了之前一项名为“首次购房”（FirstBuy）的计划，该计划仅针对首次购房者。

Help to Buy initially enabled all purchasers to put down a 5% deposit on a newly built home, with a value of up to a Â£600,000.

“购房援助”最初允许所有购房者以5%的首付购买新建房屋，价值最高可达60万英镑。

Up to 20% of the cost of the home was funded by a shared equity loan, which was interest-free for the first five years.

房屋成本的最高20%由共享股权贷款提供资金，该贷款在前五年免息。

The first version of the programme was active until 2021. A second iteration of the scheme, which ran until 2023, restricted access to first-time buyers and applied regional-level price caps to the properties that could be bought.

该计划的第一版一直实施到2021年。该计划的第二版一直持续到2023年，限制首次购房者参与，并对可购买的房产实行区域价格上限。

An evaluation of the scheme , external found more than 387,000 properties were purchased through it, of which 328,000 were by first-time buyers.

对该计划的外部评估发现，通过该计划购买了超过38.7万套房产，其中32.8万套由首次购房者购买。

In its conclusion, the report noted Help to Buy was "effective in supporting people into home ownership in some areas, but did not remove affordability barriers for first-time buyers in areas that were already relatively expensive".

报告在结论中指出，“购房援助”在“一些地区有效支持人们拥有住房，但并未消除那些已经相对昂贵地区首次购房者的负担能力障碍”。

Housing Secretary Angela Rayner said "Your First Home" would "build on the lessons learned from previous schemes, while retaining the benefits".

住房大臣安吉拉·雷纳（Angela Rayner）表示，“你的第一套房”将“借鉴以往计划的经验教训，同时保留其优点”。

The Home Builders Federation welcomed the proposals, saying it had "long called for action to improve access to home ownership".

房屋建筑商联合会（Home Builders Federation）对这些提议表示欢迎，称其“长期以来一直呼吁采取行动改善购房机会”。

"Our analysis shows a well-designed scheme can make a meaningful difference for households struggling to get on to the property ladder," it added.

该联合会补充说：“我们的分析表明，一个设计良好的计划可以为那些难以登上房产阶梯的家庭带来有意义的改变。”

Conservative shadow housing secretary Katie Lam accused Labour of wanting first-time buyers to "take on even more debt to afford the few homes that have been built".

保守党影子住房大臣凯蒂·拉姆（Katie Lam）指责工党希望首次购房者“承担更多债务，以负担已经建成的少数房屋”。

She added taxpayers would take a "stake in their homes" and be "picking up the bill for months".

她补充说，纳税人将“在他们的房屋中持有股份”，并“为数月买单”。

Lam said: "Slapping another levy on developers risks pushing up the price of new homes too.

拉姆说：“对开发商再征收一项税费也可能推高新房价格。”

"This is a colossal admission of failure. This Labour government is making homes harder to build, then asking buyers to borrow more to pay for them."

“这是对失败的巨大承认。这个工党政府让房屋更难建造，然后要求买家借更多钱来支付。”

Lam said the Conservatives want to scrap stamp duty, "back British house builders" and ensure that houses are "built in the places that people actually want to live".

拉姆表示，保守党希望取消印花税，“支持英国房屋建筑商”，并确保房屋“建在人们真正想居住的地方”。

Rayner told the BBC earlier this month that there was only a "slim chance" the government would meet its target of building 1.5m homes in England by the next election, but said she was "not giving it up".

雷纳本月早些时候告诉BBC，政府在下届选举前实现英格兰建造150万套住房的目标只有“微弱的机会”，但表示她“不会放弃”。

Data up to June this year suggested the government was behind on the rate of construction needed to meet the goal , which housebuilders have previously warned was too optimistic.

截至今年6月的数据表明，政府所需的建设速度落后于实现该目标的速度，房屋建筑商此前曾警告该目标过于乐观。

BBC Verify analysis showed a decline in the number of new houses being built during Labour's first year in power - continuing a fall that began under the Conservatives.

BBC Verify的分析显示，在工党执政的第一年，新建房屋数量下降——延续了保守党执政时期开始的下降趋势。

The housing policy announcement came as Burnham arrived in Liverpool ahead of his first Labour Party conference since becoming prime minister.

这一住房政策宣布之际，伯纳姆抵达利物浦，准备参加他成为首相以来的首次工党大会。

The PM said he wanted to make it a "really important week where we set out a new direction for the country".

首相表示，他希望将其打造成“真正重要的一周，我们为国家制定新方向”。

He added: "We've had a decade since Brexit and it's been a decade of too much drift, too much division, and we need to draw a line on that."

他补充说：“脱欧以来我们已经过了十年，这十年有太多的漂移，太多的分裂，我们需要对此划清界限。”

Another pre-conference announcement included a pledge to offer quicker access of a guaranteed job to young people receiving health or disability support.

会前的另一项宣布包括承诺为领取健康或残疾支持的年轻人提供更快获得保障性工作的机会。

The offer would be made three months after they start claiming benefits. This approach would be introduced from next April.

该提议将在他们开始领取福利三个月后提出。这种方法将从明年4月开始实施。

Labour said the government would cover 100% of wage costs for up to 25 hours a week for six months.

工党表示，政府将承担每周最多25小时、为期六个月的100%工资成本。

Currently, jobless young people aged 18 to 24 must have been on universal credit for 18 months before receiving an offer under the government's job guarantee scheme.

目前，18至24岁的失业年轻人必须领取通用信贷18个月，才能根据政府的就业保障计划获得工作机会。

Conservative shadow work and pensions secretary Helen Whately said Labour should have cut "burdens" on businesses to help ensure disabled people are employed.

保守党影子工作和养老金大臣海伦·惠特利（Helen Whately）表示，工党应该削减企业的“负担”，以帮助确保残疾人就业。

She said: "Instead, they are bringing back a Conservative post-pandemic scheme. Andy Burnham clearly has no new ideas."

她说：“相反，他们带回了一项保守党疫情后的计划。安迪·伯纳姆显然没有新想法。”

Whately also questioned how the change would be funded.

惠特利还质疑这一变化将如何获得资金。
