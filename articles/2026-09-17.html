Body found in search for missing three-year-old Noah Woods

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Noah Woods was described as looking "happy and healthy" by one of the last people to see him

诺亚·伍兹（Noah Woods）被最后见到他的人之一描述为看起来“快乐而健康”。

A body has been found in the search for missing three-year-old boy Noah Woods, police have confirmed.

警方证实，在寻找失踪的三岁男孩诺亚·伍兹的过程中发现了一具尸体。

Noah disappeared at about 15:00 BST on Tuesday from a playground in Brantham, Suffolk, after becoming separated from a relative.

诺亚于周二英国夏令时15:00左右在萨福克郡布兰瑟姆的一个游乐场与亲戚走散后失踪。

Supt Tom Pearse of Suffolk Police said dive teams recovered the body of a child from a nearby pond.

萨福克郡警察局的汤姆·皮尔斯警司（Supt Tom Pearse）表示，潜水队从附近的池塘中找到了一具儿童尸体。

He said the death was being treated as unexplained but not suspicious.

他说，死亡被视为原因不明，但无可疑之处。

CCTV footage shows the last sighting of Noah at the playground

闭路电视录像显示了诺亚在游乐场的最后身影

Supt Pearse said formal identification had yet to take place, but Noah's family had been informed.

皮尔斯警司表示，正式身份确认尚未进行，但诺亚的家人已被告知。

"Our thoughts are with Noah's family and all those who knew him," he said.

他说：“我们的心与诺亚的家人以及所有认识他的人同在。”

"We have been overwhelmed by the outpouring of public support and people putting on their boots to help."

“公众的支持和人们踊跃参与帮助，让我们深受感动。”

Police said at least 1,300 members of the public had helped in the search for the missing boy, who was non-verbal and partially deaf.

警方表示，至少有1300名公众参与了寻找这名失踪男孩的行动，该男孩不会说话且部分失聪。

People from as far afield as Birmingham and Hampshire had turned up to help.

来自远至伯明翰和汉普郡的人们也赶来帮忙。

Helicopters, dog units and members of the public had been scouring the area, close to the estuary of the River Stour on the Essex border.

直升机、警犬队和公众一直在该地区进行搜索，该地区靠近埃塞克斯郡边界的斯陶尔河河口。

Volunteer efforts had been co-ordinated from Brantham Leisure Centre and the village hall, which ran out of hi-vis vests due to the number of volunteers.

志愿者工作由布兰瑟姆休闲中心和村政厅协调，由于志愿者人数众多，高可见度背心已用完。

Those looking for Noah had been told he would not respond to shouting.

寻找诺亚的人们被告知，他不会对呼喊做出回应。

Supt Tom Pearse of Suffolk Police said: "Our thoughts are with Noah's family and all those who knew him"

萨福克郡警察局的汤姆·皮尔斯警司说：“我们的心与诺亚的家人以及所有认识他的人同在。”

Michael Crowley, who was working opposite the playground on Tuesday and was one of the last people to see Noah before he went missing, said: "He looked happy and healthy as anything."

迈克尔·克劳利（Michael Crowley）周二在游乐场对面工作，是诺亚失踪前最后见到他的人之一，他说：“他看起来非常快乐和健康。”

Jessica Gammons, a mother-of-two from Kelvedon, in Essex, was among the many volunteers who went out to look for the toddler.

来自埃塞克斯郡凯尔维登的两个孩子的母亲杰西卡·加蒙斯（Jessica Gammons）是众多出去寻找这名幼儿的志愿者之一。

She said she felt compelled join the search, despite being heavily pregnant, because her son, like Noah, is three years old.

她说，尽管身怀六甲，她仍觉得必须加入搜索，因为她的儿子和诺亚一样，也是三岁。

"I kept thinking to myself 'If this was my son, I'd want everyone possible to help,'" she said.

她说：“我一直在想，‘如果这是我的儿子，我会希望所有人都来帮忙。’”

"It's such a sad situation. I can't stop thinking about him."

“这太令人悲伤了。我无法停止想他。”

Jessica Gammons, a mother of two, was among the volunteers who helped in the search

杰西卡·加蒙斯，两个孩子的母亲，是参与搜索的志愿者之一

Searches had widened to include East Bergholt and Manningtree, over the Essex border, on Wednesday.

周三，搜索范围已扩大到包括埃塞克斯郡边界外的东贝霍尔特和曼宁特里。

Divers from the Metropolitan Police were called in to search Decoy Pond, a nearby fishing lake.

伦敦警察厅的潜水员被召来搜索附近的钓鱼湖迪科伊池塘。

The pond is about 350 yds (320m) away from the playground where Noah was last seen.

该池塘距离诺亚最后被看到的游乐场约350码（320米）。

Police said at least 1,300 members of the public had joined emergency services in the search for the missing boy

警方表示，至少有1300名公众加入了紧急服务部门寻找失踪男孩的行动

Supt Pearse continued: "Since Noah was reported as missing, a vast number of our officers and staff have been deployed in numerous functions in the search for Noah and support for his family.

皮尔斯警司继续说：“自从诺亚被报告失踪以来，我们的大量警员和工作人员已被部署到众多岗位，寻找诺亚并支持他的家人。”

"We've been supported by Suffolk HART (Hazardous Area Response Team), SULSAR (Suffolk Lowland Search and Rescue), Environment Agency, HM Coastguard, Fire and Rescue Service and the ambulance service.

“我们得到了萨福克郡HART（危险区域响应小组）、SULSAR（萨福克低地搜索与救援）、环境署、英国海岸警卫队、消防与救援服务以及救护服务的支持。”

"As a parent myself, I completely understand why people would want to help and such a response is one that typifies the strong sense of community and the heart-warming nature of Suffolk people that make the county such a special place.

“作为一名家长，我完全理解人们为何想要帮忙，这样的响应体现了强烈的社区意识和萨福克人民温暖人心的天性，使这个郡成为一个如此特别的地方。”

"I am sure you appreciate the family now need time, space and privacy and I thank you for your understanding in this."

“我相信您能理解，这家人现在需要时间、空间和隐私，我感谢您在这方面的理解。”
