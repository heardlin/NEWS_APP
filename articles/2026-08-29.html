A 97-year-old woman pulled from flood debris in Nepal is recovering in hospital, according to her doctors.

据她的医生称，一位97岁的老妇人在尼泊尔从洪水废墟中被救出，目前正在医院康复。

Guna Maya Bohara's great-nephew, Dipak Bohara, has told BBC Nepali she looked like "a warrior" who had returned victorious from battle.

古娜·玛雅·博哈拉的侄孙迪帕克·博哈拉告诉BBC尼泊尔频道，她看起来像一位从战场凯旋归来的“战士”。

The operation to pull Bohara out of the rubble of her care home took four hours.

将博哈拉从她养老院的废墟中救出的行动耗时四个小时。

Bohara travelled from Kathmandu to help rescue his great-aunt.

博哈拉从加德满都赶来救援他的姑奶奶。

Hundreds of people have been confirmed dead after Wednesday's flash floods, which scientists believe were triggered by a glacier collapsing in the Himalayan border region with Tibet.

周三的山洪已确认造成数百人死亡，科学家认为这次山洪是由喜马拉雅地区与西藏交界处的一座冰川崩塌引发的。
