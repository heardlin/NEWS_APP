Burnham defends Jackdaw decision as Badenoch urges PM to overturn ban on new oil and gas licences

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Scottish Secretary Douglas Alexander, Energy Secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh and Chancellor John Healey visited the St Fergus Gas Terminal on Wednesday

苏格兰事务大臣道格拉斯·亚历山大（Douglas Alexander）、能源大臣米亚塔·法恩布勒（Miatta Fahnbulleh）和财政大臣约翰·希利（John Healey）于周三参观了圣弗格斯天然气终端（St Fergus Gas Terminal）。

The UK government has approved production from the Jackdaw gas field in the North Sea amid intense debate about the project's environmental and economic impact.

英国政府已批准北海杰克道（Jackdaw）气田的生产，该项目在环境和经济效益方面引发了激烈辩论。

Ministers said the first gas could be produced by the end of this month, helping heat millions of homes, and also announced the creation of a new energy wealth fund.

部长们表示，首批天然气可能在本月底前产出，有助于为数百万家庭供暖，并宣布成立一个新的能源财富基金。

But environmental campaigners said the decision would hamper efforts to tackle climate change and make "no meaningful difference" to UK energy security.

但环保人士表示，这一决定将阻碍应对气候变化的努力，并对英国能源安全“没有实质性影响”。

Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch accused the prime minister of "dithering" on Jackdaw and said a ban on new oil and gas licences should be repealed - but Andy Burnham said her party had "messed up" the original decision.

保守党领袖凯米·巴德诺赫（Kemi Badenoch）指责首相在杰克道问题上“犹豫不决”，并表示应废除对新油气许可证的禁令——但安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）表示，她的政党“搞砸了”最初的决定。

Jackdaw is situated 150 miles (241km) east of Aberdeen and is operated by Adura, a joint venture between Shell and Norwegian state energy firm Equinor.

杰克道位于阿伯丁以东150英里（241公里）处，由Adura运营，这是壳牌（Shell）和挪威国家能源公司Equinor的合资企业。

A decision is yet to be taken on whether production can begin at Rosebank, the UK's largest known untapped oil field, which is located off the coast of Shetland in the North Atlantic.

关于是否可以在罗斯班克（Rosebank）开始生产尚未做出决定，这是英国已知最大的未开发油田，位于北大西洋设得兰群岛海岸附近。

Adura says Jackdaw will provide about 6% of UK gas production, enough to power 1.4 million homes. After imports are included, that amounts to about 2% of total UK demand.

Adura表示，杰克道将提供约6%的英国天然气产量，足以供应140万户家庭。计入进口后，这约占英国总需求的2%。

The gas will be pumped to the existing Shearwater platform for processing â known as a tie-back in industry terms â before being piped to the St Fergus gas terminal in Aberdeenshire.

天然气将被输送到现有的 Shearwater 平台进行处理——业内称为回接——然后通过管道输送到阿伯丁郡的圣弗格斯天然气终端。

The UK government said that over its expected 11-year lifespan, Jackdaw would support the UK's gas supply, protect hundreds of jobs and add Â£2.9bn to the economy.

英国政府表示，在其预计11年的寿命期内，杰克道将支持英国的天然气供应，保护数百个工作岗位，并为经济增加29亿英镑。

Energy Secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh said the gas from Jackdaw would be in homes and businesses this month - although she told BBC Radio Scotland Breakfast that it would not lower energy bills this winter.

能源大臣米亚塔·法恩布勒表示，杰克道的天然气将于本月进入家庭和企业——尽管她告诉BBC苏格兰早餐节目，这不会降低今年冬天的能源账单。

She said the decision would "help minimise the UK's use of imported higher emission fuels as we continue our transition to renewables and nuclear".

她说，这一决定将“有助于最大限度地减少英国对进口高排放燃料的使用，因为我们继续向可再生能源和核能过渡”。

"The decision is compatible with our legally binding climate commitments, and we remain resolute in ending new licences for oil and gas and achieving net zero by 2050," she said.

“该决定符合我们具有法律约束力的气候承诺，我们仍然坚决终止新的油气许可证，并在2050年前实现净零排放，”她说。

The UK government also announced the establishment of an energy wealth fund using tax revenue from oil and gas extraction, with an initial Â£2bn of funding, to invest in renewable and low carbon energy projects.

英国政府还宣布利用油气开采的税收收入建立一个能源财富基金，初始资金为20亿英镑，用于投资可再生能源和低碳能源项目。

At Prime Minister's Questions, Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch thanked Burnham for "finally" approving Jackdaw but accused the government of "dithering" over the decision.

在首相问答环节，保守党领袖凯米·巴德诺赫感谢伯纳姆“终于”批准了杰克道，但指责政府在这一决定上“犹豫不决”。

She also urged Labour to repeal its ban on all new oil and gas licences.

她还敦促工党废除对所有新油气许可证的禁令。

"We have the resources, the expertise and the people. What we have lost is the confidence to make tough decisions," she said.

“我们有资源、有专业知识、有人才。我们失去的是做出艰难决定的信心，”她说。

The prime minister said he had made a decision that the previous Tory government had "messed up".

首相表示，他做出了一个前保守党政府“搞砸了”的决定。

He said they had got decisions wrong because they did not give any regard to climate obligations, and that "these things have to be considered in the round".

他说，他们做出了错误的决定，因为他们没有考虑气候义务，而“这些事情必须全面考虑”。

Burnham also said he had asked the chancellor and energy secretary to look into whether standing charges could be removed from gas bills.

伯纳姆还表示，他已要求财政大臣和能源大臣研究是否可以从燃气账单中取消固定费用。

The Jackdaw platform is located east of Aberdeen

杰克道平台位于阿伯丁以东。

Scotland's First Minister John Swinney said he hoped the announcement would provide some certainty for the north east of Scotland.

苏格兰首席大臣约翰·斯温尼（John Swinney）表示，他希望这一宣布能为苏格兰东北部提供一些确定性。

"It is vital that the economic benefits of our energy are felt here in Scotland and used to bring energy bills down, rather than flowing to the Treasury to plug more short-term spending gaps," said the SNP leader.

“至关重要的是，我们能源的经济效益应在苏格兰这里感受到，并用于降低能源账单，而不是流向财政部来填补更多的短期支出缺口，”这位苏格兰民族党领袖说。

He also welcomed the creation of an energy wealth fund, which he said should be "prioritised for offshore renewable opportunities and be ringfenced to be focused on the north east of Scotland".

他还欢迎能源财富基金的设立，并表示该基金应“优先考虑海上可再生能源机会，并专门用于苏格兰东北部”。

Scottish Conservative leader Russell Findlay said the decision was a victory for Scotland's oil and gas workers, but added: "They should never have had to wait this long for common sense to prevail."

苏格兰保守党领袖拉塞尔·芬德利（Russell Findlay）表示，这一决定是苏格兰油气工人的胜利，但他补充说：“他们本不应该等待这么久才让常识占上风。”

Business leaders in Aberdeen said it was "exactly the sort of pragmatic call the North Sea has been crying out for", and unions also welcomed the decision.

阿伯丁的商业领袖表示，这是“北海一直呼吁的务实决定”，工会也欢迎这一决定。

Why is the Jackdaw gas field approval so controversial?

为什么杰克道气田的批准如此有争议？

But the co-leader of the Scottish Greens, Ross Greer, described the approval as "climate vandalism".

但苏格兰绿党联合领袖罗斯·格里尔（Ross Greer）将这一批准描述为“气候破坏”。

He said: "It is a devastating betrayal of our children's future and it won't even lower energy bills in the here and now."

他说：“这是对我们孩子未来的毁灭性背叛，而且甚至不会降低当下的能源账单。”

Environmental campaigners argue that this summer's heatwaves demonstrate the need to tackle climate change by rejecting both Jackdaw and Rosebank.

环保人士认为，今年夏天的热浪表明有必要通过拒绝杰克道和罗斯班克来应对气候变化。

Areeba Hamid, the co-executive director of Greenpeace, said approving Jackdaw was "the wrong decision" in terms of lowering energy bills, providing energy security, and addressing climate change.

绿色和平组织联合执行主任阿雷巴·哈米德（Areeba Hamid）表示，批准杰克道在降低能源账单、提供能源安全和应对气候变化方面是“错误的决定”。

Oxfam said the government had "drilled straight through the UK's climate commitments" and accused the prime minister of sending "the wrong signal as the impacts of climate change intensify".

乐施会表示，政府“直接钻穿了英国的气候承诺”，并指责首相在气候变化影响加剧之际发出了“错误的信号”。

The charity says the government must commit to not approving any new oil and gas licences, including the proposals for Rosebank.

该慈善机构表示，政府必须承诺不批准任何新的油气许可证，包括罗斯班克的提案。

Protesters gathered outside the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero in Westminster on Wednesday morning

周三上午，抗议者聚集在威斯敏斯特的能源安全与净零排放部门外。

Jackdaw sits close to the boundary between the British and Norwegian sectors of the North Sea, in an area where Westminster originally granted licences to explore for oil and gas in 1970 and 1989.

杰克道靠近北海英国和挪威扇区的边界，位于威斯敏斯特最初于1970年和1989年授予油气勘探许可证的区域。

The field was discovered in 2005 but it was expensive and technically difficult to exploit.

该气田于2005年被发现，但开采成本高昂且技术难度大。

Shell did not commit to its development until 2022, shortly after the Conservative government had granted consent for production.

壳牌直到2022年才承诺开发，当时保守党政府刚刚批准生产。

But in January 2025, after a challenge by environmentalists, the Court of Session in Edinburgh ruled that consent for Jackdaw and Rosebank had been granted unlawfully .

但在2025年1月，在环保人士的挑战下，爱丁堡的苏格兰最高民事法院裁定，杰克道和罗斯班克的许可为非法授予。

The judge, Lord Ericht, said the developers should have provided a more detailed account of the fields' impact on climate change as part of the permitting process.

法官埃里希特勋爵（Lord Ericht）表示，开发商应在许可过程中提供更详细的气田对气候影响的说明。

The judge allowed the companies to continue with preparatory work short of producing oil and gas while they crafted new environmental impact assessments taking into account the eventual effect of actually burning the fossil fuels, not just the emissions which would be created in extracting them.

法官允许公司在不生产油气的情况下继续进行准备工作，同时制定新的环境影响评估，考虑实际燃烧化石燃料的最终影响，而不仅仅是开采过程中产生的排放。

然而，英国政府此前曾表示，杰克道对能源安全影响甚微，对降低国内燃气和电费没有影响。

8月，在对该国天然气供应未来咨询的临时回应中，能源部长迈克尔·尚克斯（Michael Shanks）表示，随着国内产量下降，英国将不可避免地更加依赖进口。

“随着过去几十年北海产量的减少，英国大陆架生产的任何天然气都只占全球需求的一小部分，因此不会降低价格，也不会实质性增加供应，”尚克斯写道。
