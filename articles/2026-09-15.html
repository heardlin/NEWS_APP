Baby 'cot cams' plan after inquiry finds Lucy Letby crimes could have been prevented

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Lucy Letby was convicted of the murders of seven babies and the attempted murder of seven others

露西·莱比（Lucy Letby）被判谋杀七名婴儿并企图谋杀另外七名婴儿。

Health Secretary Yvette Cooper has said officials will "urgently develop plans" to introduce live-streaming cameras on England's baby wards after an inquiry into Lucy Letby's murders and attempted murders.

卫生大臣伊薇特·库珀（Yvette Cooper）表示，在对露西·莱比的谋杀和谋杀未遂案进行调查后，官员们将“紧急制定计划”，在英格兰的婴儿病房引入实时监控摄像头。

Inquiry chair Lady Justice Thirlwall called for a series of urgent reforms to neonatal units, including CCTV for all cots and incubators, and restricting access to insulin.

调查委员会主席瑟尔沃尔女法官（Lady Justice Thirlwall）呼吁对新生儿病房进行一系列紧急改革，包括为所有婴儿床和保温箱安装闭路电视，并限制胰岛素的获取。

Thirlwall said some of the babies Letby killed could have been saved if managers at the Countess of Chester Hospital had taken action earlier.

瑟尔沃尔表示，如果切斯特伯爵夫人医院（Countess of Chester Hospital）的管理层早些采取行动，莱比杀害的一些婴儿本可以获救。

Her report criticises a "complete failure to protect babies" and a "profound failure of management, governance and safeguarding" at the hospital.

她的报告批评该医院“完全未能保护婴儿”，以及“管理、治理和保护方面的严重失职”。

Thirlwall said this was because "no one seems to have understood that safeguarding action is required when a member of staff is suspected of causing deliberate harm and does not require colleagues to be sure of guilt."

瑟尔沃尔表示，这是因为“似乎没有人理解，当怀疑工作人员故意造成伤害时，就需要采取保护措施，而不需要同事确信其有罪。”

She said it would never be possible to know exactly how many lives could have been saved if safeguarding procedures had been properly followed.

她说，如果保护程序得到正确遵循，永远无法确切知道本可以挽救多少生命。

One of the senior doctors who tried to raise concerns about Letby with hospital managers, consultant paediatrician Dr John Gibbs, now retired, told the BBC the Thirlwall Inquiry report was "grim reading".

曾试图向医院管理层提出对莱比的担忧的高级医生之一、现已退休的儿科顾问医生约翰·吉布斯博士（Dr John Gibbs）告诉BBC，瑟尔沃尔调查报告是“令人沮丧的阅读材料”。

In a rare interview he said he accepted that consultants must bear "collective" responsibility for some of the failings identified in the report, and said he wished they had been "brave enough to follow our suspicions and escalate things to the police earlier".

在一次罕见的采访中，他说他接受顾问医生必须对报告中指出的一些失误承担“集体”责任，并表示他希望他们“有足够的勇气追随我们的怀疑，并更早地向警方报告”。

Chief executive and registrar at the Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC), Paul Rees, said he was "truly sorry for the NMC's failings" in the Letby case and that "we also did not act quickly enough to suspend Lucy Letby".

护理和助产委员会（Nursing and Midwifery Council, NMC）首席执行官兼注册官保罗·里斯（Paul Rees）表示，他对NMC在莱比案中的“失职 truly sorry”，并且“我们也没有足够快地暂停露西·莱比的职务”。

Sharing her findings at Liverpool Town Hall, Thirlwall described "dysfunctional management and governance, a gulf between hospital leadership and clinicians, and failure to understand the fundamentals of safeguarding".

在利物浦市政厅分享她的调查结果时，瑟尔沃尔描述了“功能失调的管理和治理、医院领导层与临床医生之间的鸿沟，以及未能理解保护的基本原则”。

Letby was moved on to administrative duties in July 2016 after consultants expressed concerns about her to the hospital's executive team.

2016年7月，在顾问医生向医院执行团队表达了对莱比的担忧后，她被调往行政岗位。

But the inquiry has found that alarm bells should have been sounded earlier.

但调查发现，警报本应更早响起。

There were several "missed opportunities" for hospital bosses to intervene, the report said, including when three babies died in one cluster in June 2015.

报告称，医院老板有多次“错失的机会”进行干预，包括2015年6月有三名婴儿在一个集群中死亡时。

Parents of the babies were "kept in the dark for years" over the concerns that their babies might have been deliberately harmed, the inquiry also found.

调查还发现，婴儿的父母“多年来一直被蒙在鼓里”，不知道他们的孩子可能被故意伤害的担忧。

Thirlwall described the lack of consideration shown to parents as "reprehensible".

瑟尔沃尔将这种对父母缺乏考虑的行为描述为“应受谴责”。

Repeatedly untruthful, callous and quiet - what we learned about Lucy Letby from inquiry report

反复不诚实、冷酷和安静——我们从调查报告中学到的关于露西·莱比的内容

The report is highly critical of managers at the Countess of Chester Hospital.

报告对切斯特伯爵夫人医院的管理层提出了高度批评。

Hospital bosses carried out a number of internal reviews into increased infant mortality in 2015 and 2016, but did not invite Cheshire Police to investigate until May 2017.

医院老板在2015年和2016年对婴儿死亡率上升进行了多次内部审查，但直到2017年5月才邀请柴郡警方（Cheshire Police）进行调查。

Letby remained on site until her arrest more than a year later.

莱比在一年多后被捕之前一直留在医院。

Cheshire Police said it would "carefully review" the contents of the report and "fully consider" relevant recommendations.

柴郡警方表示将“仔细审查”报告内容，并“充分考虑”相关建议。

Thirlwall said the hospital's former chief executive Tony Chambers behaved in a "dictatorial manner" and had "intended" to obstruct a police investigation into deaths on the ward.

瑟尔沃尔表示，医院前首席执行官托尼·钱伯斯（Tony Chambers）行为“独裁”，并“意图”阻碍警方对病房死亡事件的调查。

Chambers, along with director of nursing and head of safeguarding Alison Kelly and medical director Ian Harvey, "dismissed the idea that Letby was deliberately harming babies", Thirlwall added.

瑟尔沃尔补充说，钱伯斯以及护理主任兼保护负责人艾莉森·凯利（Alison Kelly）和医疗主任伊恩·哈维（Ian Harvey）“驳回了莱比故意伤害婴儿的想法”。

But whether these managers believed Letby was harming babies or not was "irrelevant", Thirlwall said, adding that action should have been taken as soon as concerns were raised.

但瑟尔沃尔说，这些管理人员是否相信莱比伤害了婴儿是“无关紧要的”，并补充说，一旦提出担忧，就应该立即采取行动。

In a joint statement, Chambers, Kelly and Harvey along with the hospital's former HR director Sue Hodkinson said: "We are carefully reviewing the Thirlwall report and its recommendations.

钱伯斯、凯利和哈维以及医院前人力资源主任苏·霍奇金森（Sue Hodkinson）在一份联合声明中表示：“我们正在仔细审查瑟尔沃尔报告及其建议。”

"Given that there are a number of investigations under way, and that the work of the Criminal Cases Review Commission has not yet completed, it would be inappropriate for us to offer any further comment at this time.

“鉴于有多项调查正在进行中，且刑事案件审查委员会（Criminal Cases Review Commission）的工作尚未完成，我们此时提供任何进一步评论都是不合适的。”

"Our thoughts remain with the families affected by the tragic events that took place at the Countess of Chester Hospital."

“我们的思绪与受切斯特伯爵夫人医院悲剧事件影响的家庭同在。”

Letby "should have been removed from the ward," Thirlwall says

瑟尔沃尔说，莱比“本应被移出病房”。

The inquiry also condemned wider NHS culture, finding evidence of "toxic negativity" that discouraged whistleblowers.

调查还谴责了更广泛的NHS文化，发现存在“有毒的消极情绪”，阻碍了举报人。

The chair of the British Medical Association said on Tuesday that whistleblowers in the NHS "deserve strong protections, and the inquiry makes it clear that these doctors did not get them".

英国医学协会（British Medical Association）主席周二表示，NHS中的举报人“应该得到强有力的保护，调查明确指出这些医生没有得到保护”。

Regulator the Care Quality Commission (CQC) was also criticised, with the report saying it did not show enough curiosity to look beyond what it was being told by the Countess of Chester Hospital.

监管机构护理质量委员会（Care Quality Commission, CQC）也受到批评，报告称它没有表现出足够的好奇心，去超越切斯特伯爵夫人医院告诉它的内容。

Responding to the report, the CQC's chief inspector of hospitals said "crucial information" was not shared by the Countess of Chester, but that the commission as a regulator did not take a "sufficiently investigative and inquiring" approach.

针对报告，CQC医院首席检查员表示，切斯特伯爵夫人医院没有分享“关键信息”，但委员会作为监管机构没有采取“足够调查和询问”的方法。

Dr Tony Onon said the regulator had since changed its approach to assessments.

托尼·奥农博士（Dr Tony Onon）表示，监管机构此后已改变了评估方法。

Health Secretary Yvette Cooper said she was "profoundly sorry" on behalf of the government for the failures set out in the report.

卫生大臣伊薇特·库珀表示，她代表政府为报告中列出的失误“深感抱歉”。

She said she had asked officials to "urgently develop plans" for cot-cams in neonatal units, and that the government would consider Thirlwall's other recommendations in full.

她说，她已要求官员们“紧急制定计划”，在新生儿病房安装婴儿床摄像头，政府将全面考虑瑟尔沃尔的其他建议。

Cooper also said she expected NHS leaders across the country to uphold their safeguarding responsibilities.

库珀还表示，她期望全国各地的NHS领导人履行其保护责任。

She plans to meet Thirlwall, who made 17 recommendations including restricting access to insulin, later this week to discuss the inquiry in more detail.

她计划本周晚些时候会见瑟尔沃尔，讨论调查的更多细节。瑟尔沃尔提出了17项建议，包括限制胰岛素的获取。

Lucy Letby has twice been denied permission to appeal against her convictions

露西·莱比两次被拒绝上诉许可。

The consultant paediatrician who had tried to raise his concerns about Letby to hospital management said he accepted the police should have been contacted sooner.

曾试图向医院管理层提出对莱比担忧的儿科顾问医生表示，他接受警方本应更早被联系。

Dr John Gibbs was working at the Countess of Chester Hospital throughout the period in 2015 to 2016 when Letby carried out her crimes.

约翰·吉布斯博士在2015年至2016年期间一直在切斯特伯爵夫人医院工作，当时莱比实施了她的罪行。

"I wish we consultants had been brave enough to follow our suspicions and escalate things to the police earlier," he said.

“我希望我们顾问医生有足够的勇气追随我们的怀疑，并更早地向警方报告，”他说。

"When I say, 'we consultants', I am responsible as well and if my other consultant colleagues didn't go to the police, I should have."

“当我说‘我们顾问医生’时，我也有责任，如果我的其他顾问同事没有去警察局，我应该去。”

He also spoke about the failure of consultants to spot the significance of blood test results that suggested a baby had been poisoned with insulin in August 2015.

他还谈到了顾问医生未能发现2015年8月血液检测结果的意义，该结果表明一名婴儿被胰岛素中毒。

He described this as "a collective team failure".

他将此描述为“团队集体失败”。

He said: "Others of us were on call and covering the neonatal unit over the next week or two before the baby moved out of the unit. We all had the opportunity to review the notes.

他说：“我们中的其他人在婴儿离开病房前的一两周内值班并负责新生儿病房。我们都有机会查看记录。”

"As I admitted when I appeared before the inquiry to give evidence, I feel we failed the babies and I apologise to the families for that.

“正如我在调查中作证时承认的那样，我觉得我们辜负了这些婴儿，我为此向家属道歉。”

"In a careful way, Lady Justice Thirlwall points out how we could and should have escalated matters ultimately to the police earlier than we did."

“瑟尔沃尔女法官以谨慎的方式指出，我们本可以而且应该比我们更早地将事情升级到警方。”

Gibbs said he agreed with the report's finding that hospital executives had multiple opportunities to act and potentially save babies' lives.

吉布斯说，他同意报告的发现，即医院高管有多次机会采取行动，并可能挽救婴儿的生命。

Chief executive and registrar at the Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC), Paul Rees, said his thoughts were with the babies that died or came to harm and their families "who have endured unimaginable suffering and pain".

护理和助产委员会（NMC）首席执行官兼注册官保罗·里斯表示，他的思绪与死亡或受到伤害的婴儿及其家人同在，“他们经历了难以想象的痛苦和煎熬”。

He said: "From the moment concerns about Letby were first brought to the NMC's attention 10 years ago we should have stepped up proactively, shown a sense of professional curiosity and triggered action at a senior level.

他说：“从10年前首次将有关莱比的担忧提请NMC注意的那一刻起，我们就应该主动站出来，表现出专业好奇心，并在高级别触发行动。”

"We should have considered the possibility of deliberate harm much earlier than we did."

“我们应该比我们更早地考虑故意伤害的可能性。”

He said the NMC had since "strengthened our guidance on interim orders", which clarified to "our decision-makers that they can seek to restrict or suspend a professional's practice much earlier".

他说，NMC此后“加强了关于临时命令的指导”，这向“我们的决策者澄清，他们可以更早地寻求限制或暂停专业人士的执业”。

Thirlwall was clear that her inquiry was not an investigation into Letby's guilt, which some supporters of the jailed nurse still contest.

瑟尔沃尔明确表示，她的调查不是对莱比罪行的调查，一些被监禁护士的支持者仍然对此提出异议。

A number of Letby supporters gathered outside Liverpool Town Hall to voice their concerns over her conviction, calling for her release, before Thirlwall delivered her inquiry findings.

在瑟尔沃尔公布调查结果之前，一些莱比的支持者聚集在利物浦市政厅外，对她的定罪表示担忧，并呼吁释放她。

Now 36, Letby is serving 15 whole-life prison terms for murdering seven babies and attempting to murder seven more.

现年36岁的莱比因谋杀七名婴儿并企图谋杀另外七名婴儿而正在服15个终身监禁。

She was convicted in 2023 and has been denied permission to appeal against her convictions twice.

她于2023年被定罪，并两次被拒绝上诉许可。

Today's Eight Words

convict /kənˈvɪkt/ v.

定罪，宣判有罪

To declare someone guilty of a crime in a court of law.

“Lucy Letby was convicted of the murders of seven babies and the attempted murder of seven others”

露西·莱比被判谋杀七名婴儿并企图谋杀另外七名婴儿。

inquiry /ɪnˈkwaɪəri/ n.

调查，询问

An official investigation to find out the truth about something.

“Inquiry chair Lady Justice Thirlwall called for a series of urgent reforms to neonatal units, including CCTV for all cots and incubators, and restricting access to insulin.”

调查主席瑟尔沃尔女法官呼吁对新生儿病房进行一系列紧急改革，包括为所有婴儿床和保温箱安装闭路电视，并限制胰岛素的使用。

safeguard /ˈseɪfɡɑːd/ v.

保护，保障

To protect something or someone from harm or damage.

“Her report criticises a 'complete failure to protect babies' and a 'profound failure of management, governance and safeguarding' at the hospital.”

她的报告批评医院“完全未能保护婴儿”以及“管理、治理和保障方面的严重失败”。

escalate /ˈeskəleɪt/ v.

升级，上报

To make something more serious or to refer an issue to a higher authority.

“In a rare interview he said he accepted that consultants must bear 'collective' responsibility for some of the failings identified in the report, and said he wished they had been 'brave enough to follow our suspicions and escalate things to the police earlier'.”

在一次罕见的采访中，他说他接受顾问们必须对报告中指出的一些失误承担“集体”责任，并表示他希望他们当初能“勇敢地追随自己的怀疑，并更早地将事情上报给警方”。

suspend /səˈspend/ v.

暂停，停职

To officially stop something or prevent someone from doing their job for a period of time.

“Chief executive and registrar at the Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC), Paul Rees, said he was 'truly sorry for the NMC's failings' in the Letby case and that 'we also did not act quickly enough to suspend Lucy Letby'.”

护理和助产委员会（NMC）首席执行官兼注册官保罗·里斯表示，他对NMC在莱比案中的失误“深感抱歉”，并且“我们也没有足够快地采取行动暂停露西·莱比的职务”。

dysfunctional /dɪsˈfʌŋkʃənl/ adj.

功能失调的，不正常的

Not working properly or normally, especially in terms of management or organization.

“Sharing her findings at Liverpool Town Hall, Thirlwall described 'dysfunctional management and governance, a gulf between hospital leadership and clinicians, and failure to understand the fundamentals of safeguarding'.”

在利物浦市政厅分享她的调查结果时，瑟尔沃尔描述了“功能失调的管理和治理、医院领导层与临床医生之间的鸿沟，以及未能理解保障的基本原则”。

obstruct /əbˈstrʌkt/ v.

阻碍，妨碍

To block or interfere with something, making it difficult to progress.

“Thirlwall said the hospital's former chief executive Tony Chambers behaved in a 'dictatorial manner' and had 'intended' to obstruct a police investigation into deaths on the ward.”

瑟尔沃尔说，医院前首席执行官托尼·钱伯斯行为“独断专行”，并“意图”阻碍警方对病房死亡事件的调查。

whistleblower /ˈwɪslbləʊə(r)/ n.

举报人，告密者

A person who reports illegal or unethical activities within an organization.

“The inquiry also condemned wider NHS culture, finding evidence of 'toxic negativity' that discouraged whistleblowers.”

调查还谴责了更广泛的NHS文化，发现存在“有毒的消极情绪”证据，这种情绪打击了举报人。
