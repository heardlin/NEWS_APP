Long-delayed defence spending plan to be published on Tuesday, with focus on drone warfare

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

The defence investment plan will be published on Tuesday, around nine months latter than expected

这份国防投资计划将于周二公布，比预期晚了大约九个月。

A long-delayed military spending plan will be published on Tuesday, with Sir Keir Starmer saying it would keep Britain "safe and secure long into the future".

一项拖延已久的军费开支计划将于周二公布，基尔·斯塔默爵士（Sir Keir Starmer）表示，这将使英国“在未来长期保持安全”。

The Ministry of Defence (MoD) said the defence investment plan (DIP) includes Â£5bn worth of investment to increase the Armed Forces' use of drones and autonomous weapons.

英国国防部（Ministry of Defence, MoD）表示，国防投资计划（defence investment plan, DIP）包括价值50亿英镑的投资，用于增加武装部队对无人机和自主武器的使用。

Earlier this month, the Treasury and No 10 agreed a Â£13.5bn funding increase, well short of the Â£28bn the MoD wanted - though new Defence Secretary Dan Jarvis has pushed for more in recent weeks. His predecessor John Healey resigned over the funding row.

本月早些时候，英国财政部和唐宁街10号同意增加135亿英镑的资金，远低于国防部希望的280亿英镑——尽管新任国防大臣丹·贾维斯（Dan Jarvis）在最近几周争取了更多资金。他的前任约翰·希利（John Healey）因资金争议辞职。

The Conservatives said the plan was "too little, too late", while the Liberal Democrats said it "dangerously short-changed our Armed Forces".

保守党表示该计划“太少、太晚”，而自由民主党则表示该计划“危险地亏待了我们的武装部队”。

Despite his impending departure from Downing Street, Prime Minister Sir Keir is pushing ahead with the DIP.

尽管即将离开唐宁街，首相基尔·斯塔默爵士仍在推进国防投资计划。

Tense Whitehall negotiations over how to fund it have been ongoing for months, with departments across government asked to make cuts.

关于如何为其提供资金的紧张白厅谈判已持续数月，政府各部门被要求削减开支。

The row has seen two defence ministers resign over what they said was an inadequate funding increase in the original version of the plan.

这场争议已导致两位国防部长辞职，他们称原版计划中的资金增加不足。

The DIP will also explain how new equipment and defence infrastructure will be funded over the coming decade.

国防投资计划还将解释未来十年如何为新装备和国防基础设施提供资金。

It was initially expected to be published in autumn 2025. Following months of talks over funding, the DIP now arrives shortly before the Nato leaders summit in Turkey on 7 July.

该计划最初预计于2025年秋季公布。经过数月的资金谈判，国防投资计划现在于7月7日北约领导人峰会前夕在土耳其发布。

Earlier this month, Healey quit the government, saying the DIP fell "well short" of what is needed to protect the UK and meet existing spending commitments, while Armed Forces Minister Al Carns quit saying it was not "transformative enough" in the face of rapidly-evolving warfare.

本月早些时候，希利退出政府，称国防投资计划“远未达到”保护英国和满足现有支出承诺所需的标准，而武装部队部长阿尔·卡恩斯（Al Carns）辞职，称该计划在快速演变的战争面前“变革性不足”。

It has been reported Jarvis has secured some extra money for the plan. He has has also spent the last two weeks "refocusing" the DIP to take on more of the lessons from Ukraine and Iran, the MoD said.

据报道，贾维斯已为该计划争取到一些额外资金。国防部表示，他还花了最后两周时间“重新调整”国防投资计划，以更多地吸取乌克兰和伊朗的教训。

This includes how drones have been used to destroy high-value targets, with Jarvis saying the "character of warfare is rapidly changing".

这包括无人机如何被用于摧毁高价值目标，贾维斯表示“战争的性质正在迅速变化”。

He said: "In Ukraine and the Middle East, uncrewed systems are defining conflicts.

他说：“在乌克兰和中东，无人系统正在定义冲突。”

"This largest ever UK investment into these evolving technologies will help our Armed Forces stay ahead of our adversaries, backed by the best of our defence industry."

“英国对这些不断发展的技术进行的这项最大投资，将有助于我们的武装部队在国防工业的最佳支持下保持领先于对手。”

The Ministry of Defence has said plans to replace ageing warship will be scrapped in favour of building at least six new modern "hybrid" vessels equipped to deploy drones.

国防部表示，更换老旧军舰的计划将被取消，转而建造至少六艘配备无人机的新型现代化“混合”舰艇。

Sir Keir said there would be "game-changing investment" to strengthen the Armed Forces on land, at sea and in the air.

基尔爵士表示，将进行“改变游戏规则的投资”，以加强陆海空武装部队。

He said: "Today's defence investment plan will help drive growth across the UK, giving our industrial base the confidence, certainty and support it needs to develop and scale the technologies that will keep our country safe and secure long into the future."

他说：“今天的国防投资计划将有助于推动英国各地的增长，为我们的工业基础提供信心、确定性和支持，以开发和扩大那些将确保我们国家长期安全的技术。”

His remarks come after unions and defence firms warned the continued delays to the DIP were a "threat" to British jobs, skills and national security.

他的言论是在工会和国防公司警告国防投资计划的持续拖延对英国就业、技能和国家安全构成“威胁”之后发表的。

弗兰克·加德纳（Frank Gardner）：海军计划反映了战争方式的巨变

Burnham will need a Moscow test as well as Makerfield test, says ex-military chief

前军事首长：伯纳姆（Burnham）不仅需要梅克菲尔德（Makerfield）考验，还需要莫斯科考验

UK forces face operational cuts without more cash, defence chief warns

国防首长警告：没有更多资金，英国军队将面临行动削减

Sir Keir's decision to publish the DIP in his final weeks as prime minister could cause tension with his successor, who may want to revise defence spending when they take over.

基尔爵士在担任首相的最后几周决定公布国防投资计划，可能会与他的继任者产生紧张关系，后者可能希望在接任后修改国防开支。

The Sunday Times reported that Andy Burnham, who is widely expected to succeed Sir Keir as prime minister next month, has seen the DIP and approved it, though No 10 declined to confirm that on Monday.

《星期日泰晤士报》报道称，被广泛预期下月接替基尔爵士成为首相的安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）已看到国防投资计划并批准了它，尽管唐宁街10号周一拒绝证实这一点。

Burnham is the only candidate to have formally joined the Labour leadership race.

伯纳姆是唯一正式加入工党领袖竞选的候选人。

He has previously said he would reduce the welfare bill by getting more people into work, thereby ensuring more money for defence.

他此前曾表示，将通过让更多人就业来减少福利支出，从而确保更多资金用于国防。

Shadow defence secretary James Cartlidge said the DIP was "being rushed through because Keir Starmer is desperate for a legacy".

影子国防大臣詹姆斯·卡特利奇（James Cartlidge）表示，国防投资计划“正在仓促通过，因为基尔·斯塔默急于留下政治遗产”。

He said: "This plan is not worth the paper it's written on - Keir Starmer is prime minister in name only.

他说：“这份计划一文不值——基尔·斯塔默只是名义上的首相。”

"The next prime minister needs to cut welfare and give our armed forces the funding they need to keep Britain safe."

“下一任首相需要削减福利，并给予我们的武装部队所需的资金，以保障英国的安全。”

Lib Dem leader Sir Ed Davey said: "The government has dangerously short-changed our armed forces when they need urgent investment after years of Conservative negligence.

自由民主党领袖埃德·戴维爵士（Sir Ed Davey）表示：“在保守党多年忽视后，政府危险地亏待了急需投资的武装部队。”

"Defence chiefs have been forced to make hard choices, when they should be given what they need."

“国防首长被迫做出艰难选择，而他们本应得到所需的一切。”

The DIP follows the wide-ranging Strategic Defence Review (SDR) published in June 2025.

国防投资计划是在2025年6月发布的广泛战略防务评估（Strategic Defence Review, SDR）之后出台的。

The SDR outlined a shift towards "warfighting readiness" to deter threats and pledged billions in extra spending for extra ammunition, next-generation fast jets, drones, and new attack submarines.

战略防务评估概述了向“战备状态”转变以威慑威胁，并承诺额外支出数十亿英镑用于更多弹药、下一代高速喷气机、无人机和新攻击潜艇。

Nato General Secretary Mark Rutte has previously urged the alliance's members to present "clear, concrete and credible plans" for how they will raise defence spending ahead of next month's summit.

北约秘书长马克·吕特（Mark Rutte）此前敦促联盟成员在下月峰会前提出“明确、具体和可信的计划”，说明他们将如何提高国防开支。

The UK government has vowed to increase defence spending to 3.5% of GDP by 2035, in line with Nato commitments.

英国政府已承诺到2035年将国防开支增加到GDP的3.5%，以符合北约承诺。
