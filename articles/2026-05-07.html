When will we know the results for the England, Scotland and Wales elections?

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

The biggest set of elections since the 2024 general election has seen millions of people head to the polls.

自2024年大选以来最大规模的选举中，数百万人前往投票站投票。

Voters in Scotland and Wales are electing representatives to their national parliaments.

苏格兰和威尔士的选民正在选举其国家议会的代表。

In England, about 5,000 seats across 136 councils and six mayoral contests are being decided.

在英格兰，136个议会约5000个席位以及六场市长竞选正在决出结果。

Now that the polls have closed the focus turns to the results.

投票站关闭后，焦点转向了结果。

The timing of the counts - and the expected results - vary across the elections.

计票时间——以及预期结果——因选举而异。

Previously, votes have been counted as soon as polls closed on Thursday.

此前，投票在周四关闭后立即开始计票。

This time, only a few local authorities will begin counting on Thursday night, with most starting on Friday morning.

这次，只有少数地方当局将在周四晚上开始计票，大多数从周五早上开始。

The first results are expected to be announced in the early hours on Friday.

首批结果预计在周五凌晨公布。

It is expected that the latest will be known by Saturday evening.

预计最晚的结果将在周六晚上之前知晓。

Votes will begin to be counted for Scotland's constituencies and regional lists on Friday morning.

苏格兰选区和地区名单的投票将于周五上午开始计票。

It's likely that the shape of the new parliament will be clear by early Friday evening.

新议会的格局可能在周五傍晚前明朗。

But each count is the responsibility of a local returning officer and there may be delays or recounts.

但每次计票由地方选举官负责，可能会有延迟或重新计票。

In a Holyrood election such delays could prevent the calculation of full results and it is possible that some seats may not be declared until late Friday evening or even Saturday.

在荷里路德选举中，此类延迟可能阻碍完整结果的计算，部分席位可能要到周五深夜甚至周六才能宣布。

Votes will begin to be counted for all 16 of Wales' constituencies on Friday morning.

威尔士全部16个选区的投票将于周五上午开始计票。

It is estimated that results could then be declared from about lunchtime, continuing into the early evening.

预计结果可能从午餐时间左右开始宣布，持续到傍晚。

But with some experts predicting a higher number of recounts compared to past elections, and the unknowns of a brand-new voting system and new constituencies in the mix, whether Wales' results will be done and dusted before the weekend remains to be seen.

但由于一些专家预测重新计票次数将比以往选举更多，加上全新的投票系统和新选区等未知因素，威尔士的结果能否在周末前尘埃落定仍有待观察。

From 23:00 BST BBC Radio 4 and BBC Radio 5 Live will join forces for an overnight results programme, co-presented by Nick Robinson and Rachel Burden.

从英国夏令时23:00起，BBC广播4台和BBC广播5台将联合播出通宵结果节目，由尼克·罗宾逊（Nick Robinson）和雷切尔·伯登（Rachel Burden）共同主持。

From 23:10 Laura Kuenssberg will host a special programme on BBC One, with analysis from polling expert Sir John Curtice and number-crunching from Reeta Chakrabarti.

从23:10起，劳拉·库恩斯伯格（Laura Kuenssberg）将在BBC一台主持特别节目，由民调专家约翰·柯蒂斯爵士（Sir John Curtice）提供分析，丽塔·查克拉巴蒂（Reeta Chakrabarti）负责数据解读。

Live pages on the BBC News website and app will keep you updated on results and reaction across the UK, with fact-checking from BBC Verify. Follow BBC News on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube for the latest updates on social media.

BBC新闻网站和应用程序的实时页面将为您更新全英各地的结果和反应，并由BBC Verify进行事实核查。关注BBC新闻的TikTok、Instagram和YouTube账号，获取社交媒体上的最新动态。

From 09:30 BST Sophie Raworth will continue election coverage on BBC One after a BBC Breakfast special, with Clive Myrie in Cardiff for the Senedd elections and Kirsty Wark in Edinburgh for the Holyrood vote.

从英国夏令时09:30起，在BBC早餐特别节目之后，索菲·拉沃斯（Sophie Raworth）将继续在BBC一台进行选举报道，克莱夫·迈里（Clive Myrie）在卡迪夫报道塞内德选举，柯斯蒂·沃克（Kirsty Wark）在爱丁堡报道荷里路德投票。

From 09:30 BBC One Scotland and BBC Radio Scotland will bring live declarations, interviews and reporting from every count in Scotland.

从09:30起，BBC一台苏格兰频道和BBC广播苏格兰频道将带来苏格兰各计票点的现场宣布、采访和报道。

From 11:00 a results programme will be broadcast on BBC One Wales and S4C, providing analysis and interviews. James Williams and Dot Davies will present a BBC Radio Wales programme and listeners can join Dewi Llwyd and Kate Crockett on BBC Radio Cymru.

从11:00起，BBC一台威尔士频道和S4C将播出结果节目，提供分析和采访。詹姆斯·威廉姆斯（James Williams）和多特·戴维斯（Dot Davies）将主持BBC广播威尔士节目，听众可以加入德维·卢伊德（Dewi Llwyd）和凯特·克罗克特（Kate Crockett）在BBC广播Cymru的节目。

About 5,000 seats across 136 local councils are up for grabs.

136个地方议会约5000个席位可供争夺。

There are also six mayoral contests, in Watford and five London boroughs - Croydon, Hackney, Lewisham, Newham and Tower Hamlets.

还有六场市长竞选，分别在沃特福德和五个伦敦自治市——克罗伊登、哈克尼、刘易舍姆、纽汉姆和塔村。

In council elections, voters generally have one vote for each available seat in an election area, known as a ward or division. Some wards have more than one representative.

在议会选举中，选民通常对选举区（称为选区或分区）中的每个可用席位投一票。有些选区有多名代表。

Like MPs in general elections, local councillors are elected under the first-past-the-post system. The candidate or candidates with the most votes in each ward win and become councillors.

与大选中的议员一样，地方议员在简单多数制下选举产生。每个选区得票最多的候选人获胜并成为议员。

The political party which wins the most seats gains control of the council.

赢得最多席位的政党获得议会的控制权。

Councils are responsible for local services including care for older people and people with disabilities, rubbish collection and recycling, housing, education and road maintenance.

议会负责地方服务，包括照顾老年人和残疾人、垃圾收集和回收、住房、教育和道路维护。

The Scottish Parliament election will decide who governs the country in key areas such as health and education and, as a result, the direction it takes on many issues.

苏格兰议会选举将决定谁在健康和教育等关键领域治理国家，并因此决定在许多问题上的方向。

All 129 Members of the Scottish Parliament (MSPs) are up for re-election.

所有129名苏格兰议会议员（MSP）都面临连任选举。

There are 73 constituency MSPs and 56 regional MSPs (seven per region) in Holyrood.

荷里路德有73名选区议员和56名地区议员（每个地区七名）。

Each voter had two ballot papers and cast two votes, one to elect their constituency MSP and one to elect their area's regional MSPs.

每位选民有两张选票并投两票，一票选举其选区议员，一票选举其地区的地区议员。

Constituency MSPs are selected directly under first past the post, with the winner being the candidate who receives the most votes.

选区议员在简单多数制下直接选举产生，得票最多的候选人获胜。

The process to select regional MSPs uses a type of proportional representation.

选举地区议员的过程使用一种比例代表制。

Voters chose either a political party or an individual candidate from a list. Political parties are allocated regional seats according to a formula which includes the number of votes they receive in each region and the number of constituencies they win outright.

选民从名单中选择一个政党或一名独立候选人。政党根据一个公式分配地区席位，该公式包括他们在每个地区获得的票数以及他们直接赢得的选区数量。

Usually the party with the most MSPs forms the Scottish government, and its leader becomes first minister.

通常，拥有最多议员的政党组成苏格兰政府，其领导人成为首席大臣。

The current Scottish Parliament was set up in 1999, when Scotland already had its own legal and education system.

目前的苏格兰议会成立于1999年，当时苏格兰已经拥有自己的法律和教育体系。

Holyrood is now responsible for a much broader range of issues including health, housing, policing and prisons, as well as many aspects of transport, welfare and tax.

荷里路德现在负责更广泛的问题，包括健康、住房、警务和监狱，以及交通、福利和税收的许多方面。

It can pass laws in any areas not "reserved" to the UK parliament at Westminster.

它可以在任何未“保留”给威斯敏斯特英国议会的领域通过法律。

The 2026 Senedd election represents the biggest change to the parliament since powers began to be transferred to Wales in 1999. It will determine who governs Wales on many key issues.

2026年塞内德选举代表了自1999年权力开始移交给威尔士以来议会最大的变化。它将决定谁在许多关键问题上治理威尔士。

The number of Members of the Senedd (MSs) is being expanded from 60 to 96 representatives from 16 newly devised constituencies .

塞内德议员（MS）人数从60人扩大到96人，来自16个新设立的选区。

Political parties could list up to eight candidates for each constituency. Independent candidates could also stand. Voters chose one party or one independent candidate.

政党可以为每个选区列出最多八名候选人。独立候选人也可以参选。选民选择一个政党或一名独立候选人。

The 96 seats will then be allocated according to the share of votes received by each party or independent candidate.

然后，96个席位将根据每个政党或独立候选人获得的选票份额进行分配。

The party that wins the most seats in the Senedd election would expect to lead the government. However, no party has ever won a majority in the Welsh Parliament and the new system makes it highly unlikely that will change at this election.

在塞内德选举中赢得最多席位的政党有望领导政府。然而，从未有政党在威尔士议会赢得多数席位，新制度使得这次选举中这种情况极不可能改变。

Any MS can nominate any member to be first minister. If there are two or more candidates, MSs will vote to decide who they want to fill the role.

任何议员都可以提名任何成员担任首席大臣。如果有两名或更多候选人，议员将投票决定他们希望谁担任该职务。

The National Assembly for Wales opened in 1999.

威尔士国民议会于1999年成立。

Renamed Senedd Cymru or Welsh Parliament in 2020, its responsibilities now include health, schools, transport and farming, as well as some tax powers.

2020年更名为塞内德Cymru或威尔士议会，其职责现在包括健康、学校、交通和农业，以及一些税收权力。
