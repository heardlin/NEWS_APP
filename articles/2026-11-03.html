Migrant care workers given 'five year path' to remain in UK - home secretary

移民护理工作者获得留在英国的“五年路径”——内政大臣

Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood has scrapped plans to make foreign care workers already in the UK wait 15 years to qualify for permanent settlement.

内政大臣沙巴纳·马哈茂德（Shabana Mahmood）取消了让已在英国的外国护理工作者等待15年才有资格获得永久居留权的计划。

Mahmood had proposed doubling the wait for indefinite leave to remain (ILR) from five years to 10 for most migrants, with some facing a 15-year wait.

马哈茂德曾提议将大多数移民获得无限期居留许可（ILR）的等待时间从五年延长至十年，其中一些人需等待15年。

But following a backlash from several Labour MPs, Mahmood revised the policy and care workers who earn at least Â£25,000 a year and arrived in the UK before May 2025 would continue to qualify after five years.

但在遭到几位工党议员的反对后，马哈茂德修订了政策，年收入至少2.5万英镑且在2025年5月前抵达英国的护理工作者将继续在五年后有资格申请。

The Conservatives said Mahmood's "U-turn" would cost taxpayers billions of pounds.

保守党表示，马哈茂德的“逆转”将让纳税人损失数十亿英镑。

ILR, also known as settlement, gives a person the right to live, work and study in the UK for as long as they like and apply for benefits if they are eligible. They can also use it to apply for British citizenship.

无限期居留许可（ILR），也称为定居，赋予个人在英国无限期生活、工作和学习的权利，如果符合条件还可以申请福利。他们也可以用它来申请英国公民身份。

Last November, Mahmood announced plans to delay settlements in response to concerns over the number of people approaching eligibility, in the wake of record post-Brexit net migration figures under the previous Conservative government, nicknamed the "Boriswave".

去年11月，马哈茂德宣布了延迟定居的计划，以回应人们对即将符合资格的人数担忧，此前保守党政府时期英国脱欧后的净移民人数创下纪录，被戏称为“鲍里斯浪潮”。

Asked by the BBC whether she had u-turned on her earlier promises, she replied "I don't accept that", adding that some 500,000 people in the UK "will not now settle at five years".

当被BBC问及她是否逆转了先前的承诺时，她回答说“我不接受这种说法”，并补充说英国约有50万人“现在不会在五年后定居”。

"They will have to wait longer before they can achieve settlement in this country, and I've come to a fair resolution for care workers," she said.

“他们必须等待更长时间才能在这个国家获得定居，我为护理工作者找到了一个公平的解决方案，”她说。

The Home Office has said its figures show net migration - the difference between those entering and leaving the country - added 2.6m people to the UK population between 2021 and 2024.

内政部表示，其数据显示净移民——进入和离开该国的人数之差——在2021年至2024年间为英国人口增加了260万人。

The department estimates that between 2026 and 2030 between 1.3m and 2.2m people will settle in the UK, adding its central estimate has increased to 1.9m.

该部门估计，在2026年至2030年间，将有130万至220万人在英国定居，其中央估计已增至190万。

The home secretary faced criticism for proposing to apply the changes to migrant workers already in the UK, with Housing Secretary Angela Rayner previously saying this would "change the goalposts" .

内政大臣因提议将变更适用于已在英国的移民工人而面临批评，住房大臣安吉拉·雷纳（Angela Rayner）此前表示这将“改变游戏规则”。

Several Labour MPs also described the retrospective approach as "un-British" and warned it could worsen the UK's skills shortage, particularly in the care sector.

几位工党议员也将这种追溯性做法描述为“不符合英国精神”，并警告这可能加剧英国的技能短缺，尤其是在护理行业。

Making a statement to the House of Commons, Mahmood said settlement "should not be automatic, it should be earned".

马哈茂德在下议院发表声明称，定居“不应是自动的，而应是赢得的”。

The changes will move those earning less than Â£50,000 a year onto a 10-year path to settlement status, unless they are exempt.

这些变更将使年收入低于5万英镑的人进入10年获得定居身份的路径，除非他们获得豁免。

Mahmood also told MPs the government would provide a five-year path to settlement for care workers who "answered our call when we needed them" following the Covid-19 pandemic.

马哈茂德还告诉议员们，政府将为在新冠疫情后“在我们需要时响应我们号召”的护理工作者提供五年获得定居的路径。

Mahmood said: "Those who earn Â£25,000 a year working in the social care sector, which was the condition of their visa, will therefore be able to settle on their existing five-year path."

马哈茂德说：“那些在社会护理行业工作、年收入2.5万英镑的人，这是他们签证的条件，因此将能够按照现有的五年路径定居。”

She added those care workers who did not meet the conditions of their visa could apply for another five-year visa and seek settlement after that.

她补充说，那些不符合签证条件的护理工作者可以申请另一个五年签证，并在之后寻求定居。

higher earners on salaries of more than Â£50,000 and those who have contributed in healthcare and education, such as doctors and teachers, will be able to qualify for settlement after five years

年收入超过5万英镑的高收入者以及在医疗和教育领域做出贡献的人，如医生和教师，将在五年后有资格获得定居。

refugees who arrived in the UK after crossing the Channel by small boat or another illegal route will have to wait 20 years if they work or study, and 30 years if they do not.

乘坐小船或其他非法途径穿越英吉利海峡抵达英国的难民，如果工作或学习，将需要等待20年，如果不工作或不学习，则需等待30年。

Mahmood said: "Our changes ensure those who are net contributors are encouraged to come to this country and stay here."

马哈茂德说：“我们的变更确保那些净贡献者被鼓励来到这个国家并留在这里。”

She added: "As a result, the Department for Work and Pensions estimates that our reforms will reduce the benefits bill by billions of pounds."

她补充说：“因此，工作和养老金部估计，我们的改革将减少数十亿英镑的福利法案。”

The original version of the plans announced by Mahmood last year would have delayed 799,000 people from gaining permanent residency. Under the updated plans announced on Monday, that number would drop to 517,000.

马哈茂德去年宣布的计划原始版本将延迟79.9万人获得永久居留权。根据周一宣布的更新计划，这一数字将降至51.7万人。

One of those affected was Ashfak Juned, a Bangladeshi chef working in Birmingham, who told the BBC he was "crying" when he heard his wait for settled status would now last until 2033.

受影响的人之一是阿什法克·朱内德（Ashfak Juned），一位在伯明翰工作的孟加拉国厨师，他告诉BBC，当他听说等待定居身份将 now 持续到2033年时，他“哭了”。

The home secretary also confirmed a number of groups would see their settlement path remain as it is today - including Hong Kong British nationals overseas, armed forces members, people with a British connection and those on legacy routes including the Windrush scheme.

内政大臣还确认，一些群体的定居路径将保持现状——包括香港英国国民（海外）、武装部队成员、与英国有联系的人以及包括温德拉什计划在内的传统路径的人。

The Refugee Council's Imran Hussain said the plans for those who had travelled to the UK illegally or by small boat and do not work or study to wait 30 years for ILR were "unfair and unworkable".

难民委员会的伊姆兰·侯赛因（Imran Hussain）表示，对于那些非法或乘小船前往英国且不工作或不学习的人等待30年才能获得ILR的计划是“不公平且不可行的”。

"The fairest reforms would be making fast and good quality asylum decisions, with those refugees granted protection being helped to rebuild their lives and to contribute, and those with no right to remain helped to leave with dignity," he said.

“最公平的改革将是做出快速且高质量的庇护决定，帮助那些获得保护的难民重建生活并做出贡献，并帮助那些无权留下的人有尊严地离开，”他说。

For the Conservatives, shadow home secretary Chris Philp said opposition from Mahmood's fellow ministers and the "Labour open borders left" had resulted in the changes from Mahmood's original proposals, adding that she was "no longer running the government's immigration policy".

对于保守党，影子内政大臣克里斯·菲尔普（Chris Philp）表示，马哈茂德的同僚部长和“工党开放边界左翼”的反对导致了马哈茂德原始提案的变更，并补充说她“不再负责政府的移民政策”。

但下议院任职时间最长的男性议员、议会之父爱德华·利爵士（Sir Edward Leigh）批评他的保守党同事在脱欧后监督了所谓的鲍里斯浪潮，当时净移民在2023年达到近100万的峰值。

"Some of us don't have amnesia," he said.

“我们有些人没有失忆症，”他说。

Most people who come to the UK do so legally, after being granted a visa before they travel.

大多数来英国的人都是合法的，在旅行前获得签证。

A visa gives someone permission to enter or stay in the UK for a specific purpose, such as work, study or joining family members.

签证赋予某人以特定目的进入或留在英国的许可，例如工作、学习或与家人团聚。

There were 727,487 people who came to the UK via visas from July 2025 to June 2026, not including visitors or people transiting through the UK. This was down by 15% on the same period the previous year.

从2025年7月到2026年6月，有727,487人通过签证来到英国，不包括游客或过境英国的人。这比去年同期下降了15%。

That does not mean all those people travelled to the UK, only that they were approved to do so.

这并不意味着所有这些人实际前往了英国，只是他们被批准这样做。

The most common type of visa people came to the UK on was study, which made up 53% of all visa entries from July 2025 to June 2026.

人们来英国最常见的签证类型是学习签证，占2025年7月至2026年6月所有签证入境的53%。

People from China were the most common group to come to the UK on study visas, making up 23% of the total.

中国人是持学习签证来英国最常见的群体，占总数的23%。

Together, people from China and India accounted for nearly half of all study visas. This has been the case for many years.

中国和印度的人合计占所有学习签证的近一半。这种情况已经持续多年。

Liberal Democrat home affairs spokesman Max Wilkinson said the government's reforms were "unravelling".

自由民主党内政事务发言人马克斯·威尔金森（Max Wilkinson）表示，政府的改革正在“瓦解”。
