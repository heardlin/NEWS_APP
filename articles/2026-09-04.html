It is just after 8pm in Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, and the light is fading.

就在晚上8点过后，位于米德尔斯堡附近的格兰奇镇，天色渐暗。

Two teenagers in balaclavas tear across a park on a muddy red quad bike, pulling wheelies as they pass a man walking his dog.

两名戴着巴拉克拉瓦帽的青少年骑着泥泞的红色四轮摩托车穿过公园，经过一位遛狗的男子时还玩起了前轮离地特技。

Younger boys, some not yet in their teens, gather excitedly around the bike.

更小的男孩们，有些还未到青少年，兴奋地围在摩托车旁。

We're here because Grangetown and neighbouring South Bank now find themselves at the centre of a large police operation, with fears organised crime is out of control after the deaths of two police officers and five young men in a car crash on the A66.

我们来这里是因为格兰奇镇和邻近的南岸现在处于大规模警方行动的中心，人们担心有组织犯罪已失控，此前A66公路上发生车祸，导致两名警察和五名年轻男子死亡。

Suddenly one of our team is hit by a pellet, which appears to have been fired from the group. It stings but he's OK.

突然，我们团队中的一人被一颗弹丸击中，似乎是从那群人中射出的。虽然刺痛，但他没事。

Then the quad riders pull over for a chat, engine running.

随后，骑四轮摩托车的人靠边停车聊天，引擎还在运转。

I ask the riders if it is normal to be driving around the park on a quad bike.

我问骑手，在公园里骑四轮摩托车是否正常。

"Yeah, this isn't any special day," the passenger says. "You're born and bred and brought up with it. What do you expect? You don't know anything different."

“是的，这没什么特别的，”乘客说。“你生在这里，长在这里，从小就这样。你还能期待什么？你不知道别的样子。”

Since the crash, more than 20 people have been arrested in Middlesbrough and the surrounding area as part of a wider operation into what police have called "serious and organised crime".

车祸发生后，米德尔斯堡及周边地区已有超过20人被捕，这是针对警方所称“严重有组织犯罪”的更大规模行动的一部分。

Sources say there are two feuding gangs at the centre of a spike in violence.

消息人士称，暴力激增的中心是两个敌对帮派。

Is this area out of control, I ask the quad bike riders.

我问骑四轮摩托车的人，这个地区是否失控了。

"No. Definitely not."

“不，绝对不是。”

Then they reveal something we repeatedly heard from young people here. They do not trust the police.

然后他们透露了我们在这里反复听到的年轻人的心声：他们不信任警察。

"They're saying there are gangs, but there are no gangs," the driver says. "It's not London, mate. Do you know what I mean?"

“他们说有帮派，但根本没有帮派，”司机说。“这里不是伦敦，哥们。你懂我的意思吗？”

Like many young people here, they blame the police for the deaths on the A66. The circumstances remain under independent investigation.

像这里的许多年轻人一样，他们指责警方应对A66上的死亡负责。具体情况仍在独立调查中。

The quad bike riders we met said they felt the area wasn't out of control

我们遇到的骑四轮摩托车的人表示，他们认为该地区并未失控

What we do know is that on Saturday 22 August, a Volkswagen Passat that had been pursued by police travelled the wrong way along the A66 and collided head-on with a police vehicle .

我们已知的是，8月22日星期六，一辆被警方追捕的大众帕萨特在A66公路上逆行，与一辆警车正面相撞。

PC Matthew Blades, 37, and PC Tom Clough, 38, were killed. So were all five people in the Passat: Michael Cahill, 23, Jakub Matusiak, 23, Makai Saddington, 18, Theo Rae and Cole Worthy, both 17.

37岁的警察马修·布莱兹和38岁的警察汤姆·克劳夫遇难。帕萨特车内的五人全部遇难：23岁的迈克尔·卡希尔、23岁的雅库布·马图西亚克、18岁的马凯·萨丁顿，以及17岁的西奥·雷和科尔·沃西。

Four of the five young men killed had amassed 30 convictions between them for offences including car theft, drug possession, assaulting police officers and driving offences. Cahill had no convictions.

遇难的五名年轻男子中，有四人共累计30项定罪，罪名包括盗窃汽车、持有毒品、袭击警察和驾驶犯罪。卡希尔没有前科。

The chain of events that ended on the A66 is believed to have begun eight miles away, in a quiet suburban street in Acklam.

据信，最终导致A66事故的事件链始于八英里外的阿克勒姆一条安静的郊区街道。

It feels a world away from Grangetown. Hanging baskets and neat front gardens line the bay windows of 1930s semi-detached homes.

那里感觉与格兰奇镇截然不同。1930年代半独立式住宅的飘窗上挂着吊篮，整洁的前花园排列成行。

Disorder at balloon release unacceptable, says MP

议员称气球释放活动中的混乱不可接受

Who has been arrested in the wake of the A66 fatal crash?

A66致命车祸后谁被逮捕了？

Schools close early for A66 crash funeral

学校因A66车祸葬礼提前放学

Many residents are reluctant to speak. Why? Because of what happened to one house here that stands behind large metal barriers.

许多居民不愿开口。为什么？因为这里的一所房子发生了什么事，现在被大金属屏障围了起来。

A car was rammed into the bay window, exposing the room inside. Some of the other windows were also smashed.

一辆车撞进了飘窗，露出了里面的房间。其他一些窗户也被打碎了。

Fragments of lights and bumper remain scattered across the driveway.

车灯和保险杠的碎片散落在车道上。

The home belongs to Cole Worthy's father, Gye Worthy, and the ramming happened on the same night Cole died .

这所房子属于科尔·沃西的父亲盖伊·沃西，撞击发生在科尔去世的当晚。

Police say the car used in the attack was a Volvo XC60. The BBC understands that Cole Worthy and the four others then set off in the Passat to find the Volvo.

警方称袭击中使用的车辆是一辆沃尔沃XC60。BBC了解到，科尔·沃西和其他四人随后乘坐帕萨特出发去寻找那辆沃尔沃。

Now neighbours describe a street living in fear and say the home has been targeted for more than a year.

现在邻居们描述这条街生活在恐惧中，并说这所房子已成为目标一年多。

There is no suggestion that Gye Worthy is involved in criminal activity.

没有迹象表明盖伊·沃西参与犯罪活动。

Gye Worthy's house was rammed on the same day as the A66 crash, which killed seven people

盖伊·沃西的房子在A66车祸（造成七人死亡）当天被撞击

All but one of the residents who speak to us do so on the condition of anonymity. They are worried that showing their faces could place them or their homes at risk.

与我们交谈的居民中，除一人外，都要求匿名。他们担心露脸可能会使自己或家园面临风险。

What message do the attacks send to people on this street, I ask one.

我问一位居民，这些袭击向这条街上的人传递了什么信息。

"Well, they should be scared, shouldn't they, that something's going to happen to their property if they say anything or get involved. So we don't."

“嗯，他们应该害怕，不是吗，如果他们说了什么或卷入其中，他们的财产可能会出事。所以我们不参与。”

Victoria, who moved here several years ago, says the escalating violence cannot be separated from the wider condition of the town.

几年前搬到这里来的维多利亚说，不断升级的暴力与该镇的更广泛状况密不可分。

"This is a social problem, especially in this area. Teesside has been massively neglected and ignored," she says.

“这是一个社会问题，尤其是在这个地区。蒂斯河畔地区被严重忽视和遗忘了，”她说。

The attack in Acklam is far from Middlesbrough's only recent case of a vehicle being used as a weapon. Back in May, a tractor was driven into a house on Priory Road, and since the A66 crash Cleveland Police has investigated at least two further rammings.

阿克勒姆的袭击远非米德尔斯堡近期唯一一起车辆被用作武器的案件。早在五月，一辆拖拉机撞进了普里奥里路的一所房子，自A66车祸以来，克利夫兰警方已调查了至少两起进一步的撞击事件。

Detectives have also linked their wider investigation to a caravan fire on Attlee Road in Grangetown.

侦探们还将他们更广泛的调查与格兰奇镇阿特利路的一起大篷车火灾联系起来。

"It's getting worse," a nearby resident says. "They're always balaclava-ed up as well, do you know what I mean?"

“情况越来越糟，”一位附近居民说。“他们也总是戴着巴拉克拉瓦帽，你懂我的意思吗？”

"It can't be anything else. Why would you be like you are unless it was drugs?" he says.

“不可能是别的。如果不是毒品，你为什么会这样？”他说。

Whether every incident is part of a single feud has not been established. But taken together, the rammings, fires and arrests have created a powerful sense that violence is being used to intimidate, punish and send messages.

是否每起事件都是单一争斗的一部分尚未确定。但综合来看，撞击、火灾和逮捕造成了一种强烈的印象，即暴力被用来恐吓、惩罚和传递信息。

What is most unsettling for these residents is the sense that nobody is fully in control.

对这些居民来说，最令人不安的是感觉没有人能完全控制局面。

Grangetown and South Bank are communities still living with the void left by the decline of heavy industry. Both now rank among England's most deprived neighbourhoods.

格兰奇镇和南岸是仍承受着重工业衰退留下的空白的社区。两者现在都位列英格兰最贫困的社区之中。

In South Bank, we see a group of teenagers tend three ponies tethered on the grass. Two perform wheelies on electric scooters while another uses a long stick to scatter rubbish.

在南岸，我们看到一群青少年照料着拴在草地上的三匹小马。两人骑着电动滑板车表演前轮离地，另一人用长棍拨弄垃圾。

On the adjoining estate, more than a dozen homes have boarded-up windows.

在相邻的住宅区，有十几户人家的窗户被木板封住。

On a wall are the words "RIP 2UZZI", said locally to have been the nickname of Jakub Matusiak, who lived one street away.

墙上写着“RIP 2UZZI”，当地人说这是雅库布·马图西亚克的昵称，他住在一条街外。

One 17-year-old boy tells us he knew him. "He was my best mate," he says. "It's sad."

一个17岁的男孩告诉我们他认识他。“他是我最好的朋友，”他说。“真难过。”

Elsewhere, three letters are repeated: "FTP" - an expletive directed at the police.

在其他地方，三个字母反复出现：“FTP”——这是针对警察的脏话。

I ask the teenager if he would call the police if another young person approached him carrying a knife.

我问这个少年，如果另一个年轻人拿着刀走近他，他是否会报警。

"No," he says. Before adding: "I've never needed the police in my life. I'd rather die than ring the police."

“不会，”他说。接着补充道：“我这辈子从不需要警察。我宁愿死也不报警。”

Again and again, the young people we speak to express a profound distrust of the police.

我们交谈的年轻人一次又一次地表达了对警察的极度不信任。

Cleveland's Chief Constable Victoria Fuller said what we'd heard was "horrifying".

克利夫兰警察局长维多利亚·富勒说，我们听到的“令人震惊”。

"Yes, there are a small minority of people in the Grangetown area who blight those communities. But we will continue relentlessly to serve our communities."

“是的，格兰奇镇地区有一小部分人破坏了这些社区。但我们将继续不懈地为我们的社区服务。”

The scale of the challenge facing the force and the community it serves is not new.

警方及其服务的社区面临的挑战规模并不新鲜。

The area has some of the highest police-recorded crime rates in England and Wales.

该地区是英格兰和威尔士警方记录犯罪率最高的地区之一。

Its leaders have been warning for years that its resources did not match the demands placed upon it.

其领导人多年来一直警告说，其资源无法满足需求。

But recent inspections show Cleveland is one of the most improved forces in England and Wales, with performance levels also judged to be some of the best in the country.

但最近的检查显示，克利夫兰是英格兰和威尔士进步最大的警察部队之一，其绩效水平也被评为全国最佳之一。

The force has recently arrested 87 people for offences linked to organised crime gangs and over the past two years 16 people have been jailed for such crimes.

该部队最近逮捕了87名与有组织犯罪团伙有关的罪犯，过去两年中有16人因此类犯罪入狱。

Since the crash, more than 200 additional officers have been deployed in Middlesbrough and surrounding communities. Their immediate task is to suppress the violence and identify those directing it.

车祸发生后，已有200多名额外警员被部署到米德尔斯堡及周边社区。他们的直接任务是压制暴力并识别指挥者。

There is also the question of restoring order and rebuilding trust and confidence.

还有恢复秩序、重建信任和信心的问题。

The graffiti is a tribute to 2Uzzi, said to be the nickname of Jakub Matusiak - one of the five people who died in the A66 crash

涂鸦是对2Uzzi的致敬，据说是雅库布·马图西亚克的昵称——他是A66车祸中遇难的五人之一

As darkness settles over Grangetown, the quad bike is still circling the field and one woman, who moved here not long ago, asks us to move our car from outside her home, frightened we will be targeted and her property will become collateral damage.

当夜幕降临格兰奇镇时，四轮摩托车仍在田野上盘旋，一位不久前搬来的女士要求我们把车从她家门前移开，担心我们会成为目标，她的财产会遭到连带损害。

But what worries her more is her children's future. "I'm terrified my son will end up like them, he's only 10, I don't want him to grow up like that," she says gesturing to the lads with balaclavas tearing up the grass on the quad bike.

但更让她担心的是孩子们的未来。“我害怕我儿子会像他们一样，他才10岁，我不想让他那样长大，”她指着那些戴着巴拉克拉瓦帽、骑着四轮摩托车在草地上飞驰的小伙子们说。
