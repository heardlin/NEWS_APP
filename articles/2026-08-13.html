Two seriously hurt as passenger train derails in East Sussex

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Twenty people have been injured, two seriously, after a train derailed in East Sussex and a major incident was declared.

东萨塞克斯郡发生火车脱轨事故，造成20人受伤，其中2人伤势严重，并已宣布发生重大事件。

Network Rail said the 14:24 BST Southern service from London Victoria to Eastbourne came off the tracks close to Lewes railway station, shortly before 16:00 BST.

英国铁路网公司表示，14:24（英国夏令时）从伦敦维多利亚站开往伊斯特本的南方铁路服务列车，在临近刘易斯火车站时脱轨，时间约为16:00（英国夏令时）前不久。

Photographs from the scene showed people standing on top of overturned carriages, with passengers describing "pandemonium" on board and screaming.

现场照片显示人们站在倾覆的车厢顶部，乘客描述车上“一片混乱”并尖叫声不断。

British Transport Police said three carriages derailed, leaving several people trapped. All 150 on board have now been evacuated.

英国交通警察表示，三节车厢脱轨，导致数人被困。目前车上所有150人已全部疏散。

Several rail routes in the area were closed, as emergency teams rushed to the scene and people were advised to avoid the area.

该地区多条铁路线路关闭，紧急救援队伍赶赴现场，并建议人们避开该区域。

Patients were assessed and treated at the scene, with some sent to local hospitals.

伤者在现场接受评估和治疗，部分人被送往当地医院。

British Transport Police Chief Superintendent Dominique Ioannou said in a press briefing on Thursday night that a number of passengers were initially trapped but were all safely evacuated from the train.

英国交通警察总警司多米尼克·伊安努在周四晚的新闻发布会上表示，最初有数名乘客被困，但现已全部安全撤离列车。

She said while two people sustained serious injuries, a further 18 suffered less serious injuries.

她说，有两人伤势严重，另有18人受轻伤。

"The train started rocking violently and then I remember clocking eyes with this guy and then there was a lot of screaming," passenger Rachel Heath said.

乘客雷切尔·希思说：“列车开始剧烈摇晃，然后我记得和一个人对视，接着就是一片尖叫声。”

"It felt like we flew in the air and the carriage landed on its side and it slid. I felt like it was going to completely flip over.

“感觉我们飞到了空中，车厢侧翻并滑行。我觉得它要完全翻过去了。”

"There was a mum who was bleeding from her mouth and a dad and his son who had cuts. It was like a movie."

“有一位母亲嘴角流血，还有一位父亲和他的儿子有割伤。就像电影一样。”

"Everyone had jelly legs," said passenger Rachel Heath

乘客雷切尔·希思说：“大家都吓得腿软。”

Rob Bradley, who was on the train with his six-year-old daughter, said it was "pandemonium".

带着六岁女儿乘车的罗布·布拉德利表示，现场“一片混乱”。

"Our carriage actually jumped upwards and you could tell there was a derailment," he said.

他说：“我们的车厢实际上向上跳起，你能感觉到脱轨了。”

"The emergency brakes then went on the train and everything got thrown forward.

“然后紧急刹车启动，所有东西都被抛向前方。”

"The train kind of skidded for quite a while before it stopped.

“列车滑行了好一会儿才停下来。”

"Only then, when we turned around, did we see the whole back of the train was open and smoke and dust were pouring into the carriage."

“直到那时，我们转过身，才看到列车尾部完全敞开，烟雾和灰尘涌入车厢。”

He said it was "incredibly hot" on the train because the air conditioning stopped working and no doors or windows were open.

他说，由于空调停止工作，且没有门窗打开，车上“非常热”。

South East Coast Ambulance Service said passengers who did not require hospital treatment had been taken to a local community centre.

东南海岸救护车服务表示，无需住院治疗的乘客已被送往当地社区中心。

Outside the centre, Yana Ludwig, who was travelling alone on the train from London to Lewes, said the experience as "completely terrifying".

在中心外，独自乘坐火车从伦敦到刘易斯的亚娜·路德维希说，这次经历“完全可怕”。

"There was a banging. Everything just went topsy turvy," the 56-year-old from the United States said.

这位56岁的美国人说：“有一声巨响。一切都天翻地覆。”

"It seemed like a really long time but it probably was 10 seconds of that before the carriage actually fell on the side."

“感觉过了很长时间，但实际上可能只有10秒钟，车厢就侧翻了。”

She said she was "pretty banged up", adding that everyone in the train was checking on their fellow passengers.

她说自己“伤得不轻”，并补充说车上的每个人都在互相查看情况。

"These moments everybody kind of pulls together," she said.

她说：“在这种时刻，大家都会团结起来。”

Network Rail said it would be "inappropriate" to comment as to what caused the derailment.

英国铁路网公司表示，对脱轨原因发表评论“不合适”。

"I would like to reassure everyone that we are working closely with the Rail Accident Investigation Branch, who are on site to establish exactly what has happened," said Lucy McAuliffe from, Network Rail.

英国铁路网公司的露西·麦考利夫说：“我想向大家保证，我们正在与铁路事故调查处密切合作，他们已到现场查明事故原因。”

The train had 150 people on board, South East Coast Ambulance Service said

东南海岸救护车服务表示，列车上有150人。

BTP assistant chief constable Ian Drummond-Smith said: "We've declared a major incident and a significant emergency service response is ongoing at the scene, but all passengers on the train have now been safely evacuated from the service."

英国交通警察助理警长伊恩·德拉蒙德-史密斯说：“我们已经宣布发生重大事件，现场正在进行大规模的紧急服务响应，但列车上的所有乘客现已安全撤离。”

"This remains a complex and ongoing multi-agency response," a spokesperson said.

一位发言人说：“这仍然是一个复杂且持续的多机构响应行动。”

Fire engines, ambulances and police were stationed on Landport Road, in Lewes, alongside a double decker bus by the community hub waiting for passengers to board.

消防车、救护车和警车停在刘易斯的兰德波特路，旁边还有一辆双层巴士在社区中心等待乘客上车。

East Sussex Fire and Rescue Service said it sent 10 fire engines, a command support unit, technical rescue teams and other resources to the scene.

东萨塞克斯消防救援服务表示，已派出10辆消防车、一个指挥支援单元、技术救援队和其他资源前往现场。

University Hospitals Sussex NHS confirmed it was responding to a major incident.

苏塞克斯大学医院NHS信托确认正在应对这一重大事件。

People were stood on top of the tipped over carriages after the derailment

脱轨后，人们站在倾覆的车厢顶部。

Some sat next to the side of the track after the derailment having escaped the carriage

脱轨后，一些人逃出车厢后坐在轨道旁。

Passenger John May, from St Leonards, said the service had been "packed".

来自圣伦纳兹的乘客约翰·梅表示，这趟车“挤满了人”。

He said: "We were coming up here and suddenly the train just started to shift side-to-side and derailed."

他说：“我们正开过来，突然列车开始左右摇晃并脱轨。”

He was in the fourth carriage from the rear of the train, he said.

他说，他当时在列车后部数起的第四节车厢。

"We were fortunate that we stayed on the embankment. The last three carriages rolled down into the field," he said.

他说：“我们很幸运，留在了路堤上。最后三节车厢滚到了田野里。”

A number of the carriages which derailed tumbled into a field next to the railway

几节脱轨的车厢翻滚到铁路旁的田野中。

He added: "There were a lot of older people and children."

他补充说：“有很多老人和孩子。”

Oriana Evans, who lives nearby, said she and her son helped passengers as they were led off the train towards the community centre.

住在附近的奥莉安娜·埃文斯说，她和儿子帮助乘客下车前往社区中心。

"I walked and chatted to some of them in shock really," the 54-year-old jewellery maker said.

这位54岁的珠宝制造商说：“我走着和他们聊天，他们真的惊魂未定。”

She said they were "really bruised".

她说他们“瘀伤严重”。

Network Rail said the derailment had closed all lines running through the station, with major disruption expected until 22:00.

英国铁路网公司表示，脱轨导致所有经过该站的线路关闭，预计严重中断将持续到22:00。

A spokesperson said: "Trains are currently unable to run through the area to allow the emergency services to attend the incident."

一位发言人说：“目前列车无法通过该地区，以便紧急服务处理事故。”

Carriages came off the tracks and ended up on their sides

车厢脱轨并侧翻。

Rail operator Southern said trains are unable to run through the area and major disruption is expected to continue until further notice. It advised passengers to check before they travel.

铁路运营商南方铁路表示，列车无法通过该地区，预计严重中断将持续到另行通知。它建议乘客出行前查询信息。

Tickets were being accepted on alternative Southern, Thameslink and Gatwick Express services with rail replacement buses arranged for travel to Lewes and to Haywards Heath.

南方铁路、泰晤士连线铁路和盖特威克快线的替代服务接受车票，并安排了铁路替代巴士前往刘易斯和海沃兹希思。

The railway operator advised those travelling to Gatwick to allow "at least an extra 150 minutes for your journey" or use an alternative route.

铁路运营商建议前往盖特威克的乘客“至少为行程预留额外150分钟”或使用替代路线。

Emergency services are at the scene

紧急服务人员已到达现场。

Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander said she was "deeply concerned".

交通大臣海蒂·亚历山大表示“深感关切”。

She wrote on X: "Grateful to emergency services who are on the scene supporting those impacted.

她在X上写道：“感谢在现场支持受影响人员的紧急服务人员。”

"We are working quickly with the rail industry and local partners to assist passengers."

“我们正在与铁路行业和地方合作伙伴迅速合作，为乘客提供帮助。”

The MP for Eastbourne, Josh Babarinde, said he had heard of one constituent who had suffered rib injuries.

伊斯特本议员乔什·巴贝林德表示，他听说有一名选民肋骨受伤。

Lewes MP James MacCleary said it was "clearly a very serious and developing incident", in a post on Facebook.

刘易斯议员詹姆斯·麦克利里在Facebook上发帖称，这“显然是一个非常严重且不断升级的事件”。

He said he was in touch with the relevant authorities and would provide further updates as soon as he had more information.

他说，他已与相关当局联系，一旦有更多信息将尽快提供进一步更新。
