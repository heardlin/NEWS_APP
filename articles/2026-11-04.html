When will we know the US midterm election results?

我们何时能知道美国中期选举结果？

The first polls have closed in the US midterm elections, which will shape the rest of Donald Trump's presidency. The outcome determines which party - Trump's Republicans or opposition Democrats - controls Congress.

美国中期选举的首批投票站已关闭，此次选举将影响唐纳德·特朗普（Donald Trump）总统任期的剩余时间。选举结果将决定哪个政党——特朗普的共和党还是反对党民主党——控制国会。

It is possible the results will not be projected for several hours, days or even weeks. Here's what you need to know.

结果可能需要数小时、数天甚至数周才能预测出来。以下是你需要知道的信息。

Congress is made up of two parts - the lower chamber, the House of Representatives and the upper chamber, the Senate.

国会由两部分组成——下议院，即众议院（House of Representatives），以及上议院，即参议院（Senate）。

To win a majority in the House, a party needs 218 seats. In the Senate, a party needs 51 seats for an outright majority. But if the upper chamber is split 50-50, the vice-president has a tie-breaking vote, which gives their party control of it.

要在众议院赢得多数席位，一个政党需要218个席位。在参议院，一个政党需要51个席位才能获得绝对多数。但如果上议院席位50比50平分，副总统拥有打破平局的一票，这使其政党控制参议院。

The first results to come in won't tell us much about the overall result because these races are in places considered safe bets for Republicans (Kentucky and Indiana) or Democrats (Vermont).

首批结果不会告诉我们太多关于整体结果的信息，因为这些竞选所在的地方被认为是共和党（肯塔基州和印第安纳州）或民主党（佛蒙特州）的安全席位。

Throughout the night, BBC analysts will focus on the competitive races which will give an indication of how well things are going for Trump.

整个晚上，BBC分析人士将关注竞争激烈的竞选，这将表明特朗普的情况进展如何。

There are several tight Senate contests in the Eastern Time zone - like Ohio, where polls close at 19:30 EST (00:30 GMT) and Maine , where they shut at 20:00 EST (01:00 GMT) . These could provide early indications of the electorate's mood.

东部时区有几场激烈的参议院竞选——比如俄亥俄州，投票站于美国东部时间19:30（格林尼治标准时间00:30）关闭，缅因州于美国东部时间20:00（格林尼治标准时间01:00）关闭。这些可能提供选民情绪的早期迹象。

Some key contests might start to be "called" by US media outlets. They use models to project which way a state has voted, even before the full vote count has been completed.

一些关键竞选可能开始被美国媒体“预测”。他们使用模型来预测一个州的投票方向，甚至在完整计票完成之前。

This happens when they believe a party or a candidate has gained a lead that cannot be beaten. But in some closely-fought states, outlets could take a long time to make their projections.

这发生在他们认为一个政党或候选人获得了无法被超越的领先优势时。但在一些竞争激烈的州，媒体可能需要很长时间才能做出预测。

The overall timing of the result will depend on how tight the races are and which races end up being decisive.

结果的整体时间将取决于竞选的激烈程度以及哪些竞选最终具有决定性。

In 2022, Republicans and Democrats were still in a tight race for control of the US Congress on 9 November, a day after the midterms.

2022年，中期选举后一天，即11月9日，共和党和民主党仍在为控制美国国会而激烈竞争。

While Republicans were favourites to win the House of Representatives, the fight for the Senate remained on a knife-edge.

虽然共和党是赢得众议院的热门，但参议院的争夺仍胜负难料。

The full picture took several days. CBS News projected Republicans had secured the 218 seats needed for a majority in the House on 17 November.

完整情况需要几天时间。CBS新闻于11月17日预测共和党已获得众议院多数所需的218个席位。

Counting in a crucial Senate race in Nevada took four days . The Democrats' narrow victory in the state brought them to 50 seats, allowing them to retain control of the Senate because then Vice-President Kamala Harris held a tie-breaking vote.

内华达州一场关键参议院竞选的计票花了四天时间。民主党在该州的微弱胜利使他们达到50个席位，从而能够保留对参议院的控制，因为当时的副总统卡玛拉·哈里斯（Kamala Harris）拥有打破平局的一票。

Georgia's Senate contest had to be settled by a run-off election because no candidate passed the 50% mark in November.

佐治亚州的参议院竞选不得不通过决选来解决，因为11月没有候选人超过50%的门槛。

The results were not available until 7 December, when Democrats won this seat .

结果直到12月7日才公布，当时民主党赢得了这个席位。

曾是一名当地新闻记者的阿什利·辛森（Ashley Hinson）在爱荷华州的一场关键竞选中对阵前篮球运动员乔什·图雷克（Josh Turek）

There are six Senate races deemed to be a toss-up by independent elections analyst Cook Political Report , external - Alaska , Kansas , Iowa , Maine , Ohio and Texas .

独立选举分析师库克政治报告（Cook Political Report）认为有六场参议院竞选难分胜负——阿拉斯加州、堪萨斯州、爱荷华州、缅因州、俄亥俄州和德克萨斯州。

Of these, six are currently held by Republicans. The Democrats would need to win three of those seats, plus five classed as likely or leaning Democratic, to hit 51 and take control of the Senate.

其中，六个席位目前由共和党持有。民主党需要赢得其中三个席位，再加上五个被归类为可能或倾向民主党的席位，才能达到51席并控制参议院。

Polls have suggested Democrats have a chance in Texas where former teacher James Talarico could become the first Democrat to win a Senate seat in the state since 1988 if he defeats Ken Paxton, the current state attorney general and close Trump ally.

民调显示民主党在德克萨斯州有机会，如果前教师詹姆斯·塔拉里科（James Talarico）击败现任州司法部长、特朗普的亲密盟友肯·帕克斯顿（Ken Paxton），他可能成为自1988年以来该州首位赢得参议院席位的民主党人。

Democrats also need to keep hold of their seat in Michigan , where Abdul El-Sayed, a left-wing political outsider, is up against Republican rival Mike Rogers, a Trump ally and former FBI agent in a closely-observed race. The state was initially classified as a "toss-up" by Cook Political Report, but was reclassified as "leans Democrat" the day before the vote.

民主党还需要保住密歇根州的席位，左翼政治局外人阿卜杜勒·埃尔-赛义德（Abdul El-Sayed）在那里与共和党对手、特朗普盟友、前FBI探员迈克·罗杰斯（Mike Rogers）展开一场备受关注的竞选。该州最初被库克政治报告归类为“难分胜负”，但在投票前一天被重新归类为“倾向民主党”。

The current Republican majority in the House, the lower chamber, is thin. They have 218 representatives, plus one independent who votes with them.

目前共和党在下议院众议院的多数优势微弱。他们有218名代表，加上一名与他们投票的独立人士。

Some of the most closely-watched races in the House are in east-coast states, according to the Cook Political Report. These include New York's 17th District, Pennsylvania's 8th District and Virginia's 1st District.

根据库克政治报告，众议院一些最受关注的竞选在东海岸州。这些包括纽约州第17选区、宾夕法尼亚州第8选区和弗吉尼亚州第1选区。

The results of a voter poll will be released at 17:00 EST (22:00 GMT) , which will give us an indication of the issues at the forefront of voters' minds.

一项选民民意调查的结果将于美国东部时间17:00（格林尼治标准时间22:00）公布，这将让我们了解选民最关心的问题。

Then, at 18:00 EST (23:00 GMT) , the first polls close in Kentucky and Indiana. After that, all eyes will be focused on the key battleground states:

然后，在美国东部时间18:00（格林尼治标准时间23:00），肯塔基州和印第安纳州的首批投票站关闭。之后，所有目光将集中在关键摇摆州：

19:30 EST (00:30 GMT) - Polls close in Ohio , where Republican Senator Jon Husted finds himself neck-and-neck with Democrat Sherrod Brown. Results from this state could give us a clue as to how the night might unfold

美国东部时间19:30（格林尼治标准时间00:30）——俄亥俄州投票站关闭，共和党参议员乔恩·赫斯特德（Jon Husted）与民主党人谢罗德·布朗（Sherrod Brown）势均力敌。该州的结果可能为我们提供今晚局势如何展开的线索。

20:00 EST (01:00 GMT) - Voting ends in the tight Senate race in Maine between Republican Senator Susan Collins and Democrat Troy Jackson and in most of Kansas and Texas

美国东部时间20:00（格林尼治标准时间01:00）——缅因州共和党参议员苏珊·柯林斯（Susan Collins）与民主党人特洛伊·杰克逊（Troy Jackson）之间激烈的参议院竞选投票结束，堪萨斯州和德克萨斯州大部分地区也结束投票。

21:00 EST (02:00 GMT) - Polls close in Iowa , as well as in Kansas and Texas . In the hours that follow, a picture might start to emerge of whether things are going to plan for the Republicans - or the Democrats

美国东部时间21:00（格林尼治标准时间02:00）——爱荷华州以及堪萨斯州和德克萨斯州的投票站关闭。在接下来的几个小时里，可能开始显现共和党或民主党的事情是否按计划进行。

01:00 EST (06:00 GMT) - Voting ends in Alaska , the last battleground state. A close Senate race between Democrat Mary Peltola and incumbent Republican Senator Dan Sullivan has been making headlines over the inclusion of a second candidate named Dan Sullivan on the ballot

美国东部时间01:00（格林尼治标准时间06:00）——阿拉斯加州最后一个摇摆州投票结束。民主党人玛丽·佩尔托拉（Mary Peltola）与现任共和党参议员丹·沙利文（Dan Sullivan）之间激烈的参议院竞选因选票上出现第二位名叫丹·沙利文的候选人而成为头条新闻。

Each state administers its own elections and has its own rules for counting and processing votes.

每个州管理自己的选举，并有自己的计票和处理规则。

Some states, like Florida, process early and mail ballots prior to election day, while others only begin tabulating on 3 November.

一些州，如佛罗里达州，在选举日之前处理提前投票和邮寄选票，而其他州只在11月3日开始计票。

Local election officials verify, process and count individual votes, in a process known as canvassing.

当地选举官员核实、处理并计算个人选票，这一过程称为 canvassing。

Most states require a post-election audit that involves a manual count of ballots checked against machine totals, although rules vary across the country. Full hand counts are generally reserved for recounts.

大多数州要求进行选举后审计，包括手动计票并与机器总数核对，尽管全国规则各异。完全手工计票通常保留给重新计票。

Every state and locality has rules which specify who can participate in the canvass, the order in which votes are processed and which parts are open to the public, including how partisan observers can watch the count and, in some states, raise formal challenges.

每个州和地方都有规则规定谁可以参与计票、处理选票的顺序以及哪些部分对公众开放，包括党派观察员如何观看计票，并在一些州提出正式质疑。

Tight races will take longer, and create the possibility of recounts being triggered and legal challenges.

激烈的竞选将需要更长时间，并可能引发重新计票和法律挑战。

Mail-in ballots take longer to count, as officials need to process envelopes and verify signatures.

邮寄选票需要更长时间计算，因为官员需要处理信封并验证签名。

More than 30 states allow ballots to be processed before election day, but other states do not. In California, all ballots must be posted before or on election day and will still be counted if they arrive within seven days of the election.

超过30个州允许在选举日之前处理选票，但其他州不允许。在加利福尼亚州，所有选票必须在选举日或之前寄出，如果在选举后七天内到达，仍将被计算。

In Alaska, ranked-choice voting could mean that tabulations take considerably longer than elsewhere in the country and the controversy over ballots could mean that legal challenges can complicate things.

在阿拉斯加州，排名选择投票可能意味着计票时间比全国其他地方长得多，而选票争议可能意味着法律挑战使事情复杂化。

Once every vote has been included in the final results, and after processes such as recounts have been completed, the election results are certified - first at the local, county, level and then state-wide.

一旦每张选票都被纳入最终结果，并且在重新计票等过程完成后，选举结果得到认证——首先在地方、县一级，然后全州范围。

The governor then typically signs the certifications for House and Senate winners to be sent to Congress.

然后州长通常签署众议院和参议院获胜者的认证，发送给国会。

Results can be challenged. Recounts can be requested by candidates in close races, and in some states recounts are automatically triggered if they fall within a certain margin.

结果可能受到挑战。在势均力敌的竞选中，候选人可以要求重新计票，在一些州，如果差距在一定范围内，重新计票会自动触发。

Candidates can also file legal election contests alleging irregularities.

候选人还可以提起法律选举诉讼，指控违规行为。

Congress has the final word. Losing candidates in both House and Senate races can file contests, with slightly different processes in each of the two chambers.

国会拥有最终决定权。众议院和参议院竞选的落选候选人可以提出质疑，两个议院的程序略有不同。

Both chambers can, theoretically, refuse to seat someone or order an investigation.

理论上，两个议院都可以拒绝让某人就职或下令调查。

Outgoing members of Congress leave office on Sunday 3 January. Because that date falls on a weekend, the new members may take office on the same date unless Congress passes a law setting a different day. , external

即将离任的国会议员于1月3日星期日离职。由于该日期是周末，新议员可能在同一日期就职，除非国会通过法律设定不同的日期。
