Kane ends England’s 25-year wait to win men’s Ballon d’Or award

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Harry Kane broke Bayern Munich's goalscoring record for a season in his third campaign at the club

哈里·凯恩（Harry Kane）在拜仁慕尼黑（Bayern Munich）的第三个赛季打破了俱乐部单赛季进球纪录。

Harry Kane has become the first English player to win the Ballon d'Or since Michael Owen in 2001 after being crowned the world's best player at a ceremony in London.

哈里·凯恩成为自2001年迈克尔·欧文（Michael Owen）以来首位赢得金球奖（Ballon d'Or）的英格兰球员，他在伦敦举行的颁奖典礼上被评为世界最佳球员。

The 33-year-old Bayern Munich striker is the fifth Englishman to win in total, with Stanley Matthews (1956), Bobby Charlton (1966) and Kevin Keegan (1978 and 1979) the others.

这位33岁的拜仁慕尼黑前锋是第五位获此殊荣的英格兰人，其他四位分别是斯坦利·马修斯（Stanley Matthews，1956年）、博比·查尔顿（Bobby Charlton，1966年）和凯文·基冈（Kevin Keegan，1978年和1979年）。

Denis Law of Scotland (1964) and Northern Ireland's George Best (1968) are the other winners from the United Kingdom.

苏格兰的丹尼斯·劳（Denis Law，1964年）和北爱尔兰的乔治·贝斯特（George Best，1968年）是另外两位来自英国的获奖者。

Real Madrid and France forward Kylian Mbappe finished second, while Barcelona and Spain attacker Lamine Yamal completed the top three.

皇家马德里（Real Madrid）和法国前锋基利安·姆巴佩（Kylian Mbappe）排名第二，巴塞罗那（Barcelona）和西班牙攻击手拉明·亚马尔（Lamine Yamal）位列第三。

"Not many people would have thought it, and maybe even I wouldn't have thought I'd be this far," Kane told Dazn.

“没有多少人会想到，甚至我自己也没想到我能走到这一步，”凯恩告诉DAZN。

"I had to go through many ups and downs, especially early in my career. I was never the person that people thought was going to be the superstar like some of the guys now.

“我经历了许多起起落落，尤其是在职业生涯早期。我从来不是人们认为会成为超级巨星的那种人，不像现在的某些球员。”

"I had to bide my time and work hard, a lot of times it was me against my own mind and I had to come through it, I had to show self-belief, determination.

“我必须等待时机并努力工作，很多时候我是在与自己的心态作斗争，我必须克服它，我必须展现自信和决心。”

"I think that grounded me even when I did make it at the highest level to keep improving and keep getting better."

“我认为这让我脚踏实地，即使在我达到最高水平时也保持谦逊，不断进步，变得更好。”

England manager Thomas Tuchel said he was "delighted" to see Kane win the award as he hailed the forward's success as a "huge honour".

英格兰队主教练托马斯·图赫尔（Thomas Tuchel）表示，看到凯恩获奖他“非常高兴”，并称赞这位前锋的成功是“巨大的荣誉”。

Prince William, meanwhile, said the former Tottenham Hotspur player was a "brilliant captain" and "remarkable player".

与此同时，威廉王子（Prince William）表示，这位前托特纳姆热刺（Tottenham Hotspur）球员是一位“出色的队长”和“非凡的球员”。

Kane scored 61 goals in 51 club matches last season, the most a Bayern player has ever struck in one campaign. Including England matches, it was 73 goals - plus eight assists - in 65 matches.

凯恩上赛季在俱乐部51场比赛中打入61球，是拜仁球员单赛季进球最多的。包括英格兰队的比赛，他在65场比赛中打入73球，另有8次助攻。

Only one player at a club in Europe's top five leagues has ever scored more goals in a season.

在欧洲五大联赛俱乐部中，只有一名球员单赛季进球数超过他。

But he wins it despite not lifting either of football's biggest prizes last season, having lost in both the World Cup and Champions League semi-finals.

但尽管上赛季未能赢得足球界两大最高奖项——世界杯和欧冠半决赛均告失利，他依然获奖。

Remarkably the Three Lions captain becomes the first Bayern player to win the Ballon d'Or since Karl-Heinz Rummenigge in 1981.

值得注意的是，这位三狮军团队长成为自1981年卡尔-海因茨·鲁梅尼格（Karl-Heinz Rummenigge）以来首位获得金球奖的拜仁球员。

To celebrate Kane's success, the Wembley arches were lit up in gold on Monday night.

为庆祝凯恩的成功，周一晚上温布利球场的拱门亮起了金色灯光。

BBC Sport looks at why Kane won it and how unusual it is to do so without securing one of those major trophies.

BBC体育探讨了凯恩为何获奖，以及在没有赢得重大奖杯的情况下获奖有多么不寻常。

'Anything is possible' - Kane wins Ballon d'Or

“一切皆有可能”——凯恩赢得金球奖

In terms of trophies, Kane may have 'only' lifted the German double last season.

在奖杯方面，凯恩上赛季可能“仅仅”赢得了德国双冠王。

But that more than doubled his career trophy total - having also won the Bundesliga the season before.

但这使他的职业生涯奖杯总数翻了一倍多——此前一个赛季他还赢得了德甲冠军。

But in terms of individual output Kane - whose previous best Ballon d'Or finish was 10th - was unmatched last season.

但就个人表现而言，凯恩上赛季无人能及——他此前金球奖最高排名是第10。

His 61 goals in all competitions for Bayern was 19 more than any other player in Europe's top five leagues managed.

他为拜仁在各项赛事中打入61球，比欧洲五大联赛中任何其他球员多出19球。

In fact it was the most anyone has recorded since Cristiano Ronaldo also notched 61 for Real Madrid 11 years ago.

事实上，这是自11年前克里斯蒂亚诺·罗纳尔多（Cristiano Ronaldo）为皇家马德里打入61球以来的最高纪录。

He was the Bundesliga's top scorer with 36 goals - 17 clear of anyone else. In the Champions League he scored 14 for the beaten semi-finalists.

他以36球成为德甲最佳射手，领先第二名17球。在欧冠中，他为止步半决赛的拜仁打入14球。

In the German Cup he scored 10 goals, including a hat-trick in the 3-0 win over Stuttgart in the final, and he also netted once in the German Super Cup.

在德国杯中，他打入10球，包括在决赛3-0战胜斯图加特（Stuttgart）的比赛中上演帽子戏法，他还在德国超级杯中打入一球。

No player has ever scored so many goals in a season for Bayern, a club who have had legendary goalscorers like Gerd Muller, Rummenigge and more recently Robert Lewandowski in their ranks.

没有球员曾为拜仁单赛季打入如此多的进球，这家俱乐部曾拥有盖德·穆勒（Gerd Muller）、鲁梅尼格以及最近的罗伯特·莱万多夫斯基（Robert Lewandowski）等传奇射手。

Kane's 12 goals for England last season - including six at the World Cup - take him up to a total of 73.

凯恩上赛季为英格兰打入12球——包括世界杯上的6球——使他的总进球数达到73球。

Only one player in history from Europe's top leagues has scored more in a season, Lionel Messi's 82 for Barcelona and Argentina in 2011-12.

历史上，欧洲顶级联赛中只有一名球员单赛季进球更多，即莱昂内尔·梅西（Lionel Messi）在2011-12赛季为巴塞罗那和阿根廷打入82球。

Next on the list are Muller (72 in 1972-73), Cristiano Ronaldo (66 in both 2011-12 and 2014-15), former Brazil striker Ronaldo (66 in 1996-97), Erling Haaland (64 in 2022-23) and Luis Suarez (64 in 2015-16).

接下来是穆勒（1972-73赛季72球）、克里斯蒂亚诺·罗纳尔多（2011-12和2014-15赛季均66球）、前巴西前锋罗纳尔多（Ronaldo，1996-97赛季66球）、埃尔林·哈兰德（Erling Haaland，2022-23赛季64球）和路易斯·苏亚雷斯（Luis Suarez，2015-16赛季64球）。

Former England striker Alan Shearer recently told BBC Sport: "The winner of the Ballon d'Or should be Harry Kane.

前英格兰前锋阿兰·希勒（Alan Shearer）最近告诉BBC体育：“金球奖得主应该是哈里·凯恩。”

"His numbers for the last 12 to 18 months have been absolutely sensational for Bayern and for England. For that reason, he should get it."

“他在过去12到18个月里为拜仁和英格兰的数据绝对惊人。因此，他应该获奖。”

Another ex-Three Lions player Danny Murphy added: "His goalscoring has been relentless, brilliant. He's had one of the best seasons of his career and it'll be a great reward for him if he gets the trophy.

另一位前三狮军团球员丹尼·墨菲（Danny Murphy）补充道：“他的进球势头不可阻挡，非常出色。他经历了职业生涯最好的赛季之一，如果他获奖，将是对他的巨大回报。”

"He's been brilliant at Bayern Munich since he's been there, adapted really well and he's been the best centre forward in the world for the last 12 months."

“自从加盟拜仁慕尼黑以来，他一直表现出色，适应得非常好，他是过去12个月世界上最好的中锋。”

The Ballon d'Or was only available to European players from its formation in 1956 until 1995 when it was opened up to everyone.

金球奖从1956年创立到1995年只授予欧洲球员，1995年后才对所有球员开放。

Since then in four of the seven World Cup years, the Ballon d'Or has been awarded to a world champion - including Messi in 2023 (as the 2022 World Cup was in the winter).

此后在七个世界杯年份中的四个，金球奖都授予了世界冠军——包括2023年的梅西（因为2022年世界杯在冬季举行）。

However, that was the first time since 2006 it had happened, with Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo and Luka Modric all winning since then despite not winning the World Cup that year.

然而，这是自2006年以来首次出现这种情况，此后梅西、克里斯蒂亚诺·罗纳尔多和卢卡·莫德里奇（Luka Modric）都在未赢得当年世界杯的情况下获奖。

This is only the fifth time in the past 20 years that the Ballon d'Or has not gone to a player who has won either the World Cup, the Champions League or a European Championship/Copa America - with Kane joining Messi (three times) and Ronaldo.

这是过去20年中仅第五次金球奖未授予赢得世界杯、欧冠或欧洲杯/美洲杯的球员——凯恩加入了梅西（三次）和罗纳尔多的行列。

Lamine Yamal, who won the World Cup with Spain, had been one of the favourites but lost out to Kane.

与西班牙一起赢得世界杯的拉明·亚马尔曾是热门之一，但输给了凯恩。

Last year, winning the Champions League helped PSG and France's Ousmane Dembele to the crown.

去年，赢得欧冠帮助巴黎圣日耳曼（PSG）和法国的奥斯曼·登贝莱（Ousmane Dembele）夺冠。

Kane may have just won the Ballon d'Or but some are already wondering if he is in a good place to retain it next year.

凯恩可能刚刚赢得金球奖，但一些人已经在猜测他是否处于明年卫冕的有利位置。

The striker has scored 10 goals in his opening 12 games for Bayern this season.

这位前锋本赛季为拜仁出战前12场比赛打入10球。

Throw in six goals in four Nations League games for England and that is a total of 16 in 16 matches so far.

加上为英格兰在四场国家联赛中打入的6球，他迄今为止在16场比赛中总共打入16球。

Bayern are unbeaten in the Bundesliga and Champions League this season.

拜仁本赛季在德甲和欧冠中保持不败。

If they were to win a Treble - and Kane keeps scoring at about a goal a game - it would be tough to look past him, especially in a season with no World Cup, European Championship or Copa America.

如果他们赢得三冠王——并且凯恩保持场均一球的进球率——很难不看好他，尤其是在没有世界杯、欧洲杯或美洲杯的赛季。

The last player other than Messi or Ronaldo to win it two years in a row was Marco van Basten in 1988 and 1989.

除梅西或罗纳尔多外，上一位连续两年获奖的球员是1988年和1989年的马尔科·范巴斯滕（Marco van Basten）。

However, Lamine Yamal will be hoping to make it third time lucky after finishing second twice in two years.

然而，拉明·亚马尔希望第三次幸运，他在两年内两次获得第二名。

He has started the season in electric form, with 10 goals and 10 assists in 13 games for Barcelona in La Liga and the Champions League.

他本赛季开局状态火热，为巴塞罗那在西甲和欧冠的13场比赛中打入10球并助攻10次。

And Barca are currently favourites to win the Champions League, which could ultimately end up being a deciding factor.

巴塞罗那目前是赢得欧冠的热门，这可能最终成为决定性因素。

He would be the youngest player to win the Ballon d'Or - aged 20 - if he does lift it next year.

如果他明年获奖，他将成为最年轻的金球奖得主——20岁。

Real Madrid's Kylian Mbappe will also fancy his chances of winning for the first time, with 14 goals in 13 appearances for club and country.

皇家马德里的基利安·姆巴佩也会期待首次获奖，他为俱乐部和国家队出场13次打入14球。

The youngest winner remains Brazilian Ronaldo, who was 21 when he won in 1997.

最年轻的获奖者仍是巴西的罗纳尔多，他在1997年获奖时21岁。

Arsenal, who have never had a Ballon d'Or winner, and Paris St-Germain are two of the other frontrunners to win the Champions League.

从未有过金球奖得主的阿森纳（Arsenal）和巴黎圣日耳曼是另外两支欧冠夺冠热门。
