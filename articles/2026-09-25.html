US President Donald Trump has been talking for months about Chinese President Xi Jinping's visit to Washington DC. He's spruced up the White House, lamented that his new ballroom won't be finished in time and ensured a new marble helipad is ready to show off to the Chinese leader.

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普(Donald Trump)数月来一直在谈论中国国家主席习近平访问华盛顿特区的事宜。他精心装饰了白宫，哀叹他的新宴会厅无法及时完工，并确保了一个新的大理石直升机停机坪准备就绪，以便向中国领导人展示。

Trump gave Xi a warm welcome on his arrival at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland, complete with a military flyover and a 100ft (30m) red carpet.

特朗普在习近平抵达马里兰州的安德鲁斯联合基地时给予了热烈欢迎，包括军用飞机飞越和100英尺（30米）长的红地毯。
