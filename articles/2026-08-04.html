Ukrainian vegetable seller targeted in 'human safari' drone attack

乌克兰菜贩遭“人类狩猎”无人机袭击

Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky has shared a video on social media of a drone hunting down a man in southern Ukraine, saying it showed an instance of a Russian "safari" attack against civilians.

乌克兰总统泽连斯基（Volodymyr Zelensky）在社交媒体上分享了一段视频，显示一架无人机在乌克兰南部追捕一名男子，称这展示了俄罗斯对平民发动“狩猎”式袭击的一个实例。

The video shows the drone pursuing a street vendor on a city street in the Kherson region. The man moves away from the drone hovering above, before it chases him as he runs around a white van. Eventually, the drone swoops down on him and explodes on impact.

视频显示，在赫尔松地区的一条城市街道上，无人机追逐一名街头小贩。该男子躲避着悬停在上方的无人机，随后无人机在他绕着一辆白色货车奔跑时追赶他。最终，无人机俯冲向他并撞击爆炸。

The man escaped with multiple shrapnel wounds, concussion and trauma, a doctor said.

一名医生表示，该男子多处弹片伤、脑震荡和外伤，但幸免于难。

Sometimes called "human safari", the deployment of remotely-operated drones against civilians has often been used by Russians to terrify residents of front line areas in Ukraine.

这种被称为“人类狩猎”的战术，即使用远程操控无人机袭击平民，常被俄罗斯用来恐吓乌克兰前线地区的居民。

"The world must see this," Zelensky wrote on Telegram. "It must see every piece of evidence that Russia has gone mad, and every fact that Russian soldiers are enjoying killings and tormenting people."

泽连斯基在Telegram上写道：“世界必须看到这一点。它必须看到俄罗斯已经疯狂的每一份证据，以及俄罗斯士兵以杀戮和折磨人民为乐的每一个事实。”

The video showed a "barbaric war crime", said Ukraine's Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha.

乌克兰外长安德里·西比哈（Andrii Sybiha）表示，该视频展示了一场“野蛮的战争罪行”。

The safaris were a "deliberate and systemic Russian strategy," Sybiha said. "The sadist operating this drone knows full well that he is targeting a civilian."

西比哈称，这些“狩猎”是“俄罗斯蓄意且系统性的战略”。他说：“操作这架无人机的虐待狂完全清楚他正在瞄准一名平民。”

He added the attack was part of a deliberate Russian campaign to intimidate civilians and demanded international condemnation.

他补充说，这次袭击是俄罗斯蓄意恐吓平民行动的一部分，并呼吁国际社会予以谴责。

The Kherson regional governor later posted a video showing the man - identified as Yuriy, a 52-year-old street vegetable vendor - speaking from a hospital bed following the drone attack.

赫尔松地区州长随后发布了一段视频，显示该男子——被确认为尤里（Yuriy），一名52岁的街头蔬菜小贩——在无人机袭击后于医院病床上讲话。

Yuriy said he and his wife had started to lay out crates of vegetables next to his van when he heard a distinctive buzzing sound overhead.

尤里说，他和妻子刚开始在货车旁摆放蔬菜箱，就听到头顶传来明显的嗡嗡声。

When his wife ran away, the drone turned to him instead. He held up some heads of garlic towards the drone's camera to show the operator that he was not trying to film it.

当他的妻子跑开时，无人机转而瞄准了他。他举起几头大蒜对着无人机的摄像头，以向操作员表明他并非试图拍摄它。

"It could see that I had tomatoes out, some cucumbers and eggplants laid out on the floor," Yuriy said. "It even ducked under the umbrella when I was running away round the other side."

尤里说：“它能看到我摆出了西红柿、一些黄瓜和茄子在地上。当我绕到另一边跑时，它甚至钻到了伞下。”

BBC Verify geolocated the video to a market space beside a roundabout in the Tavriiskyi district in northern Kherson. It is not clear who filmed the attack, or how - but the earliest public copy BBC Verify found was shared by a pro-Ukrainian Instagram account on Tuesday morning.

BBC Verify（英国广播公司核实部）将该视频定位到赫尔松北部塔夫里斯基区一个环岛旁的集市空间。目前尚不清楚是谁拍摄了这次袭击，以及如何拍摄的——但BBC Verify发现的最早公开副本是周二上午由一个亲乌克兰的Instagram账号分享的。

"Safari" attacks have long been carried out by Russian troops in Kherson, which sits only a few miles from the front line.

长期以来，俄罗斯军队一直在赫尔松实施“狩猎”袭击，该地距离前线仅几英里。

A 2025 UN report said it had found Russian armed forces "recurrently killed and injured civilians" in the area by using drones to target them.

2025年联合国的一份报告称，俄罗斯武装部队在该地区“经常使用无人机瞄准并杀害和伤害平民”。

The videos filmed by the drones before they explode on to their targets are often posted online, allowing thousands to witness the last moments of the terrified victims.

无人机在撞击目标前拍摄的视频经常被发布到网上，使成千上万的人目睹惊恐受害者的最后时刻。

"Posting videos of civilians being killed and injured amounts to the war crime of outrages upon personal dignity," the UN said.

联合国表示：“发布平民被杀和受伤的视频相当于犯下侵犯个人尊严的战争罪。”

Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022 and denies targeting civilians.

俄罗斯于2022年2月对乌克兰发动全面入侵，并否认以平民为目标。

Additional reporting by Richard Irvine-Brown, BBC Verify

理查德·欧文-布朗（Richard Irvine-Brown），BBC Verify，补充报道
