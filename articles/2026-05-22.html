Attorney general to review teen boys' rape sentences

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

The government is to review the sentencing of three teenage boys who raped two girls in separate attacks, after criticism their sentences were too lenient.

政府将审查三名青少年男孩分别强奸两名女孩的量刑，此前有批评称量刑过于宽大。

The boys, two aged 15 and one aged 14, were not given custodial sentences for the attacks in Fordingbridge, Hampshire, in 2024 and 2025.

这些男孩（两名15岁，一名14岁）因2024年和2025年在汉普郡福德林布里奇的袭击事件未被判处监禁。

They "brazenly filmed" the rapes on their phones and later shared some of the footage online. The teenagers were given youth rehabilitation orders (YRO) and walked out of court with 11 rape convictions between them.

他们“厚颜无耻地”用手机拍摄了强奸过程，随后在网上分享了部分视频。这些青少年被判处青少年改造令（YRO），并带着11项强奸罪名走出法庭。

A government spokesperson said the attorney general's office had received "multiple" requests for the sentences to be reviewed under the Unduly Lenient Sentence (ULS) scheme.

一位政府发言人表示，总检察长办公室已收到“多份”请求，要求根据“不当宽大量刑”（ULS）计划审查这些判决。

It said it shared the public's shock at the details of this "horrific case" and its thoughts were with the young victims - and that its law officers, the attorney general and the solicitor general, were "urgently reviewing the case with the utmost care and attention".

发言人称，公众对此“骇人听闻案件”的细节感到震惊，政府感同身受，并心系年轻受害者——其法律官员，即总检察长和副检察长，正在“以最大谨慎和关注紧急审查此案”。

They will now have up to 28 days to make a decision on the case and could then refer it to the Court of Appeal for a hearing.

他们现在将有最多28天时间对此案做出决定，之后可将案件提交上诉法院进行听证。

Warning: This story contains details some may find distressing

警告：本报道包含可能令人不安的细节。

Earlier, the former Home Office minister Jess Phillips condemned the sentences as "unduly lenient".

此前，前内政部大臣杰斯·菲利普斯谴责这些判决“不当宽大”。

Phillips, who served as minister for safeguarding and violence against women and girls until her resignation earlier this month , said: "For those young women going through a rape trial like this will not have been a simple thing to do, it will have been many, many months if not years to achieve any sort of justice and I am afraid to say it sends a bad message."

菲利普斯在本月早些时候辞职前曾担任保护妇女和女孩免受暴力事务大臣，她说：“对于这些年轻女性来说，经历这样的强奸审判绝非易事，要获得任何形式的正义可能需要数月甚至数年，恐怕这传递了一个糟糕的信号。”

Speaking on the BBC Radio 4's Today programme, the Labour MP suggested social media had negatively influenced young boys.

这位工党议员在BBC广播4台的《今日》节目中表示，社交媒体对年轻男孩产生了负面影响。

"These young people it seems were essentially raping for content in order to put it on social media and share it to their friends gloating about raping these poor young women," she said.

她说：“这些年轻人似乎基本上是为了内容而强奸，以便上传到社交媒体并与朋友分享，炫耀他们强奸了这些可怜的年轻女性。”

"It seems unduly lenient to me and has wider public interest beyond just the case itself in the message that it sends."

“在我看来这似乎不当宽大，并且除了案件本身之外，它传递的信号也具有更广泛的公共利益。”

Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch referred to the sentences as "a disgrace" and shadow Justice Minister Dr Kieran Mullan said: "It cannot be right that teenage boys can commit brutal crimes of rape like this and avoid prison entirely. We have appealed these sentences to the attorney general."

保守党领袖凯米·巴德诺赫称这些判决是“耻辱”，影子司法大臣基兰·穆兰博士表示：“青少年男孩犯下如此残忍的强奸罪行却完全避免监禁，这是不合理的。我们已向总检察长上诉这些判决。”

Anyone who thinks that a sentence for a serious offence is too short can ask for it to be reviewed under the ULS scheme.

任何认为严重罪行判决过短的人都可以请求根据ULS计划进行审查。

The boys, who cannot be named because they are children, had denied the charges but were found guilty in March after a trial at Southampton Crown Court.

这些男孩因未成年而不能透露姓名，他们曾否认指控，但在南安普顿刑事法院审判后于3月被判有罪。

Explaining his sentencing decision on Thursday, Judge Nicholas Rowland said he would avoid "criminalising" the "very young" boys.

周四在解释其量刑决定时，法官尼古拉斯·罗兰表示，他会避免“刑事化”这些“非常年轻”的男孩。

The judge stressed the "seriousness" of the crimes and said the filming of the assaults made them even "more serious".

法官强调了犯罪的“严重性”，并称拍摄袭击行为使其“更加严重”。

The first girl was 15 when she was raped three times in an underpass by the River Avon in Fordingbridge.

第一名女孩在福德林布里奇埃文河下的地下通道中被强奸三次时年仅15岁。

She had travelled to meet one of the boys for the first time after he had begun a "relationship" with her on social media platform Snapchat - but then two other boys appeared.

她是在一名男孩通过社交媒体平台Snapchat与她建立“关系”后首次赴约见面，但随后出现了另外两名男孩。

The second girl was 14 when she met the boys at Fordingbridge Recreation Ground and was raped repeatedly in a nearby field.

第二名女孩在福德林布里奇娱乐场与男孩们见面时年仅14岁，并在附近田野中被多次强奸。

Video footage previously seen in court showed her lying motionless on the ground with "her face buried in her hands", while another boy was heard shouting words of encouragement.

此前在法庭上播放的视频显示，她一动不动地躺在地上，“脸埋在手里”，而另一名男孩则听到喊出鼓励的话语。

Prosecutor Jodie Mittel KC said videos of the first incident were shared online leading to people to make jokes about the girl. She also received messages calling her a "slag".

检察官乔迪·米特尔KC表示，第一起事件的视频被分享到网上，导致人们拿女孩开玩笑。她还收到称她为“荡妇”的信息。

Speaking in court on Thursday, screened from the view of the boys, she read a poem she had written which included the line: "All I want to do is die, I no longer have fear for when that comes."

周四在法庭上，她隔着屏幕避开男孩们的视线，朗读了自己写的一首诗，其中包含这样的句子：“我只想死，当死亡来临时我不再恐惧。”

In a statement read on behalf of the second victim, she described suffering nightmares and struggling to sleep, saying: "I feel ashamed, insecure and uncomfortable in my own body."

在代表第二名受害者宣读的声明中，她描述了自己做噩梦和难以入睡的痛苦，说道：“我在自己身体里感到羞耻、不安全和不舒服。”

Phillips said that while rehabilitation of offenders was "vital", perpetrators should be able to be rehabilitated "within our youth estate".

菲利普斯表示，虽然罪犯改造“至关重要”，但犯罪者应该能够在“我们的青少年管教机构内”得到改造。

The MP for Birmingham Yardley accused social media of playing a role in the rise of misogyny amongst young men.

这位伯明翰亚德利选区的议员指责社交媒体在年轻男性中厌女情绪上升中扮演了角色。

She said: "The truth is for about 10 years we have allowed young people, especially young boys, to be experimented on by social media companies."

她说：“事实是，大约十年来，我们允许年轻人，尤其是年轻男孩，被社交媒体公司当作实验品。”

"Very little" had been done in the last decade to see what effect violent pornography was having on young people and the victims in this case had "paid the price", she said.

她说，过去十年中“很少”有人关注暴力色情内容对年轻人的影响，而本案中的受害者“付出了代价”。

Under the unduly lenient sentence scheme , a member of the public can go to a government webpage and ask the attorney general to look at the outcome.

根据不当宽大量刑计划，公众可以访问政府网页，请求总检察长审查判决结果。

If the attorney general and expert lawyers agree that the sentence is out of line with normal expectations for such a crime, they send the case to the Court of Appeal.

如果总检察长和专家律师同意该判决不符合此类犯罪的正常预期，他们会将案件提交上诉法院。

There, three of the most senior judges in England and Wales will hear arguments about whether the sentence was too short or appropriate, taking into account detailed guidelines to trial judges and the specific circumstances of the case.

在那里，英格兰和威尔士最资深的三名法官将听取关于判决是否过短或适当的辩论，同时考虑对初审法官的详细指导以及案件的具体情况。

Phillips, meanwhile, has invited the girls' families to contact her if they want help in challenging the sentences.

与此同时，菲利普斯已邀请女孩的家属联系她，如果他们需要帮助挑战判决。

Det Sgt Naomi Stocker from Hampshire Police said on Thursday that the force was "liaising with our partners at the Crown Prosecution Service in relation to the sentence passed".

汉普郡警方的探长娜奥米·斯托克周四表示，警方“正在与皇家检察署的合作伙伴就所判处的刑罚进行联络”。
