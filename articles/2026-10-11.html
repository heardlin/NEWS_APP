Twelve killed in attack on airport in Saudi capital Riyadh

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

At least 12 people have been killed and 309 injured in an attack on an airport in the Saudi capital Riyadh, local authorities say, days after Yemen's Houthis claimed similar strikes.

沙特当局称，沙特首都利雅得机场遭到袭击，造成至少12人死亡、309人受伤。几天前，也门胡塞武装声称发动了类似袭击。

The deaths included four Saudis, two people from Bangladesh and one person each from the US, Jordan, Palestine, Syria, Sudan and Egypt, the Saudi civil aviation authority said. Operations at King Khalid International Airport have been suspended.

沙特民航局表示，死者包括四名沙特人，以及来自孟加拉国、美国、约旦、巴勒斯坦、叙利亚、苏丹和埃及的各一人。哈立德国王国际机场的运营已暂停。

A Saudi-led military coalition has vowed to respond to the attack, which it has blamed on the Houthis and called a "war crime".

沙特领导的军事联盟誓言对此次袭击作出回应，并指责胡塞武装发动了这次袭击，称其为“战争罪行”。

Fighting has recently escalated between Yemen's Saudi-backed government forces and the Houthis, who control most of the country.

近期，也门沙特支持的政府军与控制该国大部分地区的胡塞武装之间的战斗升级。

On Saturday, a projectile also fell near King Fahd International Airport in Dammam in eastern Saudi Arabia, the Saudi-led coalition said.

沙特领导的联盟称，周六，一枚炮弹还落在沙特东部达曼的法赫德国王国际机场附近。

The attacks come a day after authorities said three Saudis including a pilot were killed in two attacks on the airport by the Iran-backed Houthis the day prior. Another three people were killed in Houthi attacks on two Saudi airports on 6 and 7 October.

这些袭击发生前一天，当局称前一天伊朗支持的胡塞武装对机场发动两次袭击，造成包括一名飞行员在内的三名沙特人死亡。10月6日和7日，胡塞武装对沙特两个机场的袭击又造成三人死亡。

In Saturday's statement, the Saudi civil aviation authority said some of the injured in were "in critical condition".

在周六的声明中，沙特民航局表示，部分伤者“情况危急”。

It said damage to the airport was being assessed.

声明称，正在评估机场的损坏情况。

An eyewitness who said they had been in the domestic terminal told the news agency that they had seen gate 401 hit and "injured [people] in front of my eyes", adding: "Everyone was running."

一名自称当时在国内航站楼的目击者告诉新闻机构，他们看到401登机口被击中，“伤者就在我眼前”，并补充说：“所有人都在跑。”

More than a dozen ambulances were seen rushing to the airport, according to news agency Reuters, citing an eyewitness.

据路透社援引一名目击者的话报道，十多辆救护车赶往机场。

Saudi-led coalition spokesman Maj Gen Turki al-Maliki later said the attack on one of the passenger terminals resulted in deaths and injuries among civilian passengers and airport staff.

沙特领导的联盟发言人图尔基·马利基少将后来表示，对一个客运航站楼的袭击导致平民乘客和机场工作人员伤亡。

He described the attack as a "full-fledged war crime".

他将此次袭击描述为“彻头彻尾的战争罪行”。

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said strongly condemned recent attacks on Saudi Arabia as he confirmed an American citizen was killed in the strikes.

美国国务卿马尔科·鲁比奥表示强烈谴责近期对沙特阿拉伯的袭击，并确认一名美国公民在袭击中丧生。

Western embassies in Saudi Arabia have warned their citizens away from the airport. The UK Foreign Office told British nationals to avoid the area "until the airport is secure".

西方驻沙特大使馆已警告本国公民远离机场。英国外交部告诉英国公民避免前往该地区，“直到机场安全为止”。

Several Gulf airlines said they had cancelled their flights to and from the airport, with Kuwait Airways citing the "security situation" there.

几家海湾航空公司表示已取消往返该机场的航班，科威特航空 citing 当地的“安全局势”。

All flights to Riyadh have either been diverted or cancelled, aviation tracker Flightradar shows.

航班追踪网站Flightradar显示，所有飞往利雅得的航班要么改道，要么取消。

The Houthis say they are targeting Saudi Arabia over its support for government forces in Yemen's civil war.

胡塞武装称，他们针对沙特阿拉伯是因为其支持也门内战中的政府军。

The conflict worsened after the Houthis seized strategically important stretches of the Red Sea coast in a rapid offensive.

胡塞武装快速进攻，夺取红海沿岸具有重要战略意义的地段后，冲突加剧。

That included the port of Mokha on the Bab al-Mandab Strait, which is the narrowest point of the Red Sea and a chokepoint for global shipping. Yemeni pro-government forces have since said they had "secured" the strait at the southern end of the Red Sea.

其中包括曼德海峡的莫卡港，这是红海最窄处，也是全球航运的咽喉要道。也门亲政府部队此后表示，他们已“确保”红海南端的海峡安全。

A Saudi-led coalition was formed in 2015 after Arab countries joined forces to stop the Houthis taking over Yemen from the country's internationally recognised government.

2015年，阿拉伯国家联合起来阻止胡塞武装从国际公认的也门政府手中夺取该国，随后成立了沙特领导的联盟。

A temporary ceasefire was brokered in 2022 after years of air strikes and ground fighting led to the deaths of hundreds of thousands of people, triggering one of the world's worst humanitarian crises.

多年的空袭和地面战斗导致数十万人死亡，引发世界上最严重的人道主义危机之一，2022年促成了临时停火。

But the informal truce began to unravel in July.

但非正式休战在7月开始瓦解。

The Houthis announced a "maritime embargo" against Saudi Arabia and began missile and drone attacks on Saudi airports, oil facilities and tankers in the Red Sea.

胡塞武装宣布对沙特阿拉伯实施“海上封锁”，并开始对沙特机场、石油设施和红海油轮进行导弹和无人机袭击。

They said they were retaliating against what they described as a years-long Saudi blockade and for an air strike on the airport in Yemen's capital Sanaa, which is controlled by the Houthis.

他们表示，这是对多年沙特封锁以及也门首都萨那机场遭空袭的报复。萨那由胡塞武装控制。
