Ann Widdecombe suspect not known to Prevent as counter terrorism police take over investigation

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Counter terrorism police are now leading the investigation into the death of Ann Widdecombe after "new information and evidence" came to light, officers have said.

警方表示，在出现“新信息和证据”后，反恐警察现已主导对安·威德库姆（Ann Widdecombe）死亡案的调查。

A 28-year-old white British man from Rotherham, South Yorkshire, originally arrested on suspicion of murder on Saturday, has now been re-arrested on suspicion of commission, preparation or instigation of acts of terrorism.

一名来自南约克郡罗瑟勒姆（Rotherham）的28岁英国白人男子，最初于周六因涉嫌谋杀被捕，现已因涉嫌实施、准备或煽动恐怖主义行为再次被捕。

Speaking in the House of Commons, Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood confirmed the suspect was not known to the government's Prevent anti-terror scheme.

内政大臣沙巴纳·马哈茂德（Shabana Mahmood）在下议院证实，该嫌疑人并不在政府的“预防”反恐计划（Prevent）的已知名单中。

Widdecombe, a 78-year-old former Conservative minister turned Reform UK spokeswoman, was found dead at her home in Devon on Thursday, having sustained serious injuries.

威德库姆，78岁，前保守党大臣、后成为改革英国党（Reform UK）发言人，周四在德文郡（Devon）的家中被发现死亡，身上有重伤。

Police were still outside the house of Ann Widdecombe in Haytor in Dartmoor on Monday

周一，警方仍守在德文郡达特穆尔（Dartmoor）海托（Haytor）的安·威德库姆住宅外。

Her death sparked a manhunt resulting in the suspect's arrest at about 21:00 on Saturday.

她的死亡引发了一场搜捕，嫌疑人于周六约21:00被捕。

National Counter Terrorism Policing head Laurence Taylor said the new arrest builds on "progress made by our colleagues in Devon and Cornwall Police", and that they were working to establish a motivation for the attack.

国家反恐警务负责人劳伦斯·泰勒（Laurence Taylor）表示，新的逮捕行动建立在“德文郡和康沃尔郡警方同事取得的进展”之上，他们正在努力确定袭击动机。

"Our priority is progressing this investigation quickly, with all the capabilities we have available to us. If anyone has any information, please share it with the police," he added.

他补充说：“我们的首要任务是利用我们所有能力迅速推进调查。如果有人掌握任何信息，请与警方分享。”

The announcement comes after the suspect in the investigation was seen in CCTV footage appearing to get into a car in Yorkshire, hours before the former Conservative minister is thought to have been attacked in her home in Devon.

这一声明发布之际，调查中的嫌疑人被监控录像拍到，在前保守党大臣据信在德文郡家中遇袭前数小时，出现在约克郡（Yorkshire）并进入一辆汽车。

录像显示，这名身穿白衬衫和短裤的男子在罗瑟勒姆一处住宅外进入一辆红色汽车，其短裤口袋中似乎有一件长物体突出。录像时间戳为周三07:51，由《太阳报》首次报道。

The distance between Widdecombe's home and Rotherham, where the 28-year-old was arrested, is about 270 miles (430km) - about a four-and-a-half-hour drive.

威德库姆的家与28岁嫌疑人被捕的罗瑟勒姆之间的距离约为270英里（430公里），大约四个半小时车程。

At least two police vehicles were seen parked outside a property in Rotherham believed to be connected to the investigation on Sunday, which was cordoned off while officers and forensic teams worked at the scene.

周日，至少有两辆警车停在罗瑟勒姆一处据信与调查有关的房产外，该房产被封锁，警方和法医团队在现场工作。

Speaking to BBC News on the same day, one neighbour said: "I was making some food in the kitchen and looked out the window, there was loads of banging, and there was armed police in the pathway and they just went into the house and pulled [the suspect] out."

同一天，一位邻居对BBC新闻说：“我在厨房做食物时看向窗外，听到很多砰砰声，武装警察在小路上，他们直接进入房子，把（嫌疑人）拉了出来。”

Another neighbour said police took a red car off the drive.

另一位邻居说，警方从车道上开走了一辆红色汽车。

In her statement to MPs on Monday afternoon, Mahmood also paid tribute to Widdecombe as "forthright and fearless" and as someone who "thought seriously about ideas but she did not take herself too seriously".

周一下午，马哈茂德在对议员的声明中也称赞威德库姆“直率无畏”，是一个“认真思考观点但不过于严肃对待自己”的人。

She said her death raised questions about the security of those in public life, and that police intend to issue guidance to MPs on safety soon.

她说，她的死亡引发了关于公众人物安全的问题，警方打算很快向议员发布安全指南。

Mahmood added she had recently commissioned former Lord Chancellor Sir Robert Buckland to review the lessons learned from the murder of Conservative MP Sir David Amess in 2021 regarding security for MPs.

马哈茂德补充说，她最近委托前大法官罗伯特·巴克兰爵士（Sir Robert Buckland）审查2021年保守党议员大卫·埃默斯爵士（Sir David Amess）被谋杀案中关于议员安全的教训。

She acknowledged Widdecombe's death would be a "particular concern" for Reform UK and its leader Nigel Farage, and said she has offered him a meeting with the chair of Royal and VIP Executive Committee (Ravec) - the public body responsible for managing the security of those in public life.

她承认威德库姆的死亡对改革英国党及其领袖奈杰尔·法拉奇（Nigel Farage）来说将是“特别关切”，并表示她已提议他与皇家和贵宾执行委员会（Royal and VIP Executive Committee, Ravec）主席会面——该公共机构负责管理公众人物的安全。

Posting on X, Farage thanked Mahmood for the offer and said he would meet with Ravec's chair to "discuss the security of all Reform politicians, including those who are not MPs".

法拉奇在X上发帖感谢马哈茂德的提议，并表示他将与Ravec主席会面，“讨论所有改革英国党政客的安全，包括那些不是议员的人”。

Also speaking in the Commons, Shadow Home Secretary Chris Philp described Widdecombe as a public figure who was "always formidable, always charming, always entertaining", while Reform UK's deputy leader Richard Tice said she was "a colossus" in public and political life and that whenever she called him, he "always stood up on my toes".

影子内政大臣克里斯·菲尔普（Chris Philp）也在下议院发言，称威德库姆是一位“总是令人敬畏、总是迷人、总是有趣”的公众人物，而改革英国党副领袖理查德·泰斯（Richard Tice）则表示，她在公共和政治生活中是“一个巨人”，每当她打电话给他时，他“总是踮起脚尖”。

On Sunday, Devon and Cornwall Police had said there was "nothing to suggest" the murder was politically motivated.

周日，德文郡和康沃尔郡警方曾表示，“没有迹象”表明谋杀有政治动机。

Assistant Chief Constable (ACC) Matt Longman had said officers remained "open-minded" about a potential motive and that there was not thought to be any threat to the wider public.

助理警长马特·朗曼（Matt Longman）表示，警员对潜在动机仍“持开放态度”，并且认为对广大公众没有威胁。

Speaking to BBC Radio 4's PM programme, Jonathan Hall KC, the government's independent reviewer of terrorism legislation, said he was "slightly surprised" the force guided the public away from the idea of there being a potential political element.

在接受BBC广播4台《PM》节目采访时，政府独立恐怖主义立法审查员乔纳森·霍尔KC（Jonathan Hall KC）表示，他对警方引导公众远离存在潜在政治因素的想法感到“有点惊讶”。

He said: "I don't know why it appears Devon and Cornwall police were trying to steer away from the idea of terrorism even once they'd discounted the idea of some local burglary that had gone wrong, and I simply can't explain that."

他说：“我不知道为什么德文郡和康沃尔郡警方似乎试图引导人们远离恐怖主义的想法，即使他们已经排除了当地入室盗窃出问题的可能性，我根本无法解释这一点。”

Hall acknowledged that although this was a live investigation, police needed to be "really thoughtful" in releasing information in the social media era, and that police communications can improve.

霍尔承认，尽管这是一项正在进行的调查，但在社交媒体时代，警方在发布信息时需要“非常深思熟虑”，警方的沟通可以改进。

"It's much better to be simply open-minded and say to the public something that's, you know, true - 'We don't know what's going on' - rather than appearing to rule something out and then having to reverse their position when new facts emerge," he said.

他说：“最好只是保持开放心态，对公众说一些真实的话——比如‘我们不知道发生了什么’——而不是似乎排除某些可能性，然后在出现新事实时不得不改变立场。”

Floral tributes to Ann Widdecombe at a memorial set up in the grounds of a local hotel, near her home in Haytor, Devon.

在德文郡海托家附近一家当地酒店场地内设立的纪念安·威德库姆的花圈。

Devon and Cornwall Police said it believed Widdecombe was attacked on Wednesday at about 12:30 and had since received more than 120 reports of information after making a public appeal.

德文郡和康沃尔郡警方表示，他们认为威德库姆于周三约12:30遇袭，并在公开呼吁后已收到超过120条信息报告。

Over the coming weeks there would remain a "heightened" police presence in the local area, ACC Longman added.

朗曼警长补充说，未来几周当地将保持“加强”的警力存在。

Widdecombe served as the Conservative MP for Maidstone for 23 years, holding ministerial roles in Sir John Major's government between 1994 and 1997.

威德库姆担任梅德斯通（Maidstone）保守党议员23年，在1994年至1997年间在约翰·梅杰爵士（Sir John Major）政府中担任大臣职务。

Following her departure from the Commons in 2010, Widdecombe appeared on BBC's Strictly Come Dancing that year, and was a runner-up on Celebrity Big Brother eight years later.

2010年离开下议院后，威德库姆同年参加了BBC的《舞动奇迹》（Strictly Come Dancing），并在八年后成为《名人老大哥》（Celebrity Big Brother）的亚军。

She joined the Brexit Party in 2019, where she represented South West England as its MEP from 2019 to 2020.

她于2019年加入脱欧党（Brexit Party），并在2019年至2020年期间代表英格兰西南部担任欧洲议会议员。

On Sunday, about 40 mourners gathered in Haytor Vale to pay tribute to Widdecombe, including senior Reform figures.

周日，约40名哀悼者聚集在海托谷（Haytor Vale）向威德库姆致敬，其中包括改革英国党高层人物。

Tice said he had spoken to Widdecombe last Monday and he "nearly fell over with shock and horror" when he heard of her death.

泰斯表示，他上周一曾与威德库姆通话，听到她的死讯时他“几乎震惊得摔倒”。

"We have lost an absolute colossus, a legend in all our lifetime," he told the crowd.

他对人群说：“我们失去了一位绝对的巨人，我们这一生的传奇。”

Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer also paid tribute to Widdecombe last week, describing her death as a "significant loss" as he called on people to "rise above" political differences.

首相基尔·斯塔默爵士（Sir Keir Starmer）上周也向威德库姆致敬，称她的死亡是“重大损失”，并呼吁人们“超越”政治分歧。

Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch described Widdecombe as a "very fun and feisty woman", adding her "heart is breaking for [Widdecombe's] family".

保守党领袖凯米·巴德诺赫（Kemi Badenoch）形容威德库姆为“非常有趣且好斗的女性”，并补充说她的“心为（威德库姆的）家人而碎”。

Farage, who visited Dartmoor to pay his respects to Widdecombe, described her as a "remarkable individual" and "the fiercest defender of free speech".

法拉奇前往达特穆尔向威德库姆表达敬意，称她为“非凡的个人”和“言论自由最坚定的捍卫者”。

A feeling or belief that someone is guilty of a crime or wrongdoing.
