US strikes Iranian fast boats as Iran attacks UAE oil facility

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

President Donald Trump says the US has struck seven Iranian "fast boats" in the Strait of Hormuz, as Washington seeks to guide stranded ships out of the Gulf through the largely closed waterway.

美国总统唐纳德·特朗普表示，美国在霍尔木兹海峡击中了七艘伊朗“快艇”，华盛顿正试图通过这条基本封闭的水道引导被困船只离开海湾。

The UAE and South Korea both reported strikes on ships in the vital channel on Monday. The UAE also said a fire broke out at the oil port of Fujairah after an Iranian attack.

阿联酋和韩国周一均报告称，在这条关键航道上发生了针对船只的袭击。阿联酋还表示，在伊朗袭击后，富查伊拉石油港口发生火灾。

Shipping company Maersk told the BBC that one of its US-flagged vessels had successfully exited the strait with US military protection - under what Trump has called "Project Freedom".

航运公司马士基告诉BBC，其旗下悬挂美国国旗的一艘船只在美国军事保护下成功驶出海峡——这正是在特朗普所称的“自由计划”下进行的。

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said that events in the strait "make clear that there's no military solution to a political crisis".

伊朗外交部长阿巴斯·阿拉格齐表示，海峡事件“清楚表明政治危机没有军事解决方案”。

He added: "Project Freedom is Project Deadlock."

他补充说：“自由计划就是僵局计划。”

Maersk said the transit of one of its commercial vessels was "completed without incident, and all crew members are safe and unharmed".

马士基表示，其一艘商业船只的过境“顺利完成，所有船员安全无恙”。

The Strait of Hormuz has remained largely blocked since the US and Israel launched air strikes on Iran in February. Tehran responded by blocking the crucial waterway through which 20% of the world's oil and liquefied natural gas is meant to pass freely.

自美国和以色列于2月对伊朗发动空袭以来，霍尔木兹海峡基本保持封锁状态。德黑兰的回应是封锁这条关键水道，全球20%的石油和液化天然气本应自由通过这里。

In early April the US and Iran announced a ceasefire under which Iran ended its drone and missile strikes on Gulf countries including the UAE, but few vessels have been able to transit the strait since then and the US imposed its own blockade on Iranian ports.

4月初，美国和伊朗宣布停火，伊朗停止了对包括阿联酋在内的海湾国家的无人机和导弹袭击，但此后很少有船只能够通过海峡，美国也对伊朗港口实施了封锁。

Trump said: "We've shot down seven small boats or, as they like to call them, 'fast' boats. It's all they have left." The US military said it had used helicopters to attack the boats. Iran denied such attacks had taken place.

特朗普说：“我们击落了七艘小船，或者像他们喜欢叫的那样，‘快’艇。这是他们剩下的全部。”美国军方表示已使用直升机攻击这些船只。伊朗否认发生过此类袭击。

Earlier the US said navy destroyers and US-flagged merchant ships sailed through the strait on Monday. Iran called the claims "entirely false" and said its military said it fired warning shots at a US warship. The US military denied this.

早些时候，美国表示海军驱逐舰和悬挂美国国旗的商船周一驶过了海峡。伊朗称这些说法“完全错误”，并表示其军方称向一艘美国军舰鸣枪示警。美国军方否认了这一点。

Later on Monday shipping firm Maersk said its US-flagged vessel the Alliance Fairfax, which had been stranded in the Gulf since the US and Israeli attack on Iran at the end of February, had exited the Strait of Hormuz.

周一晚些时候，航运公司马士基表示，其悬挂美国国旗的船只“联盟费尔法克斯号”已驶出霍尔木兹海峡。该船自2月底美国和以色列袭击伊朗以来一直被困在海湾。

The firm had been contacted by the US and "offered the opportunity for the vessel to exit the Gulf under US military protection", the firm said. "The vessel subsequently exited the Persian Gulf accompanied by US military assets, the firm, said in a statement.

该公司表示，美国已联系该公司，“为其船只在美国军事保护下离开海湾提供了机会”。该公司在一份声明中说：“该船随后在美国军事资产的护送下驶出波斯湾。”

Meanwhile the UAE's foreign ministry reported a tanker affiliated with Adnoc, its state-owned oil company, was hit in the Strait of Hormuz. South Korea also reported an explosion on one of its ships anchored just off the UAE.

与此同时，阿联酋外交部报告称，其国有石油公司阿布扎比国家石油公司的一艘油轮在霍尔木兹海峡被击中。韩国也报告称，其停泊在阿联酋附近的一艘船只发生爆炸。

The UAE authorities also reported air defences had engaged 12 ballistic missiles, three cruise missiles and four drones. Local officials said one strike caused a large fire and three injuries at its key oil port of Fujairah.

阿联酋当局还报告称，防空系统拦截了12枚弹道导弹、3枚巡航导弹和4架无人机。当地官员表示，一次袭击在其关键石油港口富查伊拉引发大火，造成三人受伤。

The UAE called the attacks a "dangerous escalation" and said it reserved the right to respond. Iranian state TV has quoted an unnamed military official as saying that Iran had "no plans to target the UAE".

阿联酋称这些袭击是“危险的升级”，并保留回应的权利。伊朗国家电视台援引一名未具名军方官员的话称，伊朗“没有计划针对阿联酋”。

The benchmark Brent crude oil price passed $115 a barrel shortly after the reports that Fujairah had come under attack, up more than 5% on the day.

在富查伊拉遭到袭击的消息传出后不久，基准布伦特原油价格突破每桶115美元，当日上涨超过5%。

Fujairah lies on the east coast of the UAE, beyond the Strait of Hormuz. A pipeline from the oilfields of Abu Dhabi runs to Fujairah, allowing limited amounts of crude to be loaded on to tankers and shipped to world markets despite the strait being effectively blockaded.

富查伊拉位于阿联酋东海岸，霍尔木兹海峡之外。一条从阿布扎比油田通往富查伊拉的管道允许有限数量的原油装载到油轮上，运往全球市场，尽管海峡实际上已被封锁。

Neighbouring Qatar condemned the attack on the tanker affiliated with Adnoc and called for the strait's "unconditional reopening".

邻国卡塔尔谴责了对阿布扎比国家石油公司油轮的袭击，并呼吁“无条件重新开放”海峡。

In Oman, two people were injured when a residential building was targeted in Bukha, along the coastline of the Strait of Hormuz, state media reported on Monday.

在阿曼，国家媒体周一报道称，霍尔木兹海峡沿岸的布哈一处住宅楼遭到袭击，两人受伤。

On Sunday, Trump said the US would start helping stranded vessels out of the shipping lane as part of "Project Freedom". An estimated 20,000 seafarers on 2,000 ships have been stuck since the US-Iran war began in February.

周日，特朗普表示，美国将开始帮助被困船只离开航道，作为“自由计划”的一部分。自2月美伊战争开始以来，估计有2000艘船上的20000名海员被困。

The president said the US had been asked by countries "from all over the World" to help free up their ships which were "locked up in the Strait of Hormuz" and were "merely neutral and innocent bystanders!".

总统表示，美国已应“来自世界各地”的国家请求，帮助释放其“被困在霍尔木兹海峡”的船只，这些船只“只是中立的无辜旁观者！”。

There has been growing concern over dwindling supplies and the effects on sailors' physical and mental health.

人们对供应减少以及海员身心健康的影响日益担忧。

But Trump did not say how they would be able to sail away - he only threatened to use force "if, in any way, this Humanitarian process is interfered with".

但特朗普没有说明他们将如何驶离——他只威胁说，“如果这一人道主义进程以任何方式受到干扰”，将使用武力。
