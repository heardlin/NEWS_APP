Today's Eight Words

constraint /kənˈstreɪnt/ n.

限制，约束

A limitation or restriction.

“We have a relatively new government with seemingly big ambitions but facing a long list of constraints.”

我们有一个相对较新的政府，看似雄心勃勃，但面临着一长串限制。

proactive /proʊˈæktɪv/ adj.

积极主动的

Creating or controlling a situation by causing something to happen rather than responding to it after it has happened.

“And how do you deliver a proudly proactive state, delivering for people in a noticeable way, when the tax burden is generationally high, the national debt is sky high, energy bills are rising and inflation is higher than expected?”

当税负达到世代高点、国家债务高企、能源账单上涨、通胀高于预期时，你如何打造一个自豪的积极主动的国家，以显著的方式为人民服务？

levy /ˈlevi/ n.

征税，税款

An amount of money, such as a tax, that you have to pay to a government or organization.

“The banks are braced for a levy or surcharge that may pay for this.”

银行正准备应对可能为此支付的征税或附加费。

devolution /ˌdevəˈluːʃn/ n.

权力下放

The transfer of power or authority from central government to local government.

“And one of the prime minister's key ideas - handing power away from Westminster and closer to you, devolution - will be central too.”

首相的关键理念之一——将权力从威斯敏斯特下放，更贴近你，即权力下放——也将是核心。

robust /roʊˈbʌst/ adj.

强劲的，稳健的

Strong and healthy; vigorous.

“The chancellor will argue the economy is becoming more robust – and yes, there are some economic indicators that are more positive than forecast: growth is better than expected, as is productivity and unemployment is down.”

财政大臣将主张经济正变得更加强劲——是的，一些经济指标比预测更积极：增长好于预期，生产力也是如此，失业率下降。

resilient /rɪˈzɪliənt/ adj.

有韧性的，能恢复的

Able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions.

“So, despite the signs that the UK economy is becoming more resilient, household and public finances are in for another shock.”

因此，尽管有迹象表明英国经济正变得更具韧性，但家庭和公共财政将面临另一次冲击。

tacit /ˈtæsɪt/ adj.

心照不宣的，默许的

Understood or implied without being stated.

“The chancellor has been talking up a desire to offer "breathing space" and "hope" - a tacit acknowledgement that there is a limit to what the government can do given the constraints it faces.”

财政大臣一直在谈论提供“喘息空间”和“希望”的愿望——这是对政府面临限制所能做的事情有限的一种心照不宣的承认。

tinker /ˈtɪŋkər/ v.

小修小补，摆弄

To make small changes to something, often without a clear plan or purpose.

“Opposition parties will argue it amounts to tinkering.”

反对党将争辩说这只不过是修修补补。
