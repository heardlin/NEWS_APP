Police have urged more victims of Simon Levy to come forward, as London's victims' commissioner said a "litany of failures" had allowed him to kill two women and rape a third.

警方敦促更多西蒙·利维的受害者站出来，同时伦敦受害者专员表示，一系列“失职”使他得以杀害两名女性并强奸第三名女性。

Andrea Simon called for a review on the "failure to monitor him appropriately" when the 40-year-old was known to police as a sexual predator.

安德烈娅·西蒙呼吁对“未能适当监控他”一事进行审查，当时这名40岁的男子已被警方认定为性侵犯者。

British Transport Police said women who recognised Levy as their attacker should report it, while the Met Police said it had investigated whether he had more victims, including further murders.

英国交通警察表示，认出利维是袭击者的女性应报案，而伦敦警察厅则表示已调查他是否还有其他受害者，包括更多谋杀案。

Meanwhile, the Independent Office of Police Conduct (IOPC) said a police constable is under investigation for gross misconduct, and a detective sergeant is facing a misconduct investigation.

与此同时，警察行为独立办公室（IOPC）表示，一名警员正因严重不当行为接受调查，一名警长面临不当行为调查。

On Friday, Levy was found guilty of murdering two women and raping a third in London while he was being monitored by the Met Police for being a convicted sex offender. He had previously committed a string of sex attacks.

周五，利维被判犯有谋杀两名女性及强奸第三名女性的罪行，作案时他正因被定罪的性犯罪者身份而受到伦敦警察厅的监控。他此前曾犯下多起性侵案。

The BBC reported how a series of grave failings by police, prosecutors and the courts left Levy free to murder, rape and sexually assault women .

BBC报道了警方、检察官和法院的一系列严重失职如何让利维得以自由地谋杀、强奸和性侵女性。

Levy is due to be sentenced on Wednesday.

利维将于周三被判刑。

The Met, British Transport Police (BTP) and Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) have all admitted failures in how Levy was monitored between his first conviction in September 2021 on sexual assault charges and his arrest for murder in September 2025.

伦敦警察厅、英国交通警察（BTP）和皇家检察署（CPS）均承认，在利维于2021年9月首次因性侵指控被定罪至2025年9月因谋杀被捕期间，对其监控存在失职。

On Saturday, Simons told BBC Breakfast a "multi-agency review" was needed, saying: "There was a lack of restrictions that could have been placed on him and a failure to monitor him appropriately - so all of those things, I think, we need answers to now."

周六，西蒙斯告诉BBC早餐节目，需要进行“多机构审查”，她说：“本可以对他施加限制，但缺乏限制，且未能适当监控他——所以我认为，我们现在需要所有这些问题的答案。”

Simons said public services "have to get better at recognising patterns of offending" before attackers become "emboldened".

西蒙斯表示，公共服务“必须更好地识别犯罪模式”，以防攻击者变得“胆大妄为”。

The Met said its detectives had "exhausted numerous lines of enquiry - including working with coroners â to identify whether Levy is responsible for any similar, unsolved incidents, including deaths".

伦敦警察厅表示，其侦探“已穷尽多条调查线索——包括与验尸官合作——以确定利维是否应对任何类似的未破案件（包括死亡事件）负责”。

No further victims have been identified so far but the force urged "anyone who believes they may be a victim of his to contact police".

目前尚未发现更多受害者，但警方敦促“任何认为自己可能是其受害者的人与警方联系”。

Levy is known to have attacked at least 10 women on public transport in London, seeking out busy carriages to assault passengers.

据悉，利维在伦敦公共交通上至少袭击了10名女性，专门寻找拥挤的车厢对乘客进行性侵。

BTP said more women "may recognise [Levy] and have not yet reported what has happened to them".

英国交通警察表示，更多女性“可能认出利维，但尚未报告发生在她们身上的事”。

Levy was found guilty of murdering Sheryl Wilkins (l), 39, and Carmenza Valencia-Trujillo, 53

利维被判谋杀谢丽尔·威尔金斯（左，39岁）和卡门萨·瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略（53岁）

Levy was on bail and being monitored by police when he committed a violent rape in January 2025.

利维在2025年1月犯下暴力强奸案时，正处于保释期间并受警方监控。

He went on to kill Carmenza Valencia-Trujillo, 53, in March of that year and Sheryl Wilkins, 39, five months later.

同年3月，他杀害了53岁的卡门萨·瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略，五个月后又杀害了39岁的谢丽尔·威尔金斯。

Earlier this year he was found guilty by a jury at Inner London Crown Court of groping 10 women on the Tube and an 11th while in prison, but details of that case could only be reported this week at the conclusion of his murder trial.

今年早些时候，他被伦敦内城刑事法院的陪审团判定在地铁上猥亵10名女性，并在监狱中猥亵第11名女性，但该案细节直到本周其谋杀案审判结束才得以报道。

The offence in the prison took place on April 2022. The Tube assaults then occurred between 24 October 2023 and May 2025.

监狱中的犯罪行为发生在2022年4月。地铁袭击则发生在2023年10月24日至2025年5月之间。

He had already been convicted of sexually assaulting two women in 2018, which he was jailed for in 2021.

他此前已于2018年因性侵两名女性被定罪，并于2021年入狱。

BTP has confirmed two officers have been moved to other roles due to "incompetence" in handling investigations into Levy's transport assaults.

英国交通警察已确认，两名警官因在处理利维交通袭击调查中“无能”而被调任其他职务。

Lisa Ramsarran, chief crown prosecutor for the CPS, said: "Our actions fell short of the standards that victims, families and the public are entitled to expect."

皇家检察署首席检察官丽莎·拉姆萨兰表示：“我们的行为未达到受害者、家属和公众有权期望的标准。”

Lisa Squire, whose daughter Libby was murdered by a man who had previously committed a string of sex crimes, told the BBC that a "catalogue of disasters" had led to Levy's murders.

丽莎·斯夸尔的女儿利比被一名曾犯下多起性犯罪的男子谋杀，她告诉BBC，一系列“灾难”导致了利维的谋杀案。

She told BBC Radio 4's Today programme "the patterns were there, he was flying those red flags", and said there were "precursor offences" for which he could have stopped by police.

她告诉BBC广播4台《今日》节目，“模式就在那里，他挥舞着那些红旗”，并说存在“前兆犯罪”，警方本可以阻止他。

"At the moment, the message going out, in my opinion, is that women's safety is not important," she continued.

“目前，在我看来，传递出的信息是女性的安全并不重要，”她继续说道。

She added: "It's not one or two missed opportunities that could have brought him to justice, that could have had him locked up and behind bars and no longer a threat to women and girls. It was an extraordinary succession."

她补充说：“不是一两次错失的机会本可以让他受到法律制裁，本可以让他被关进监狱，不再对女性构成威胁。这是一连串的失职。”
