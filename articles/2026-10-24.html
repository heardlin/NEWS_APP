More than 10,000 homes are without power as Storm Austen continues to batter southern England and Wales, with more bad weather expected this weekend.

随着风暴“奥斯汀”（Storm Austen）继续袭击英格兰南部和威尔士，超过1万户家庭断电，预计本周末还会有更多恶劣天气。

The first named storm of the season brought gusts of up to 84mph (135km/h) to Jersey, while in southern England the wild weather disrupted travel with fallen trees blocking railway lines and roads.

本季首个被命名的风暴给泽西岛带来了高达84英里/小时（135公里/小时）的阵风，而在英格兰南部，恶劣天气导致交通中断，倒下的树木阻塞了铁路线和道路。

In Brighton, part of the iconic West Pier - an abandoned structure that first opened in 1866 - collapsed into the sea.

在布莱顿，标志性的西码头（West Pier）的一部分——一座始建于1866年的废弃建筑——坍塌入海。

On Saturday, Storm Boelo is forecast to bring more strong winds and heavy rain. The Met Office has put in place an amber warning for wind on Saturday and Sunday to cover parts of Scotland, northern England and Northern Ireland.

周六，风暴“博埃洛”（Storm Boelo）预计将带来更多强风和大雨。英国气象局（Met Office）已发布周六和周日的琥珀色大风预警，覆盖苏格兰、英格兰北部和北爱尔兰的部分地区。

Austen made landfall in the south-west overnight and tracked eastwards throughout the day. All schools on Jersey and Guernsey closed for the day and some in Cornwall were closed or started later than normal.

“奥斯汀”在夜间登陆西南部，并全天向东移动。泽西岛和根西岛的所有学校当天停课，康沃尔郡的一些学校停课或推迟上课。

Ahead of the worst of the storm on Thursday afternoon, families were evacuated from a holiday park in Perranporth, Cornwall. Among them was Hannah Dawson-Turk, who told the BBC "the night before last, it sounded like our caravan was going to be ripped apart".

在周四下午风暴最严重之前，康沃尔郡佩兰波思的一个度假公园疏散了家庭。其中汉娜·道森-特克（Hannah Dawson-Turk）告诉BBC：“前天晚上，听起来我们的房车要被撕裂了。”

"The storm is very loud outside now, and the waves look huge from our windows," she said from a nearby hotel.

“现在外面的风暴非常响，从窗户看出去海浪巨大，”她在附近的一家酒店说。

Choppy seas led to a dramatic display at Newhaven, East Sussex

波涛汹涌的海面在东萨塞克斯郡纽黑文上演了戏剧性的一幕

The National Grid said engineers had restored power to more than 60,000 properties since Storm Austen began.

国家电网（National Grid）表示，自风暴“奥斯汀”开始以来，工程师已为超过6万户家庭恢复供电。

Some ferry services between the UK and France were cancelled, while National Rail advised many routes were travelling at slower speeds.

英国与法国之间的部分渡轮服务被取消，而国家铁路（National Rail）建议许多线路减速行驶。

In Brighton, onlookers filmed as a section of the West Pier fell into the sea as it was pummeled by large waves.

在布莱顿，旁观者拍摄到西码头的一部分在巨浪冲击下落入海中。

Stephen Royle, who lives opposite, said the waves rolling in were "enormous" and he is "shocked" by how much of the pier ended up in the sea.

住在对面的斯蒂芬·罗伊尔（Stephen Royle）说，涌来的海浪“巨大”，他对码头有如此大的一部分落入海中感到“震惊”。

The pier closed to the public in 1975 and fell into disrepair, but the ruined structure became a symbol of Brighton for some.

该码头于1975年对公众关闭，年久失修，但这座废墟结构对一些人来说已成为布莱顿的象征。

On the Channel Islands, fresh food shortages are expected in the coming days due to a lack of ferry services, according to the boss of the Co-op there.

据当地合作社（Co-op）的老板称，在海峡群岛，由于渡轮服务缺乏，预计未来几天将出现新鲜食品短缺。

"It's rare we get this level of disruption to storms backing up so quickly and probably a little bit more heavier than what we've had to deal with in the past," Mark Cox said.

“我们很少遇到如此迅速加剧的风暴破坏，而且可能比我们过去应对的还要严重一些，”马克·考克斯（Mark Cox）说。

Paul Aked, head of Jersey Met, said there were gusts in Guernsey up to 76mph (122km/h), equivalent to a severe gale, force nine. Residents told BBC Radio Guernsey that they could hear the wind rattling through their homes.

泽西岛气象局（Jersey Met）负责人保罗·阿凯德（Paul Aked）表示，根西岛的阵风高达76英里/小时（122公里/小时），相当于九级烈风。居民告诉BBC根西岛电台，他们能听到风穿过房屋的嘎嘎声。

Jersey Airport said flights won't be running "until at least 17:00" while Cornwall Airport Newquay is warning passengers to check with their airline before travel.

泽西岛机场表示航班“至少到17:00”才能运行，而康沃尔郡纽基机场警告乘客在出行前与航空公司确认。

Departure boards at Exeter airport were showing multiple cancellations.

埃克塞特机场的出发信息牌显示多趟航班取消。

Train operators across the country have warned that services are likely to be disrupted over the weekend.

全国各地的火车运营商警告称，本周末服务可能中断。

A car in Horsham was badly damaged after a tree fell onto it, however the driver was unharmed

霍舍姆的一辆汽车被倒下的树砸中严重受损，但司机没有受伤

The UK will continue to be battered by strong winds and heavy rain over the weekend as Storm Boelo follows on the heels of Storm Austen.

随着风暴“博埃洛”紧随风暴“奥斯汀”之后，英国本周末将继续遭受强风和大雨袭击。

The Met Office has issued an amber weather warning for wind for much of Scotland, excluding the Northern Isles, as well as the far north of Northern Ireland and England.

英国气象局已对苏格兰大部分地区（不包括北部群岛）以及北爱尔兰和英格兰的最北部发布琥珀色大风天气预警。

Wind gusts of 60-70mph are forecast, with exposed coastal areas potentially seeing speeds of up to 80mph.

预计阵风时速60-70英里，暴露的沿海地区可能高达80英里/小时。

The Central Lowlands could also experience disruption as strong winds funnel through the region.

中部低地也可能因强风穿过该地区而受到影响。

Damage caused by strong winds could lead to further power cuts, while travel disruption is also predicted.

强风造成的破坏可能导致进一步停电，预计交通也会中断。

Yellow wind warnings also cover Orkney, the rest of Northern Ireland and northern England.

黄色大风预警还覆盖奥克尼群岛、北爱尔兰其余地区和英格兰北部。

Gusts of 55-75mph are possible in these areas, with isolated gusts potentially reaching 80mph.

这些地区的阵风可能达到55-75英里/小时，孤立阵风可能达到80英里/小时。

Alongside the strong winds, heavy and persistent rain is forecast across the UK on Saturday.

除了强风，周六英国各地预计还将有大雨和持续降雨。

Spray and surface water could make driving conditions hazardous, while further downpours increase the risk of localised flooding.

水花和地表水可能使驾驶条件危险，而进一步的倾盆大雨增加了局部洪水的风险。

The Met Office has advised the public to drive cautiously in strong wind, and to secure loose items outside their homes such as bins and trampolines.

英国气象局建议公众在强风中谨慎驾驶，并固定好家外的松散物品，如垃圾桶和蹦床。

People living or working in coastal areas were advised to take extra care during windy or stormy weather, including staying indoors as much as possible.

建议在沿海地区生活或工作的人在多风或暴风雨天气中格外小心，包括尽量待在室内。

A collapsed tree in the Jersey town of Gorey

泽西岛戈里镇一棵倒塌的树

The storm came as the government released general advice for households to stock up on tinned food and bottled water in order to be prepared for emergencies.

风暴来袭之际，政府发布了家庭一般性建议，储备罐头食品和瓶装水以应对紧急情况。

The government has drawn up recommendations for households to have three days' worth of bottled water and tinned food to deal with a crisis such as a war, an online attack or a natural disaster.

政府已制定建议，要求家庭准备三天的瓶装水和罐头食品，以应对战争、网络攻击或自然灾害等危机。

Prime Minister Andy Burnham said: "It's not about panic buying... It's about just taking sensible measures to have enough in the house to tide you over should there be a difficult situation."

首相安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）表示：“这不是恐慌性购买……只是采取明智措施，家里有足够的物资，以便在困难情况下渡过难关。”

Last month, the National Audit Office warned Britain's food supply chain is at risk given the growing threat of cyber-attacks and the climate crisis.

上个月，国家审计署（National Audit Office）警告称，鉴于网络攻击和气候危机的威胁日益严重，英国的食品供应链面临风险。

It said the Department for Food, the Environment and Rural Affairs had not done enough in recent years to improve supply chain resilience.

该机构表示，环境、食品和农村事务部近年来在提高供应链韧性方面做得不够。
