Three dead after family get into difficulty in sea

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Emergency services at scene of Shoreham incident

肖勒姆事件现场的应急服务

Three members of the same family have died after getting into difficulty in the water off Shoreham-by-Sea.

同一家庭的三名成员在肖勒姆海上遇险后不幸身亡。

Sussex Police said three people - a man, a woman, and a teenage girl - had died, while a younger girl from the same family was in hospital in a critical condition.

苏塞克斯警方表示，三人——一名男子、一名女子和一名少女——已经死亡，而同家庭的另一名较年幼女孩正在医院接受治疗，情况危急。

Emergency services were called shortly after 15:00 BST on Tuesday to reports of four people in difficulty in the sea close to Shoreham Port.

周二英国夏令时15:00刚过，应急服务接到报告称有四人肖勒姆港附近海域遇险，随即赶往现场。

Witnesses described seeing large numbers of emergency vehicles and helicopters arriving at the seafront as coastguard crews, RNLI lifeboats, air ambulances, police officers and ambulance crews responded.

目击者描述看到大量应急车辆和直升机抵达海滨，海岸警卫队、皇家全国救生艇协会（RNLI）救生艇、空中救护车、警察和救护人员均作出响应。

Ch Supt James Collis said relatives had been informed and were being supported by specialist officers.

总警司詹姆斯·科利斯表示，已通知遇难者亲属，并由专业警员提供支持。

"At this point, we believe all four entered the water from the land for a swim, and sadly got into difficulty," he said.

他说：“目前我们认为，这四人是从岸边下水游泳，不幸遇险。”

Coastguards have completed an extensive search of the water and are not searching for anyone else, he said.

他表示，海岸警卫队已完成对水域的全面搜索，并未在寻找其他人。

He said the incident had had a profound effect on the local community.

他说，这一事件对当地社区产生了深远影响。

Appealing for witnesses, he said inquiries were ongoing to understand the full details.

他呼吁目击者提供信息，并表示调查正在进行，以了解全部细节。

A spokesperson for Air Ambulance Charity Kent Surrey Sussex said both of its helicopters and critical care team had been sent to the scene as part of the response, and Shoreham RNLI said its lifeboat had beencalled out at 15:00 BST.

肯特、萨里和苏塞克斯空中救护慈善机构发言人表示，作为响应的一部分，其两架直升机和重症监护团队已被派往现场，肖勒姆皇家全国救生艇协会（RNLI）表示其救生艇于英国夏令时15:00出动。

South East Coast Ambulance Service (Secamb) said it had been called to "reports of four people in need of medical attention".

东南海岸救护车服务（Secamb）表示，他们接到“四人需要医疗救助”的报告。

A section of the A259 â a main commuter road which runs through Brighton, Shoreham and Worthing â was closed.

A259公路——一条贯穿布莱顿、肖勒姆和沃辛的主要通勤道路——的部分路段被封闭。

BBC reporter Callum French said despite warm weather, sea conditions had been "pretty rough" because of high winds, describing the response as " the biggest turnout [of emergency services] I have seen ".

BBC记者卡勒姆·弗伦奇表示，尽管天气温暖，但由于强风，海况“相当恶劣”，并将此次响应描述为“我所见过的（应急服务）最大规模出动”。

Witnesses described seeing large numbers of emergency vehicles and helicopters arriving at the seafront.

目击者描述看到大量应急车辆和直升机抵达海滨。

Resident Shirley Pumfrey said: "I have never seen them do that. The whole road was just full of emergency services.

居民雪莉·庞弗雷说：“我从未见过他们这样。整条路上都是应急服务车辆。”

"It was horrific and quite scary to see that many."

“看到这么多，真是可怕又吓人。”

Steve Chapman, who lives opposite the beach, said he couldn't get into his road because it was cordoned off.

住在海滩对面的史蒂夫·查普曼说，他无法进入自己的街道，因为已被封锁。

"There were all sorts of rumours flying around, it was very upsetting," he said.

他说：“各种谣言四起，非常令人不安。”

Another resident, Pete Book, said he was walking home from the pub when he saw ambulances, coastguard crews and helicopters converging on the area.

另一位居民皮特·布克说，他正从酒吧步行回家时，看到救护车、海岸警卫队和直升机聚集在该地区。

Shelley Ayres, who also lives opposite the beach at Shoreham, said: "The sea has been very calm for months and months, but today it has got a lot rougher."

同样住在肖勒姆海滩对面的雪莉·艾尔斯说：“几个月来海面一直很平静，但今天变得汹涌多了。”

Tom Rutland, East Worthing and Shoreham MP, earlier urged people to stay away from the area while emergency services dealt with the incident.

东沃辛和肖勒姆议员汤姆·拉特兰早些时候敦促人们在应急服务处理事件期间远离该地区。

After news emerged of the deaths, he said his thoughts were with the family and friends of those who had died and he added: "I know the whole community here in Shoreham will be hoping and praying for the recovery of the young girl , external receiving medical treatment in hospital."

在死亡消息传出后，他表示对遇难者的家人和朋友表示慰问，并补充说：“我知道肖勒姆整个社区都会为正在医院接受治疗的年轻女孩祈祷，希望她康复。”

Shoreham Port said it was working with multiple agencies and also urged the public to avoid the area.

肖勒姆港表示正在与多个机构合作，并敦促公众避开该地区。

A huge response from emergency services was launched to the waterfront at Shoreham on Tuesday afternoon

周二下午，应急服务对肖勒姆海滨展开了大规模响应。
