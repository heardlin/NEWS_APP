A lawyer for convicted murderer Christa Pike says that she woke up in hospital "angry and confused" after she survived a botched execution attempt last week.

被定罪的杀人犯克里斯塔·派克（Christa Pike）的律师表示，上周她在一次拙劣的处决尝试中幸存下来后，在医院醒来时“愤怒且困惑”。

Her first words were to ask "Where am I?" and "Who are you?", her lawyer Randy Spivey recalled during a news conference.

她的律师兰迪·斯派维（Randy Spivey）在新闻发布会上回忆说，她说的第一句话是“我在哪里？”和“你是谁？”。

Pike, who was sentenced to death after murdering Colleen Slemmer with her then-boyfriend in 1995, survived two lethal injections on 30 September in Tennessee.

派克因在1995年与当时的男友一起谋杀科琳·斯莱默（Colleen Slemmer）而被判处死刑，于9月30日在田纳西州（Tennessee）接受了两次致命注射后幸存下来。

Experts and her legal team say her apparent recovery is unprecedented, and that no other US inmate has survived a lethal injection from executioners.

专家和她的法律团队表示，她的明显恢复是史无前例的，没有其他美国囚犯在处决者的致命注射中幸存下来。

A court on Wednesday ordered the state of Tennessee to preserve all evidence related to the failed execution.

周三，法院命令田纳西州（Tennessee）保存与此次失败处决相关的所有证据。

Pike, 50, continues to receive critical medical care and is being restrained at her hands and feet in her hospital bed, her lawyers said at the news conference after a court hearing on Wednesday.

律师们在周三的法庭听证会后于新闻发布会上表示，50岁的派克仍在接受重症医疗护理，并在医院病床上被束缚着手脚。

Spivey said that her legal team believed for days that she was brain dead, and expected her to be taken off life support at any moment.

斯派维说，她的法律团队数天来认为她脑死亡，并预计她随时会被撤除生命支持。

However, in a "shock to everyone", she began to talk on Tuesday, while recalling only limited details about the execution attempt.

然而，在“令所有人震惊”的情况下，她于周二开始说话，但对处决尝试的细节回忆有限。

Pike, he said, had helped the execution team find a vein in her arm, and she was kind and polite while she was being injected.

他说，派克曾帮助处决小组找到她手臂上的静脉，并且在被注射时她友善而有礼貌。

Then she woke up in a hospital with a tube down her throat and people she didn't know. Both of her arms, he said, are now "tremendously swollen".

然后她在医院醒来，喉咙里插着管子，周围是她不认识的人。他说，她的双臂现在“极度肿胀”。

"We don't know the toll all of this will take" on her mental health, Spivey said, noting how she was left "angry and confused" by the ordeal.

斯派维说，“我们不知道这一切会对她的心理健康造成什么影响”，并指出她因这次折磨而“愤怒且困惑”。

He added that she also has a blood clot in one of her arms, pneumonia, and is not able to swallow.

他补充说，她的一只手臂还有血栓、肺炎，并且无法吞咽。

Pike is believed to be the only person to survive an execution attempt by lethal injection.

据信，派克是唯一一个在致命注射处决尝试中幸存下来的人。

After it became clear Pike would not die, following two rounds of the drug pentobarbital, she was taken to hospital, where she remained unconscious for days as medical staff cleared her body of the injected chemical.

在第二轮戊巴比妥（pentobarbital）药物注射后，派克显然不会死亡，随后她被送往医院，在那里昏迷了数天，医疗人员清除她体内的注射化学物质。

Earlier on Wednesday, lawyers appeared before a judge for an emergency hearing, where her team requested that all evidence from the botched execution be preserved.

周三早些时候，律师们出现在法官面前进行紧急听证会，她的团队要求保存此次拙劣处决的所有证据。

That evidence could lay the groundwork for a future lawsuit against the state.

这些证据可能为未来针对该州的诉讼奠定基础。

Luke Ihnen, one of Pike's lawyers, began by describing the "roadblocks" that have made it challenging to communicate with his client.

派克的律师之一卢克·伊南（Luke Ihnen）首先描述了与客户沟通所面临的“障碍”。

"Up until yesterday our client was unconscious, and unable to communicate," he told Judge Chancellor I'Ashea Myles. Pike is now "speaking limitedly", he said.

他告诉法官校长伊阿谢亚·迈尔斯（I'Ashea Myles）：“直到昨天，我们的客户还处于昏迷状态，无法沟通。”他说，派克现在“说话有限”。

Tennessee Assistant Attorney General Will Ayers called attention to the "unusual procedural position here", prompting agreement from the judge. He argued that an emergency motion was unnecessary because the state is already compelled to preserve evidence.

田纳西州（Tennessee）助理总检察长威尔·艾尔斯（Will Ayers）提请注意“此处不寻常的程序立场”，促使法官同意。他辩称，紧急动议没有必要，因为该州已被迫保存证据。

Still, Judge Myles ordered that "anything that was in the actual room needs to be preserved and maintained," including IV lines and syringes, as well as waste, like packaging, inserts, and labels.

尽管如此，迈尔斯法官仍命令“实际房间内的任何物品都需要保存和维护”，包括静脉输液管和注射器，以及废物，如包装、插页和标签。

The judge also demanded documentation on Pike's psychological condition prior to, during and after the 30 September execution attempt.

法官还要求提供派克在9月30日处决尝试之前、期间和之后心理状况的文件。

The judge agreed when a state lawyer called the situation 'unusual'

当一名州律师称情况“不寻常”时，法官表示同意。

She advised the state to take "special care" not to misplace or accidentally delete any information that may be pertinent to the case.

她建议该州“特别小心”，不要 misplaced 或意外删除任何可能与案件相关的信息。

Asked by the judge whether the state intended to reschedule execution for Pike, Ayers said he did not know the answer.

当法官询问该州是否打算重新安排对派克的处决时，艾尔斯说他不知道答案。

Tennessee Governor Bill Lee has ordered a pause on all executions in the state for the rest of the year, and has said he wants answers about what went wrong. The state's prisons chief said he is resigning after the failed execution.

田纳西州（Tennessee）州长比尔·李（Bill Lee）已下令暂停该州今年剩余时间的所有处决，并表示他希望了解出了什么问题。该州监狱负责人表示，在处决失败后他将辞职。

Ihnen asked the judge to allow Pike access to a phone so she can call her lawyers, and they can have "continuous liberal access to Ms Pike". He said they were kept waiting for over two hours during a recent attempt to visit her in hospital.

伊南要求法官允许派克使用电话，以便她可以给律师打电话，并且他们可以“持续自由地接触派克女士”。他说，在最近一次试图去医院探望她时，他们被等待了两个多小时。

Ihnen added that Tennessee prison officials are keeping her "shackled" to her hospital bed, despite requests from medical staff that the handcuffs be removed.

伊南补充说，田纳西州（Tennessee）监狱官员将她“铐”在医院病床上，尽管医疗人员要求移除手铐。

Her lawyer also requested that the Tennessee Department of Corrections be required to notify his team 48 hours before she is transferred from the hospital to any state hospital or other government-run facility.

她的律师还要求田纳西州惩教署（Tennessee Department of Corrections）在她从医院转移到任何州立医院或其他政府运营设施前48小时通知他的团队。

Ayers (left) argued the case for the state, while Ihnen (far right) represented Pike

艾尔斯（左）为州方辩护，而伊南（最右）代表派克

Pike was 18 when she and her then-boyfriend, Tadaryl Shipp, beat, tortured and killed 19-year-old Slemmer, whom they had met at a job training camp for troubled teens.

派克18岁时，她和当时的男友塔达里尔·希普（Tadaryl Shipp）殴打、折磨并杀害了19岁的斯莱默，他们是在一个为问题青少年举办的工作训练营中认识的。

Shipp was 17 years old at the time, meaning that he was spared the death penalty for his part in the attack because he was not yet legally an adult at the time. He remains in prison.

希普当时17岁，这意味着他因参与袭击而免于死刑，因为他当时还未成年。他仍在监狱中。
