Bus hits train stopped on French side of Channel Tunnel, with 19 injured

A bus has hit a stationary freight train near the French entrance to the Channel Tunnel, injuring the driver and 18 passengers, French authorities say.

法国当局称，一辆巴士在英吉利海峡隧道法国入口附近撞上了一列停靠的货运列车，导致司机和18名乘客受伤。

The bus, which was carrying lorry drivers due to take a shuttle rail service, struck the train at the loading platform of the tunnel in Coquelles, near Calais, at 19:27 (18:27 BST) on Thursday.

这辆巴士载有准备乘坐穿梭列车服务的卡车司机，于周四19:27（英国夏令时18:27）在加来附近的科凯勒隧道装载平台撞上了列车。

The bus driver and one passenger were severely injured and evacuated by helicopter. None of the injuries were life threatening, a local official said.

巴士司机和一名乘客伤势严重，已由直升机撤离。当地官员表示，所有伤者均无生命危险。

Local sub-prefect Agathe Cury said freight and Eurostar passenger traffic through the tunnel had not been disrupted after a brief stoppage to evacuate injured people. An investigation has begun.

当地副省长阿加特·库里表示，在短暂停车疏散伤者后，通过隧道的货运和欧洲之星客运交通未受影响。调查已经开始。

Trains near the French entrance to the Channel Tunnel (file image)

英吉利海峡隧道法国入口附近的列车（资料图）

"Two trains were on either side of the platform. One was being loaded by heavy goods vehicles ahead of a cross-Channel journey, and another train that had previously been loaded was departing," Cury said.

库里说：“两列火车分别位于站台两侧。一列正在装载重型货车，准备进行跨海峡旅程，另一列之前已装载完毕的列车正在出发。”

"For a reason that currently remains unknown, the minibus collided with the departing train.

“目前原因不明，小巴撞上了正在出发的列车。”

"The bus did not overturn, it is still upright â but the shock and the collision caused injuries to the people involved."

“巴士没有翻车，仍然直立——但撞击和碰撞导致相关人员受伤。”

The incident involved a minibus transporting workers who were about to board Le Shuttle. All were men of different nationalities, she added.

事件涉及一辆运送即将登上Le Shuttle的工人的小巴。她补充说，所有人都是不同国籍的男性。

It is not clear whether any British nationals are among the injured.

目前不清楚伤者中是否有英国公民。

Cury said it was "the first time an incident of this type has occurred" on an Eurotunnel loading platform.

库里表示，这是“欧洲隧道装载平台上首次发生此类事件”。

The BBC's Evan Davis, who was travelling through the Eurotunnel to Calais at the time of the incident, witnessed blue light vehicles "concentrated on one of the platforms" as he entered France.

BBC的埃文·戴维斯在事故发生时正通过欧洲隧道前往加来，他进入法国时目睹了蓝色灯光车辆“集中在其中一个站台上”。

"There were plenty of cars queueing up at check-in when I drove past just now, and I saw some cars going through check-in, so despite the collision they weren't being held," he said from the scene.

他在现场说：“我刚才开车经过时，检查站有很多车在排队，我看到一些车正在通过检查站，所以尽管发生了碰撞，他们并没有被拦住。”

Photos published by local French newspaper La Voix du Nord , external showed a small bus with damage to its front and side standing alongside a train, among a crowd of emergency workers.

法国当地报纸《北方之声》发布的外部照片显示，一辆前部和侧面受损的小型巴士停在列车旁边，周围是紧急救援人员。

Eurostar has released an update saying: "An incident occurred several kilometers away from the Channel Tunnel.

欧洲之星发布更新称：“在距离英吉利海峡隧道几公里处发生了一起事件。”

"This incident does not involved or impact Eurostar trains running from and to London. All of Eurostar trains will run as expected."

“此事件不涉及或影响往返伦敦的欧洲之星列车。所有欧洲之星列车将按计划运行。”

The Eurotunnel runs under the sea between England and France, from Folkestone in Kent to Coquelles on the French side.

欧洲隧道在英格兰和法国之间的海底运行，从肯特郡的福克斯通到法国侧的科凯勒。

Three different rail services operate through the tunnel - the Eurostar, a passenger train that begins in London and goes to several European destinations, freight trains which transport goods and Le Shuttle, which transports vehicles.

隧道内运营着三种不同的铁路服务——欧洲之星，从伦敦出发前往多个欧洲目的地的客运列车；货运列车，运输货物；以及Le Shuttle，运输车辆。
