European nations to boycott World Cup if Fifa goes ahead with investment plan

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Winners Spain were one of six European teams to reach the quarter-finals of this summer's World Cup

今年夏季世界杯的八强中有六支欧洲球队，包括冠军西班牙队。

European football's 55 member associations have voted to boycott World Cups if the sport's governing body proceeds with a plan to sell stakes in its competitions to private investors.

欧洲足球的55个成员协会投票决定，如果国际足联（Fifa）推进将其赛事股份出售给私人投资者的计划，他们将抵制世界杯。

The decision was made at an emergency meeting called to discuss the proposal announced earlier this week by Fifa, which oversees world football.

这一决定是在一次紧急会议上做出的，会议讨论了国际足联本周早些时候宣布的提案，该机构负责监管世界足球。

Concacaf, which governs football in North and Central America and hosted this year's World Cup, said its 41 member associations have "rejected" the proposal made by Fifa president Gianni Infantino, after it met on Thursday.

中北美及加勒比海足球协会（Concacaf）在周四召开会议后表示，其41个成员协会“拒绝”了国际足联主席詹尼·因凡蒂诺（Gianni Infantino）提出的提案。该协会负责管理北美和中美洲的足球事务，并主办了今年的世界杯。

Sources say the Concacaf nations are also losing faith in Infantino.

消息人士称，Concacaf成员国也对因凡蒂诺失去信心。

Uefa, which governs European football, had already made its opposition clear by releasing two damning statements - and that strength of feeling has been reaffirmed.

欧洲足球管理机构欧足联（Uefa）此前已通过发布两份措辞严厉的声明表明了反对立场，而这种强烈的情绪已得到再次确认。

"The World Cup cannot be treated as an investment product," it said on Thursday.

“世界杯不能被当作投资产品，”欧足联在周四表示。

"It is one of football's greatest sporting legacies. It has been built over generations by players, national teams and supporters across every continent. No part of it should ever be surrendered to private investors. The World Cup is not for sale."

“它是足球最伟大的体育遗产之一，由各大洲的球员、国家队和球迷历经几代人共同打造。任何部分都不应被交给私人投资者。世界杯不出售。”

Uefa's boycott would cover all Fifa competitions, including the men's and women's World Cups and Club World Cup, and be triggered if Fifa president Gianni Infantino's proposals are voted through by member associations.

欧足联的抵制将涵盖所有国际足联赛事，包括男足和女足世界杯以及俱乐部世界杯，如果因凡蒂诺的提案获得成员协会投票通过，抵制将启动。

The first time this stance will be tested at a senior tournament is in October, when the Women's World Cup play-offs are due to be held.

这一立场首次在高级别赛事中受到考验将是10月，届时女足世界杯附加赛将举行。

It is understood FA chairwoman Debbie Hewitt and chief executive Mark Bullingham were among those to speak during Uefa's meeting, with the atmosphere described as venomous, such was the strength of feeling against the proposals.

据了解，英足总主席黛比·休伊特（Debbie Hewitt）和首席执行官马克·布林厄姆（Mark Bullingham）在欧足联会议期间发表了讲话，会议气氛被描述为充满敌意，反映出对提案的强烈反对情绪。

Despite being one of eight Fifa vice-presidents, Hewitt had no knowledge of the discussions that Infantino had been having regarding the proposals.

尽管休伊特是国际足联八位副主席之一，但她对因凡蒂诺就提案进行的讨论一无所知。

Is World Cup sell-off plan a step too far by Infantino?

Fifa wants to create a commercial subsidiary to run its main events, including its World Cups, and external investors will be able to buy stakes in it.

国际足联希望成立一个商业子公司来运营其主要赛事，包括世界杯，外部投资者将能够购买该子公司的股份。

It said it would "invite third parties to make minority, non-controlling investments" in a new subsidiary - Fifa Forward Enterprise (FFE).

国际足联表示，将“邀请第三方进行少数、非控制性投资”进入一个新子公司——国际足联前进企业（Fifa Forward Enterprise, FFE）。

The plan needs to be passed by a vote of Fifa's 211 members.

该计划需要获得国际足联211个成员国的投票通过。

Infantino needs 106 votes in favour for the proposal to go through. Uefa and Concacaf members combined make up 96 associations, who appear set to vote against.

因凡蒂诺需要106票赞成才能通过提案。欧足联和Concacaf的成员合计有96个协会，他们似乎将投反对票。

Infantino has written to Fifa members saying they will receive $40m (Â£30m) if they back his controversial proposal. He has set a deadline of 19 September for federations to accept his plans if they want to access an initial $20m (Â£15m).

因凡蒂诺已致信国际足联成员，称如果他们支持他有争议的提案，将获得4000万美元（约3000万英镑）。他设定了9月19日的截止日期，各联合会如果想获得首笔2000万美元（约1500万英镑），就必须在此之前接受他的计划。

In a video released by Fifa on Wednesday, Infantino defended the plans, calling them "an offer, not an obligation".

在国际足联周三发布的一段视频中，因凡蒂诺为这些计划辩护，称其为“一个提议，而非义务”。

If approval is granted, Fifa says Thrive Eternal is expected to lead the proposed investor group for FFE.

如果获得批准，国际足联表示，Thrive Eternal预计将领导拟议的FFE投资者集团。

Thrive is an American venture capital firm founded by Joshua Kushner - the brother of US President Donald Trump's son-in-law Jared.

Thrive是一家美国风险投资公司，由美国总统唐纳德·特朗普的女婿贾里德·库什纳（Jared Kushner）的兄弟约书亚·库什纳（Joshua Kushner）创立。

Fifa sources have told BBC Sport there has been no discussion over Infantino, or anyone else, becoming chief executive of FFE.

国际足联消息人士告诉BBC体育，没有讨论过因凡蒂诺或其他人成为FFE首席执行官的问题。

The White House said it had "nothing" to say on Infantino's proposals "at the moment".

白宫表示，目前对因凡蒂诺的提案“无话可说”。

In a statement released after Thursday's meeting, which was held virtually and chaired by president Aleksander Ceferin, Uefa said it and its 55 member associations "stand as one".

在周四的虚拟会议后发布的声明中，欧足联表示，其与55个成员协会“团结一致”。会议由主席亚历山大·切费林（Aleksander Ceferin）主持。

"We unanimously and unequivocally reject Fifa's proposal to transfer ownership interests in the World Cup and other Fifa competitions to private investors," it said.

“我们一致且明确地拒绝国际足联将世界杯及其他国际足联赛事的所有权权益转让给私人投资者的提案，”声明称。

On Wednesday, Uefa had accused Fifa of using football "to enrich themselves and their friends".

周三，欧足联指责国际足联利用足球“为自己及其朋友谋利”。

Its statement on Thursday was even more damning, calling it "irresponsible and indefensible that a proposal of such significance for football was conceived in secret" and "without any meaningful consultation" with those who steward the game.

周四的声明更为严厉，称“秘密构思如此对足球意义重大的提案，且未与赛事管理者进行任何有意义的磋商，是既不负责任又不可辩护的”。

It added: "This is not merely a profound failure of leadership, but an abdication of Fifa's duty as the custodian of world football.

声明补充说：“这不仅是领导力的严重失败，更是国际足联作为世界足球托管人职责的放弃。”

"National associations around the world are now presented with an ultimatum: accept the irreversible capture of football's greatest competitions or bear the consequences.

“世界各地的国家协会现在面临最后通牒：接受足球最伟大赛事的不可逆转的侵占，或承担后果。”

"This is not a 'democratic decision', but governance by intimidation - an act of coercion unworthy of an institution entrusted with the stewardship of the global game."

“这不是‘民主决定’，而是通过恐吓进行治理——一种与受托管理全球赛事的机构不相称的胁迫行为。”

While Uefa only accounts for a quarter of Fifa's membership, it does include most of the world's most successful teams.

尽管欧足联仅占国际足联成员的四分之一，但它包括了世界上大多数最成功的球队。

Six of the eight quarter-finalists - including winners Spain - at this summer's World Cup were European. In the 2022 and 2018 editions, the figures were five and six respectively.

今年夏季世界杯八强中有六支欧洲球队，包括冠军西班牙队。在2022年和2018年的赛事中，这一数字分别为五支和六支。

Of the 23 World Cups to have taken place to date, 13 have been won by European countries. The other 10 have been won by South American sides.

在迄今为止举行的23届世界杯中，13届由欧洲国家赢得，其余10届由南美球队赢得。

Uefa vice-president Laura McAllister said Fifa's proposals posed a "genuine existential threat to the game".

欧足联副主席劳拉·麦卡利斯特（Laura McAllister）表示，国际足联的提案对足球构成了“真正的生存威胁”。

The former Wales international told BBC Radio Wales she hoped boycotts would not happen, adding: "I feel optimistic they won't, because the reality is the powerhouse of world football is Europe."

这位前威尔士国脚告诉BBC威尔士电台，她希望抵制不会发生，并补充说：“我乐观地认为不会，因为现实是，世界足球的重心在欧洲。”

Speaking to Radio 4's PM programme, McAllister said there was "real unity" among Uefa members and an "easy consensus over the need to take the strongest possible action given the existential threat that the Fifa proposal poses to our sport".

在Radio 4的PM节目中，麦卡利斯特表示，欧足联成员之间“真正团结”，并且“很容易达成共识，认为需要采取最强有力的行动，因为国际足联的提案对我们的运动构成了生存威胁”。

She added: "The reality is we had no choice other than to issue a threat commensurate with this dreadful, catastrophic proposal that has been rushed through with no due diligence, no proper governance and no consultation."

她补充说：“现实是，我们别无选择，只能发出与这个可怕、灾难性的提案相称的威胁，这个提案是在没有尽职调查、没有适当治理、没有协商的情况下仓促通过的。”

Scottish FA president Mike Mulraney is also the chair of Fifa's finance committee.

苏格兰足总主席迈克·穆尔雷尼（Mike Mulraney）同时也是国际足联财务委员会主席。

文化大臣丽莎·南迪（Lisa Nandy）表示：“这是一个我们强烈支持的原则性决定。足球属于球迷，而不是亿万富翁投资者。够了。是时候站出来保护我们的运动了。”

UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham criticised the plans on Tuesday, saying going ahead with the idea would mean Fifa had "sold out".

英国首相安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）周二批评了这些计划，称推进该想法意味着国际足联“出卖了自己”。

Concacaf said its members "expressed deep concerns about the lack of due process surrounding the proposal" and the "artificially short deadline imposed".

Concacaf表示，其成员“对提案缺乏正当程序以及人为设定的短截止日期表示深切担忧”。

It also questioned the need for private investment after the most profitable World Cup in history, according to Fifa.

Concacaf added that it has tasked its Fifa council members with engaging the governing body on how to use existing reserves to fund development across the region and instruct Infantino to ensure proper governance processes are followed.

Concacaf补充说，已责成其国际足联理事会成员与该管理机构接洽，讨论如何利用现有储备金资助该地区的发展，并指示因凡蒂诺确保遵循适当的治理流程。
