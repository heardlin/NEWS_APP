Levy used the card to get free travel on public transport, including the Tube network where he carried out the sexual assaults.

列维用这张卡免费乘坐公共交通，包括他实施性侵的地铁网络。

He also used it when he travelled to south London on the day he murdered Valencia-Trujillo in March 2025.

2025年3月谋杀瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略当天，他也用这张卡前往伦敦南部。

The Met also gave the card back to Levy when it originally arrested and released him the next month on suspicion of the murder. The Met says that it too missed the fact he had been using a stolen travelcard.

伦敦警察厅在最初逮捕他并于次月因谋杀嫌疑释放他时，也将卡归还给了列维。伦敦警察厅表示，它也忽略了他在使用被盗旅行卡的事实。

Levy was arrested in April 2025 on suspicion of murdering Valencia-Trujillo, but later released without charge

列维于2025年4月因涉嫌谋杀瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略被捕，但后来未获起诉获释

A pattern emerged: Levy repeatedly sexually assaulted women but was also repeatedly released on bail, even after being charged with multiple assaults.

一个模式出现了：列维反复性侵女性，但也反复获释保释，即使在被控多项袭击之后。

Levy was twice bailed by Highbury Magistrates Court, in May and June 2025. On both occasions BTP's formal written recommendation was that Levy be remanded in custody.

列维在2025年5月和6月两次被海布里地方法院（Highbury Magistrates Court）保释。两次英国交通警察局的正式书面建议都是将列维还押候审。

In one document, the force stated: "Levy's offending appears to be escalating in terms of frequency. He is considered by police to be a danger to the public and at high-risk of reoffending. Police have significant concerns that if granted bail, Levy will continue in his predatory sexual offending against female members of public."

在一份文件中，该机构表示：“列维的犯罪频率似乎在升级。警方认为他对公众构成危险，并且有很高的再犯风险。警方非常担心，如果获准保释，列维将继续对女性公众进行掠夺性性犯罪。”

Lisa Ramsarran, chief crown prosecutor for the CPS, says the service should have done more to ensure Levy was kept in custody and that "we accept there was relevant information regarding bail that was not provided to the court, when bail applications were being considered".

皇家检察署首席皇家检察官丽莎·拉姆萨兰（Lisa Ramsarran）表示，该机构本应做得更多以确保列维被拘留，并且“我们承认，在考虑保释申请时，没有向法院提供有关保释的相关信息”。

In a statement, she added: "Our actions fell short of the standards that victims, families and the public are entitled to expect. In particular, there were points where we could and should have taken a more robust approach to representations about bail, based on the information available.

在一份声明中，她补充说：“我们的行为没有达到受害者、家庭和公众有权期望的标准。特别是，在某些时候，我们本可以根据现有信息对保释陈述采取更坚决的态度。”

"We cannot know what decisions the courts would have made, had different or stronger representations been advanced. But we recognise our responsibility and we are sorry for the shortcomings we have identified."

“我们无法知道如果提出不同或更有力的陈述，法院会做出什么决定。但我们认识到我们的责任，并对我们发现的不足之处表示歉意。”

Levy was then twice bailed by Inner London Crown Court after he failed to appear for scheduled appearances, claiming he was feeling unwell.

列维随后在未能出庭后两次被伦敦内城刑事法院（Inner London Crown Court）保释，他声称自己身体不适。

Arrests warrants were not sought by BTP or prosecutors.

英国交通警察局或检察官没有寻求逮捕令。

While carrying out his campaign of sexual assaults on public transport, Levy also prowled London streets targeting vulnerable women involved in sex work.

在公共交通上实施一系列性侵的同时，列维还在伦敦街头游荡，针对从事性工作的弱势女性。

Many women involved in such work do not trust the police or feel able to report crime.

许多从事此类工作的女性不信任警察，或觉得无法举报犯罪。

The woman Levy raped in the car park, in Tottenham in January 2025, gave officers a precise description of what her attacker had looked like and where he lived.

列维于2025年1月在托特纳姆（Tottenham）停车场强奸的女性向警官提供了袭击者外貌和住处的准确描述。

An aerial view of the car park where Levy raped one woman and murdered Sheryl Wilkins

列维强奸一名女性并谋杀谢丽尔·威尔金斯的停车场的鸟瞰图

She spoke to police a few days after the rape, when she was arrested for breaching a community notice which prevented her from being in the area. In a distressed state, she explained she was on her way to the hospital because of the injuries inflicted during the attack.

她在强奸几天后与警方交谈，当时她因违反社区通知而被捕，该通知禁止她进入该地区。在痛苦的状态下，她解释说她正在去医院，因为袭击造成的伤害。

Levy had violently forced her to the ground and jumped on her, breaking her collarbone and banging her head against the ground. He then suffocated and raped her. The woman thought she was going to die and lost consciousness, only regaining it later - seriously injured and alone.

列维暴力地将她推倒在地，并跳到她身上，导致她锁骨骨折，头撞在地上。然后他使她窒息并强奸了她。这名女性以为自己会死，失去了意识，后来才恢复——伤势严重，独自一人。

She told her arresting officers she did not know Levy's name but she had seen him in the area. Indeed, years before, he had asked her for sex and tried to put his hand between her legs when she refused.

她告诉逮捕她的警官，她不知道列维的名字，但她曾在附近见过他。事实上，几年前，他曾向她索要性服务，并在她拒绝时试图将手伸进她两腿之间。

The Met proceeded to arrest the woman and took her into police custody.

伦敦警察厅随后逮捕了这名女性，并将她带入警方拘留。

Niki Adams, from the English Collective of Prostitutes, is critical of police priorities and says officers are arresting sex workers, "rather than actually paying attention to the violence and abuse that women are suffering".

来自英国妓女集体（English Collective of Prostitutes）的尼基·亚当斯（Niki Adams）批评警方的优先事项，并表示警官们正在逮捕性工作者，“而不是真正关注女性遭受的暴力和虐待”。

Officers made some attempt to speak to the woman further about the rape while she was locked up, but she was experiencing heroin withdrawal symptoms and was not in a good state to give more information or do a full recorded interview.

警官们在她被关押期间曾试图进一步与她谈论强奸案，但她正在经历海洛因戒断症状，状态不佳，无法提供更多信息或进行完整的录音采访。

After her release there were limited further attempts to speak to the woman, with police saying she could not be traced because she was homeless. But, during Levy's recent trial, a local officer accepted "we did very little" beyond checking what CCTV coverage there had been of the car park.

在她获释后，警方与她交谈的进一步尝试有限，警方表示由于她无家可归，无法找到她。但在列维最近的审判中，一名当地警官承认“我们做得很少”，除了检查停车场有哪些闭路电视覆盖。

The woman was spoken to again on the street in late May 2025, but said she did not want to talk about the rape. Her case was then closed.

2025年5月底，该女子在街上再次被谈话，但她说不想谈论强奸案。她的案件随后结案。

CCTV captured Simon Levy leaving the scene of Sheryl Wilkins' murder

闭路电视拍到西蒙·列维离开谢丽尔·威尔金斯谋杀现场

The Met insists it took proper steps to speak to the woman and only closed the case when she chose not to engage.

伦敦警察厅坚称，它采取了适当步骤与该女子交谈，并且只有在她选择不参与时才结案。

Officers have privately acknowledged to the BBC that the force needs to improve how it engages with vulnerable street sex workers, and that efforts to change practices in the Tottenham area are under way.

警官们私下向BBC承认，该机构需要改进与弱势街头性工作者互动的方式，并且正在努力改变托特纳姆地区的做法。

But nevertheless, a year ago, Met officers had intelligence about a violent man in the area, who had strangled and raped a woman into unconsciousness, but they made no effort to identify him or address the wider risk to other women.

但尽管如此，一年前，伦敦警察厅掌握情报，该地区有一名暴力男子，他曾勒晕并强奸一名女性，但他们没有努力识别他或解决对其他女性的更广泛风险。

Levy would soon kill for a second time - in the same Tottenham car park where he had carried out the brutal rape.

列维很快将第二次杀人——就在他实施残酷强奸的同一个托特纳姆停车场。

Sheryl Wilkins was murdered in August last year. The location of her body - the car park - meant officers set out to find the rape victim once again.

谢丽尔·威尔金斯于去年8月被谋杀。她尸体的位置——停车场——意味着警官们再次出发寻找强奸受害者。

Indeed, when her body was first found, officers had wondered if it was the body of the rape victim.

事实上，当她的尸体最初被发现时，警官们曾怀疑这是否是强奸受害者的尸体。

The force knew her routine and where she spent time - and an empathetic officer obtained a detailed account from the woman.

该机构知道她的日常活动和常去的地方——一位富有同情心的警官从该女子那里获得了详细的陈述。

It would lead to Levy being charged.

这将导致列维被起诉。

Wilkins' death was also the moment when the Met's specialist murder detectives began investigating the killing of Valencia-Trujillo five months before, in Walworth, south London.

威尔金斯的死也是伦敦警察厅专业凶杀案侦探开始调查五个月前在伦敦南部沃尔沃斯（Walworth）发生的瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略谋杀案的时刻。

The Met accepts the life of Sheryl Wilkins could have been saved

伦敦警察厅承认谢丽尔·威尔金斯的生命本可以得救

When Levy had murdered Valencia-Trujillo he made no attempt to hide her body. She was discovered, with visible injuries, lying face-down in the stairwell of a derelict block of flats.

当列维谋杀瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略时，他没有试图隐藏她的尸体。她被发现脸朝下躺在一栋废弃公寓楼的楼梯间，身上有明显伤痕。

The officers failed to class Valencia-Trujillo's death as a murder and did not pass it to a homicide team for a fuller investigation, as happens with suspected homicides. The force says this is because the formal cause of death was unclear.

警官们未能将瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略的死归类为谋杀，也没有将其移交给凶杀案小组进行更全面的调查，而这是疑似凶杀案的标准做法。该机构表示，这是因为正式死因不明。

A homicide assessment team had attended the scene, recommending the case stay with local officers for the time being - but those local officers did not subsequently seek advice from any homicide specialists.

一个凶杀案评估小组曾到现场，建议案件暂时由当地警官处理——但当地警官随后没有向任何凶杀案专家寻求建议。

This echoes the Met's failures in the Stephen Port case a decade ago, when the force failed to treat four murders as murders, until weeks after the final one.

这呼应了伦敦警察厅十年前在斯蒂芬·波特案中的失败，当时该机构未能将四起谋杀案视为谋杀案，直到最后一起发生数周后。

The homicide team that eventually took over Valencia-Trujillo's case found there had been no analysis of local CCTV footage that, officers discovered, put Levy near Valencia-Trujillo on the day of the crime.

最终接手瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略案的凶杀案小组发现，没有对当地闭路电视录像进行分析，警官们发现这些录像显示列维在案发当天出现在瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略附近。

While the local team had found Levy's DNA on Valencia-Trujillo's body and arrested him, he had been released without charge.

虽然当地小组在瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略的尸体上发现了列维的DNA并逮捕了他，但他未获起诉获释。

The Met did what it could to investigate Carmenza Valencia-Trujillo's death, Southworth now says, adding that "in hindsight" it is easier to make the assessment about what happened.

索斯沃思现在说，伦敦警察厅尽其所能调查卡门萨·瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略的死，并补充说“事后看来”更容易评估发生了什么。

But in stark terms, the Met had a murder that it had not treated as such, and the man responsible had gone on to rape and assault more women, and ultimately, kill again.

但坦率地说，伦敦警察厅有一桩未被如此对待的谋杀案，而责任人继续强奸和袭击更多女性，并最终再次杀人。

Wilkins was, like Valencia-Trujillo, highly vulnerable.

威尔金斯和瓦伦西亚-特鲁希略一样，非常脆弱。

Both women, and the others, were failed by the system - murdered by a man who should never have been free to harm them.

这两名女性以及其他人都被系统辜负了——被一个本不该自由伤害她们的男人杀害。
