Starmer warns leadership contest would 'plunge us into chaos' in pleas to ministers and MPs

Source: BBC News

00:00 --:--

Embattled Sir Keir Starmer has warned his ministers and MPs a challenge against his leadership could "plunge us into chaos", after attempting to win them over with what he called a "radical" plan to change the country.

四面楚歌的基尔·斯塔默爵士（Sir Keir Starmer）警告其部长和议员，对他的领导地位的挑战可能“让我们陷入混乱”，此前他试图用他所谓的“激进”国家变革计划来赢得他们的支持。

Sir Keir sought to rally his quarrelling MPs behind a package of new laws, which he said would "end the status quo that has failed working people".

基尔爵士试图团结争吵不休的议员们支持一揽子新法律，他表示这些法律将“终结辜负劳动人民的现状”。

He promised reforms to areas including health, housing and immigration, before meeting ministers and MPs in Parliament.

在议会会见部长和议员之前，他承诺对医疗、住房和移民等领域进行改革。

Sir Keir is battling to save his job after four ministers quit and dozens of Labour MPs urged him to resign, with Health Secretary Wes Streeting thought to be plotting a leadership challenge.

基尔爵士正在努力保住自己的职位，此前四名部长辞职，数十名工党议员敦促他辞职，卫生大臣韦斯·斯特里廷（Wes Streeting）被认为正在策划一场领导权挑战。

Supporters of Streeting told the BBC they expected the health secretary to challenge Sir Keir for the Labour leadership as soon as Thursday.

斯特里廷的支持者告诉BBC，他们预计卫生大臣最早将于周四挑战基尔爵士的工党领导权。

Streeting would need the support of 81 MPs to trigger a leadership contest under the Labour Party's rules.

根据工党规则，斯特里廷需要获得81名议员的支持才能触发领导权竞争。

Streeting met the prime minister in No 10 Downing Street for less than 20 minutes on Wednesday morning, after days of intense speculation that he is preparing a bid to replace him.

周三上午，斯特里廷在唐宁街10号与首相会面不到20分钟，此前数日外界强烈猜测他正准备接替首相职位。

The prime minister's spokesman said Sir Keir had "full confidence" in Streeting but would not comment on the details of their meeting.

首相发言人表示，基尔爵士对斯特里廷“充满信心”，但拒绝透露会面细节。

On Wednesday evening, Sir Keir held a series of meetings with Labour MPs and ministers in Parliament, telling colleagues: "We cannot let a leadership contest plunge us into chaos - a challenge would 100% do that."

周三晚上，基尔爵士在议会与工党议员和部长举行了一系列会议，他告诉同僚：“我们不能让领导权竞争让我们陷入混乱——挑战百分之百会导致这种情况。”

The BBC has been told there were two meetings, one with ministers of state and one with more junior ministers, and they lasted about 15 minutes.

BBC获悉，会议有两场，一场与国务大臣，另一场与初级部长，每场持续约15分钟。

Ministers told Sir Keir the government needed to act more like insurgents and govern differently, and the prime minister accepted he needed to change.

部长们告诉基尔爵士，政府需要更像反叛者那样行动，以不同的方式执政，首相承认他需要改变。

Streeting and Starmer met shortly before the Labour government set out its legislative programme in the King's Speech, which marked the start of a new parliamentary session.

斯特里廷和斯塔默在工党政府于国王演讲中公布立法计划前不久会面，这标志着新议会会期的开始。

The legislation announced by King Charles III in the House of Lords included plans to abolish NHS England, to introduce digital ID, limit trials by jury and end the leasehold system in England and Wales.

国王查尔斯三世在上议院宣布的立法包括废除英格兰国民医疗服务体系（NHS England）、引入数字身份证、限制陪审团审判以及结束英格兰和威尔士的租赁产权制度。

There were also proposals to nationalise British Steel, fast-track green energy infrastructure, forge closer trading ties with the EU, and invest in major improvements to rail services in northern England.

还有提议将英国钢铁公司国有化、加速绿色能源基础设施建设、与欧盟建立更紧密的贸易关系，以及投资大幅改善英格兰北部的铁路服务。

Outlining the bills in the House of Commons, Sir Keir said the King's Speech was an "agenda of radical reform across our major public services".

在下议院概述这些法案时，基尔爵士表示，国王演讲是“我们主要公共服务领域激进改革的议程”。

He said he was leading an "urgent, activist, Labour government" that "tilts power back to workers, renters and the less fortunate, gives voice to the working class, and all those that the status quo has repeatedly ignored and dismissed".

他说，他领导的是一个“紧迫、积极的工党政府”，它“将权力重新倾斜给工人、租房者和不幸者，为工人阶级以及所有被现状一再忽视和轻视的人发声”。

The language echoes Sir Keir's attempted reset speech on Monday, when the prime minister said "incremental change won't cut it" and promised to "face up to the big challenges" the country faced.

这些措辞呼应了基尔爵士周一试图重振旗鼓的演讲，当时首相表示“渐进式变革行不通”，并承诺“直面国家面临的重大挑战”。

But the prime minister failed to convince many of his MPs that his leadership matches the boldness they believe voters expect from a Labour government.

但首相未能说服许多议员相信他的领导力与选民期望工党政府展现的魄力相匹配。

Speaking in the Commons, Labour MP Jonathan Brash said Sir Keir could not offer the "hope" which the public was "desperate for".

工党议员乔纳森·布拉什（Jonathan Brash）在下议院发言时表示，基尔爵士无法提供公众“迫切渴望”的“希望”。

"Some people will say this is about personality," Brash said. "It is not, it's about policy and whether we are prepared to meet the moment with the scale of change it demands."

布拉什说：“有些人会说这关乎个性。其实不是，这关乎政策，以及我们是否准备好以所需的变革规模来迎接这一时刻。”

Brash is one of the more than 80 Labour MPs who have urged Sir Keir to resign immediately or announce a timetable for his departure.

布拉什是80多名敦促基尔爵士立即辞职或公布离职时间表的工党议员之一。

Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch responded to the King's Speech on Wednesday, leaving Labour MPs stony-faced with a string of blistering criticisms of their party's record in government and the prime minister's leadership.

保守党领袖凯米·巴德诺赫（Kemi Badenoch）周三对国王演讲作出回应，对工党政府的执政记录和首相的领导力提出一连串尖锐批评，让工党议员们面无表情。

Badenoch said Sir Keir was "in office, but not in power", arguing it was clear "he will not be able to deliver what little there is in this King's Speech".

巴德诺赫表示，基尔爵士“在位，但无权”，并认为很明显“他无法实现这份国王演讲中哪怕很少的内容”。

Turning to Streeting, who was sitting alongside other cabinet ministers on the front benches, Badenoch mocked the health secretary about his leadership ambitions, saying he "has been a bit distracted lately".

巴德诺赫转向坐在前排其他内阁部长旁边的斯特里廷，嘲笑卫生大臣的领导野心，说他“最近有点分心”。

"There's no point in giving me dirty looks," Badenoch said. "We all know what he's been up to."

巴德诺赫说：“对我摆臭脸没用。我们都知道他在忙什么。”

On Wednesday evening, cabinet ministers were working the tearoom and attempting to persuade colleagues a leadership election would "paralyse the ability of the government to get things done for months", and "cause chaos".

周三晚上，内阁部长们正在茶室活动，试图说服同僚，领导权选举将“在数月内瘫痪政府办事能力”，并“引发混乱”。

Streeting's next move is highly anticipated but there are also other senior Labour politicians who are known to have ambitions to become party leader.

斯特里廷的下一步行动备受期待，但还有其他资深工党政治家已知有成为党领袖的野心。

Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham has strong support from Labour MPs and polls suggest he is the most popular Labour politician with voters.

大曼彻斯特市长安迪·伯纳姆（Andy Burnham）获得工党议员的强力支持，民调显示他是选民中最受欢迎的工党政治家。

But Burnham is not currently an MP and it would be a tall order for him to get back into the House of Commons, a process that involves winning a parliamentary by-election.

但伯纳姆目前不是议员，他重返下议院将是一项艰巨任务，这需要赢得议会补选。

He did apply to be Labour's candidate in the Gorton and Denton by-election earlier this year but was blocked by Sir Keir's allies on the party's ruling body.

他确实在今年早些时候申请成为戈顿和丹顿补选的工党候选人，但被基尔爵士在党领导机构中的盟友阻止。

Multiple MPs - including Afzal Khan and Jeff Smith - have denied preparing to stand down to allow Burnham to attempt to return to Parliament.

包括阿夫扎尔·汗（Afzal Khan）和杰夫·史密斯（Jeff Smith）在内的多位议员否认准备辞职以便让伯纳姆尝试重返议会。

Burnham has pulled out of his regular phone-in radio slot on BBC Radio Manchester on Thursday, as speculation mounted over a potential challenge to Sir Keir's leadership.

伯纳姆已取消周四在BBC曼彻斯特电台的常规电话连线节目，外界对基尔爵士领导权可能受到挑战的猜测升温。

A spokesperson for Burnham told the BBC: "As you know, Andy doesn't like to miss the Hot Seat.

伯纳姆的一位发言人告诉BBC：“如你所知，安迪不喜欢错过‘热门座位’节目。”

"But this week, to get the best deal for Greater Manchester, he has to prioritise discussions arising from last week's local elections."

“但本周，为了给大曼彻斯特争取最佳利益，他必须优先处理上周地方选举引发的讨论。”
